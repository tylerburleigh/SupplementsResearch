## Promotion Rules

- Promote the anchor when the claim affects a decision-critical field or table.
- Keep mechanistic, animal, human, and genetics evidence separate during promotion.
- If no anchor can be found, leave the claim as `[!unverified]` or convert it into a `[!gap]`.
- Remove or mark completed rows after the anchor source-summary page exists and the target page cites it.
- Entries marked >30 days surface in `wiki/research-backlog.md` for review.
