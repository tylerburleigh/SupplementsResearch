# Purpose

## Goal
<!-- What are you trying to understand or build? -->
Build an evidence-based understanding of supplements for longevity and healthspan optimization, enabling informed decisions about what to take, at what dose, and why.

## Key Questions
<!-- The primary questions driving this research -->
1. Which supplements have the strongest evidence for longevity-related outcomes?
2. What are the optimal forms, doses, and timing for each supplement?
3. How do supplements interact — synergies and conflicts?
4. What does a well-supported longevity supplementation stack look like?

## Scope
<!-- What is in scope? What is explicitly out of scope? -->
**In scope:**
- Individual supplement research (mechanism, evidence, dosing, safety)
- Longevity-relevant pathways (Nrf2, mTOR, AMPK, sirtuins, telomeres, etc.)
- Supplement-supplement and supplement-drug interactions
- Stack design and optimization
- Bioavailability and formulation considerations

**Out of scope:**
- Prescription medications (except interaction notes)
- Diet and exercise (except where directly relevant to supplement efficacy)
- Anti-aging cosmetics or topical products
