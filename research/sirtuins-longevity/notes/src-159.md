# src-159: NRPT Repeat-Dose Trial (Dellinger et al., 2017)

## Core Findings

This is the first randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of repeat-dose NRPT (nicotinamide riboside + pterostilbene, commercially known as Basis) in 120 healthy adults aged 60-80. NRPT significantly increased whole-blood NAD+ levels in a dose-dependent manner: ~40% increase at the standard dose (250 mg NR + 50 mg PT) and ~90% at the double dose (500 mg NR + 100 mg PT) after 4 weeks. NAD+ elevations were sustained through 8 weeks. No serious adverse events occurred. The double-dose group showed significant within-group improvements in mobility (chair stand and 6-minute walk tests), though these secondary endpoints were underpowered.

## Key Evidence

### NAD+ Primary Endpoint
- NRPT 1X: ~40% NAD+ increase at day 30, sustained at day 60
- NRPT 2X: ~90% NAD+ increase at day 30, partially declining to ~55% at day 60
- Placebo: no change in NAD+ over 60 days
- NAD+ measured via LC-MS/MS in whole-blood lysate (pH 5, RBC lysis)
- Dose-response was significant between groups at both time points

### Safety
- 66 adverse events in 45 participants, distributed across groups (no significant between-group difference, p=0.088)
- NRPT 2X had 5 possibly/probably related AEs (GI disorders, fatigue, headache) vs 1 each in placebo and NRPT 1X
- No serious adverse events
- No clinically meaningful changes in CBC, electrolytes, kidney function

### Functional Outcomes (Q5)
- NRPT 2X group showed significant within-group increases in both 30-second chair stand test and 6-minute walk test at day 60
- No differences in placebo or NRPT 1X groups
- Authors note these results are exploratory and not sufficiently powered

### Other Findings
- ALT decreased significantly in NRPT 1X group (possible liver health signal)
- Diastolic blood pressure decreased significantly in NRPT 1X at day 60
- Small LDL cholesterol increases (~3% for NRPT 1X, larger for NRPT 2X) -- confined to overweight BMI subgroup in NRPT 1X; baseline group differences in cholesterol confound interpretation

## Methodology

- **Design**: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 3-arm parallel group
- **Population**: 120 healthy adults, ages 60-80, BMI 18-35
- **Arms**: Placebo (n=40), NRPT 1X = 250 mg NR + 50 mg PT daily (n=40), NRPT 2X = 500 mg NR + 100 mg PT daily (n=40)
- **Duration**: 8 weeks supplementation + 30-day follow-up
- **Sites**: London ON (Canada), Orlando FL, Irvine CA
- **Primary endpoint**: Safety and tolerability; NAD+ concentration in whole blood
- **Statistics**: ANCOVA adjusting for baseline; Kruskal-Wallis for non-normal data; ITT (n=118) and Per Protocol (n=113) analyses
- **Clinical trial registration**: NCT02678611
- **Conducted by**: KGK Synergize (CRO), funded by Elysium Health

## Limitations

1. **Conflict of interest**: All authors are Elysium Health employees and shareholders; Elysium markets NRPT as Basis
2. **Population**: Only older adults (60-80), 87% Western European white, 68% female -- limited generalizability
3. **Secondary endpoints underpowered**: Mobility, liver, cholesterol findings are exploratory
4. **Baseline imbalances**: Significant differences in total and LDL cholesterol at randomization confound lipid analyses
5. **Short duration**: 8-week supplementation period; long-term effects unknown
6. **NRPT 2X NAD+ decline**: The partial decline from 90% to 55% at day 60 in the 2X group is unexplained (authors speculate homeostatic mechanisms / CD38 induction)

## Relevance to Research Questions

**Q2 (Pharmacological sirtuin compounds -- NR + pterostilbene)**: This is a directly relevant clinical trial. It provides strong evidence that the NR + pterostilbene combination safely raises NAD+ in humans. The rationale for the combination is that NR provides NAD+ substrate for all sirtuins while pterostilbene (a SIRT1 activator with better bioavailability than resveratrol) provides additional SIRT1 activation. However, the study does not directly measure sirtuin activity -- only NAD+ levels as a proxy.

**Q5 (NAD+ precursor trials on functional outcomes)**: Provides moderate evidence. The NRPT 2X group showed within-group improvements in mobility tests (chair stand, 6-minute walk) but these were not powered as primary endpoints. No between-group statistical comparison results are reported for mobility. The blood pressure and ALT findings suggest potential functional benefits but need replication.

## Claims to Verify

- The ~90% NAD+ increase figure at day 30 for NRPT 2X is stated multiple times but the underlying per-subject data are in supplementary Table S3, which is not included in the source file. The percentage is plausible given the figure but exact calculation from raw data is not verifiable from this text alone.
- The mobility improvements are described as "significant within-group increases" without reporting specific p-values or effect sizes in the main text (referenced to Tables S5 and S6, which are not included).
- Participant counts: ITT analysis used n=118 (not 120) because 2 participants were excluded; PP analysis used n=113. This is clearly accounted for in the text.
