# src-135: Cannabinoids and Healthy Ageing (Wang & Arnold, 2024)

## Core Findings

This is a review paper on the anti-ageing effects of cannabinoids (primarily CBD) in preclinical models, with emphasis on C. elegans. CBD extends lifespan and healthspan in nematodes via autophagy induction, oxidative stress reduction, and glyoxalase pathway enhancement. The paper is only tangentially relevant to the research question on sirtuin activation vs. NAD+ boosting, as it discusses sirtuin involvement peripherally in the context of CBD mechanisms.

## Relevance to Q3 (Sirtuin Activation vs NAD+ Boosting)

The paper provides limited but notable evidence regarding sirtuins in the context of cannabinoid-mediated longevity:

1. **SIRT1/sir-2.1 requirement for CBD lifespan extension (lines 579-587, 636-643):** CBD-mediated lifespan extension in C. elegans requires sir-2.1 (the SIRT1 ortholog). When sir-2.1 was knocked down via RNAi, CBD failed to extend lifespan. Similarly, aak-2 (AMPK) mutant worms did not benefit from CBD treatment. The authors propose that CBD promotes longevity through sir-2.1-dependent induction of autophagy, with SIRT1 regulating autophagy via mTOR and AMPK modulation.

2. **Cannabinoids may activate sirtuins in mammalian cells (lines 1034-1042):** THC increased SIRT6 expression and CBD (combined with triacetylresveratrol) increased SIRT1 mRNA in human skin fibroblasts. The authors note sirtuin activation has been suggested to extend lifespan/healthspan "albeit with some controversy," citing Brenner 2022 and Bonkowski & Sinclair 2016.

3. **No data on NAD+ boosting, NRK1 pathway, or NMN/NR metabolism:** The paper does not address NAD+ supplementation, the NRK1 pathway, or comparisons between direct sirtuin activation vs. indirect NAD+ boosting. The sirtuin mentions are incidental to the cannabinoid longevity mechanism discussion.

## Methodology

- Review article synthesizing preclinical evidence from multiple model systems
- Primary model: C. elegans (wild-type N2, transgenic AD/PD models)
- Mechanistic evidence from RNAi knockdown studies and mutant strains
- Also covers Drosophila, zebrafish, and limited rodent data

| Finding | Model | Details |

## Limitations for Q3

- **Tangential relevance:** The paper is about cannabinoids and ageing, not sirtuin/NAD+ strategies. Sirtuin data are a minor component.
- **No comparison of direct sirtuin activation vs. NAD+ boosting:** The paper does not address this tradeoff at all.
- **C. elegans model limitations:** sir-2.1 is the SIRT1 ortholog, but C. elegans lacks the full complement of mammalian sirtuins, limiting translational relevance.
- **Fibroblast sirtuin data are preliminary:** Single-cell-type in vitro findings without functional longevity endpoints.
- **Conflict of interest:** Author JCA has patents related to cannabinoid therapeutics and consulting ties to the cannabis industry.
