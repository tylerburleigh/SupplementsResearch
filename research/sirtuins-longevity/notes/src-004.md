# Source: src-004 -- Zietara, Dziewiecka, Augustyniak (2023)

**Title:** Why Is Longevity Still a Scientific Mystery? Sirtuins--Past, Present and Future

**Journal:** Int J Mol Sci. 2022 Dec 31;24(1):728. doi: 10.3390/ijms24010728

**PMCID:** PMC9821238 | **PMID:** 36614171

**Type:** Peer-reviewed review article (open access, CC BY)

**Quality assessment:** Full text available, substantive content. This is a comprehensive narrative review covering sirtuin biology, modulators, disease connections, and longevity evidence.

---

## Core Findings

1. Sirtuins (SIRT1-7) are NAD+-dependent histone deacetylases with broad roles in metabolism, antioxidant defense, and cell cycle regulation, but their causal role in extending vertebrate lifespan remains uncertain and difficult to attribute to any single sirtuin isoform.

2. Natural sirtuin modulators (resveratrol, quercetin, fisetin) suffer from low bioavailability insufficient for clinical use; synthetic STACs (SRT1720, SRT1460, SRT2183) that mimic resveratrol are in clinical trials combined with other drugs, but the review does not report specific trial outcomes.

3. The review cautions that sirtuin biology is far more complex than initially hoped: sirtuins can act simultaneously as tumor promoters and suppressors, and excessive uncontrolled sirtuin expression can promote cancer and therapeutic resistance.

## Key Evidence and Data Points

### Lifespan Evidence (Q1)
- **C. elegans:** SIR-2.1 ortholog increases viability by ~50%, dependent on DAF-16 transcription factor, regulated via insulin/IGF-1 pathway.
- **Drosophila:** dSir2 does NOT significantly affect viability; it only regulates heterochromatin. dSir2 mutant flies do not have shorter lifespans.
- **Mice (SIRT6):** SIRT6 knockout mice die prematurely with severe defects (lymphopenia, loss of subcutaneous fat, reduced bone mineral density, impaired glucose homeostasis). SIRT6 overexpression extends lifespan in mice.
- **Mice (SIRT1):** Correlated with lifespan extension; SIRT1 activity characteristically decreases with age.
- **Key caveat:** "It is difficult to clearly define which of the sirtuins significantly increases lifespan in vertebrates."

### Pharmacological Compounds (Q2)
- Synthetic SIRT1 activators (SRT1720, SRT1460, SRT2183) are described as being in clinical trials in combination with other drugs -- no specific trial names, phases, or outcomes reported.
- SRT1720 tested in mouse model of Parkinson's disease: reduced cytotoxicity.
- Resveratrol: broad biological activity but remains in cells for limited time; may not be sufficient.
- Natural polyphenols (resveratrol, quercetin, fisetin) all have low bioavailability insufficient for clinical use.

### STACs vs NAD+ Boosting (Q3)
- All sirtuin isoforms are activated during caloric restriction/starvation due to increased NAD+/NADH ratio from elevated electron transport chain activity.
- Sirtuins require NAD+ as a co-substrate for deacetylation; this is their functional distinction from class I/II HDACs.
- Caloric restriction at 70% of energy demand increased SIRT3 expression in liver, muscles, and adipose tissue of obese rats.
- Short-term caloric restriction in mice contributes to increased expression of all seven sirtuin proteins.
- Exercise activates SIRT1 in humans (higher SIRT1 mRNA in middle-distance runners vs sedentary individuals), but vitamin C/E supplementation during training hindered SIRT1 activation.

### Arguments Against Sirtuin-Longevity Hypothesis (Q4)
- SIR2 effects on lifespan are species-specific: strong in C. elegans, absent in Drosophila.
- Palmer and Vaccarezza (2020): current research focuses on benefits rather than potential side effects.
- Overexpression of SIRT1 may cause memory deficit in mice with no neuroprotective effect.
- SIRT1 is a crucial stimulator of proliferation and angiogenesis; increased expression in women leads to endometriosis and ovarian cancer.
- SIRT5 promotes venous thrombosis; SIRT5 promotes breast cancer metastasis.
- SIRT genes are upregulated in many types of cancer.
- Animal model results are "often inadequate or difficult to interpret" for clinical translation.
- "Many scientists believe these processes are too complex to plan our future with sirtuins today."

### NAD+ Precursor Trials (Q5)
- No direct discussion of NR or NMN supplementation trials. The review focuses on caloric restriction as the primary NAD+-elevating strategy.
- NAD+ is noted as a metabolite consumed by sirtuins that plays an essential role in pain regulation and peripheral neuropathic pain.

### Researcher Disagreements (Q6)
- The review frames the field as having conflicting data: sirtuins as both promoters and suppressors of neoplastic disease.
- Notes the historical arc: from Guarante's 1991 work through Sinclair's 2003 resveratrol discovery, to current uncertainty about translational potential.
- "The future of sirtuins as longevity enzymes remains unclear and difficult to predict."

## Methodology

This is a narrative review article, not a systematic review or meta-analysis. The authors surveyed literature from 2017-2022 on sirtuin modulators (summarized in extensive tables) and covered sirtuin classification, enzymatic activity, exercise/dietary modulation, and disease associations. No formal quality assessment of included studies was performed.

## Limitations

- Narrative review without systematic methodology; potential selection bias in cited studies.
- Does not report specific clinical trial data (trial names, phases, outcomes, participant counts).
- No discussion of NAD+ precursor supplementation trials (NR, NMN).
- Published 2022/2023; does not capture the most recent clinical trial results.
- Does not engage deeply with the反驳 literature (e.g., Burnett et al. 2011, the Interventions Testing Program results) -- the Drosophila negative result is mentioned briefly but not contextualized within the broader debate.
- The paper expresses cautious skepticism but does not systematically weigh evidence for and against the sirtuin-longevity hypothesis.

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q1 (Causal lifespan extension):** Provides moderate evidence -- reviews model organism data showing mixed results across species; strongest for SIRT6 in mice; no human lifespan data.
- **Q2 (Clinical trials):** Weak evidence -- mentions STACs are in trials but provides no specific trial data or outcomes.
- **Q3 (STACs vs NAD+ boosting):** Moderate evidence -- covers NAD+ dependency mechanism and bioavailability issues of natural modulators; no direct comparison of strategies.
- **Q4 (Arguments against):** Moderate evidence -- discusses cancer risks, side effects, and translation gaps; cites Palmer & Vaccarezza (2020) critique.
- **Q5 (NAD+ precursor functional outcomes):** Negligible -- no NR/NMN trial data.
- **Q6 (Researcher disagreements):** Weak -- frames uncertainty generally but does not profile specific researcher disagreements or competing camps.

## Claims to Verify

- "SRT1720, SRT1460, SRT2183 are in clinical trials in combination with other drugs" (line ~594-596): No trial identifiers or phases given; this claim needs verification against clinical trial registries.
- "SIR-2.1 ortholog increases viability of individuals by almost 50%" (line ~386-388): This is a commonly cited but sometimes contested figure; the specific study should be verified.
- "Short-term caloric restriction in mice contributes to increased expression of all seven sirtuin proteins" (line ~468-469): This is a strong claim attributed to Maldonado et al. (2021) and should be verified for whether all seven were actually measured simultaneously.
