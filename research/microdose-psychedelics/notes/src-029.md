# src-029: Microdosing Psilocybin for MDD - Phase II Trial Protocol

**Citation:** Beidas et al. (2026). BJPsych Open, 12, e65. doi:10.1192/bjo.2025.10968
**Type:** Study Protocol (no results yet)
**Registration:** ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05259943
**Quality:** ok (full protocol text available, ~58K characters, 493 sentences)

## Core Findings

This is a detailed study protocol for a phase II, double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomised partial crossover trial evaluating microdosing psilocybin (2 mg weekly for up to 8 weeks) in 40 adults with mild-to-moderate MDD. No results are reported -- this is purely a methods paper. The trial claims to be the first and largest prospective microdosing study for MDD.

## Trial Design

- **Design:** Phase II, double-blind, triple-masked, inactive-placebo-controlled, randomised partial crossover (2x1)
- **Phase 1 (weeks 1-4):** Group 1 receives psilocybin (IP); Group 2 receives placebo (PBO); double-blind
- **Phase 2 (weeks 5-8):** Both groups receive open-label psilocybin (IP); no washout period between phases
- **Allocation:** 1:1 randomisation via computer-generated sequential random numbers
- **Rationale for crossover:** Reduce intra-subject variability, increase statistical power, model carryover effects, test placebo role in outcomes
- **Single-site** trial in Ontario, Canada
- **Duration:** 8-week treatment + 4-week follow-up + optional 2-year long-term follow-up (surveys every 6 months)

## Dosing Protocol (Q7)

- **Dose:** 2 mg psilocybin (PEX010) per capsule
- **Formulation:** Naturally extracted psilocybin mushrooms, manufactured by Filament Health (Burnaby, BC, Canada), GMP-compliant
- **Placebo:** 2 mg maltodextrin in identical capsules
- **Route:** Oral, self-administered in clinic
- **Frequency:** Once weekly (sessions exactly 7 days apart per REB approval)
- **Total doses:** 4 doses in experimental phase + 4 doses in open-label phase = 8 total
- **Dose rationale:** ~10% of threshold doses used in clinical trials; satisfies microdose definition (not >0.1 mg/kg); endorsed by real-world microdosers for ecological validity
- **Fixed dose:** Not modified in response to patient preference or symptom trajectory

## Population

- **N:** 40 adults (planned)
- **Age:** 18-65
- **Diagnosis:** Mild-to-moderate MDD, diagnosed in-person by independent physician, confirmed by SCID-5
- **Key exclusions:**
  - Bipolar I/II, schizophrenia, psychotic disorders (current or past)
  - First- or second-degree relative with these disorders
  - Current or past moderate/severe substance use disorder (last 5 years, excluding caffeine/nicotine)
  - Active suicidal ideation or past suicide attempt
  - Psychotropic medications, opioids, serotonin medications
  - Receiving any form of psychotherapy
  - Cardiovascular conditions, epilepsy, unstable medical conditions
  - Psychedelic use in last 6 months
  - Sensitivity to maltodextrin
- **Lifestyle controls:** No major changes in caffeine, no new therapies, no night shifts; no cannabis/alcohol within 24h of dosing

## Outcome Measures

### Primary
1. **PHQ-SADS** (Patient Health Questionnaire-Somatic, Anxiety and Depressive Symptoms Scale): 32-item self-report, at every visit
2. **SCID depression modules**: Structured diagnostic interview at sessions 1, 4, 8

### Safety
- **C-SSRS** (Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale): At every visit
- Vital signs, sobriety tests (balance, motor functioning, coordination) at pharmacodynamic peak and pre-discharge
- Participants not discharged until passing sobriety tests and approved by investigator

### Secondary
- Attention: MRT, SART
- Creativity: UUT, 5-DOT, RAT, insight problems (weeks 1, 4, 8 only)
- Prosociality: IOS, MIE
- Well-being: GSE, DAS-A-17, BFI-II, SQS, PANAS, BPI-SF, QOLI
- Mindfulness: FFMQ, MAIA-2
- Mystical experiences: MEQ-30
- Qualitative: QRSPD, QRSB, QR-SR

## Power Analysis

- Used hierarchical linear model in R (lme4 package, REML)
- Expected effect size: small-to-medium (based on mindfulness intervention analogy, not macrodose psychedelic trials)
- PHQ-9 used as main predictor with assumed variance of 0.16 in scores
- At N=40: 93% power for Group x Session interaction (0.75-point weekly change); 99% power for Group x Session 5 (pre-crossover, 0.75-point change)
- No formal DMC due to budget constraints; independent clinical monitor at start, midpoint, end

## Key Methodological Details

- Randomisation by unblinded pharmacists who do not interact with participants
- Blinding integrity assessed via participant guesses at group assignment each visit
- Data captured in REDCap v14.0.21
- Missing data handled by multiple imputation
- Analysis population: all who completed at least one dosing session and one follow-up survey
- Results committed to publication regardless of outcome

## Limitations

- Small sample size (N=40) though powered for expected effect
- No formal Data Monitoring Committee (budget constraints)
- Single-site design limits generalisability
- Fixed dose with no titration may not capture dose-response
- No washout between crossover phases (by design, to model real-world microdosing)
- Results not yet available -- this is a protocol paper only
