## Core Findings

The PAM trial is the first study to test the feasibility, acceptability, and safety of LSD microdosing alongside meaning-centred psychotherapy (PA-MCP) for advanced-stage cancer patients with anxiety/depression. It is a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled parallel-group feasibility trial (N=40) conducted at the University of Auckland, New Zealand. The protocol introduces several novel design features for clinical microdosing research including dose titration (4-20 micrograms LSD), mild deception blinding, supervised and unsupervised (home) dosing, break-week provisions, and culturally responsive design for Maori participants.

## Trial Design

- **Design:** Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled parallel-group feasibility trial
- **Population:** Stage IV solid tumour cancer patients with moderate-to-severe distress (distress thermometer >=4), N=40 total (20 Maori, 20 non-Maori)
- **Arms:** PA-MCP (LSD microdose + MCP, n=20) vs. Placebo-MCP (inactive placebo + MCP, n=20)
- **Randomisation:** Randomly permuted blocks with stratification by Maori/non-Maori ethnicity, performed by biostatistician
- **Site:** Clinical Research Centre, Grafton Campus, University of Auckland, New Zealand
- **Ethics:** HDEC approval 14 July 2022 (Reference: 13074); registered ANZCTR ACTRN12623000478617
- **Not powered for efficacy** -- primary aim is feasibility/acceptability/safety assessment

## Dosing Protocol

- **Compound:** GMP-quality LSD hemitartrate (MB-22001), liquid sublingual formulation
- **Starting dose:** 8 micrograms (lower than the 10 micrograms used in the MDLSD healthy volunteer study)
- **Titration range:** 4-20 micrograms, adjusted in 1-2 microgram increments based on participant feedback and subjective drug effects
- **Schedule:** Twice weekly for 6 weeks + 1 day = 13 total doses
  - 7 doses administered in-clinic aligned with MCP sessions (days 1, 8, 15, 22, 29, 35, 43)
  - 6 doses self-administered at home (days 4, 11, 18, 25, 32, 39)
  - All doses by 2pm to minimise sleep disruption
- **Break weeks:** Up to 7 break weeks available per participant (extends treatment to max 13 weeks 1 day)

## Blinding Strategy

- **Mild deception:** Participants told that one of three placebos may be used (inactive, caffeine, methylphenidate) with stated 1/3:1/3:1/3 allocation
- **Actual allocation:** 1:0:0 (inactive placebo only)
- Rationale: LSD microdoses can produce perceptible effects leading to functional unblinding; the deception design aims to maintain blinding integrity
- Participants also told that doses up to 20 micrograms may not produce perceptible effects, to further support blinding during titration

## Psychotherapy Component

- 7 weekly sessions of individual meaning-centred psychotherapy (MCP)
- Sessions conducted by registered psychologists trained in MCP
- Delivery in-clinic or via Zoom (flexibility for illness/medical commitments)
- Session 1 occurs 45-60 minutes post-dosing; subsequent sessions follow similar timing
- 30% of sessions video-recorded for treatment fidelity assessment via Memorial Sloan Kettering Fidelity Rating Scale
- MCP topics: meaning concepts, cancer and identity, historical/legacy meaning, attitudinal meaning, creative meaning, experiential meaning, transitions

## Patient Population

- Stage IV incurable solid organ malignancy
- Prognosis >=6 months life expectancy
- Distress thermometer >=4 (moderate distress)
- Age >=25 years
- Half Maori, half non-Maori
- Allowed: concurrent noncurative cancer treatment, antidepressants, pain medication, CBD
- Key exclusions: psychotic/bipolar disorders, PTSD, panic disorder, OCD, eating disorders, elevated suicide risk (C-SSRS), cerebral metastases, Karnofsky <50, BP >160/90, liver function >3x ULN, lifetime psychedelic microdosing history, psychedelic use within past year

## Feasibility Measures

| Measure | Feasibility Threshold |
|---------|----------------------|
| Dose adherence | >=80% of doses taken (11/13) |
| MCP attendance | >=4 of 7 sessions |
| Recruitment rate | >=70% of consented randomised |
| Attrition | <=30% post-randomisation |
| MCP treatment fidelity | Memorial Sloan Kettering Fidelity Rating Scale |
| Data completeness | Percentage of complete data |

## Safety Monitoring

- Vital signs (HR, BP, body temperature) at 0, 30, 120, 240, 360 min post-dosing (session 1); 0 and 45 min at sessions 2-7
- Serotonin syndrome checks at every in-clinic visit
- ECG at final study visit
- PCOC-SAS (9-item symptom scale) weekly
- Adverse event recording on all dose days (home and clinic)
- SAEs reported to MedSafe and HRC Data Monitoring Committee in real-time
- Independent DMC (Health Research Council of NZ) meets every 6 months
- Trial Steering Committee oversight

## Secondary Outcome Measures

- Sense of meaning (LAP-R Personal Meaning Index)
- Quality of life (FACT-G)
- Spiritual well-being (FACIT-SP-12)
- Connectedness (Watts Connectedness Scale)
- Anxiety/depression (DASS-21, HADS)
- Maori holistic well-being (Hua Oranga, Te Whare Tapa Wha domains)
- Demoralisation (Demoralization Scale)
- Attitudes toward hastened death (SAHD)
- Pain (BPI-SF)
- Personality (BFI-2-XS), absorption (MODTAS), treatment expectancy (TEX-Q)
- Altered states (5D-ASC), subjective drug effects (VAS), blinding assessment
- Caregiver stress and closeness (support persons)

## Cultural Considerations

- Equal recruitment target for Maori and non-Maori (20 each)
- Maori consultation in research design; Maori researchers, advisors, and psychologists involved
- Te Ao Maori worldview integrated into assessments (Te Whare Tapa Wha, wairua/spirituality)
- Participants encouraged to bring whanau (family) support to sessions
- Whanau perspectives included in analyses
- Research team trained in relevant tikanga Maori

## Prior Safety Data Cited

- In 48 healthy older volunteers receiving 6 doses of 0/5/10/20 micrograms LSD, only headaches showed statistically significant group differences; all were mild-moderate; no severe AEs; vital signs/ECG/labs within normal limits (Family et al. 2020)
- In the MDLSD study (healthy volunteers), 10% reported anxiety leading to discontinuation; dose titration successfully improved retention
- Surveys of microdosers: 30% report no side effects; only 20% report adverse effects; effects are acute not long-term

## Relevance to Research Questions

### Q1: Clinical trial design
This is a primary source for clinical trial design methodology in microdosing research. Key innovations include: (1) the first microdosing trial in an advanced cancer population, (2) a titration protocol allowing 4-20 micrograms dose flexibility, (3) mild deception blinding to counter functional unblinding, (4) combined supervised and home dosing for ecological validity, (5) integration with an evidence-based psychotherapy (MCP), (6) break-week provisions for clinical populations, and (7) culturally responsive design for indigenous populations.

### Q6: Safety considerations in clinical populations
Comprehensive safety protocol for medically complex patients: extensive exclusion criteria targeting cardiovascular risk, psychiatric vulnerabilities, and drug interactions; multi-timepoint vital sign monitoring; serotonin syndrome checks; SAE reporting to regulatory bodies; independent DMC. Important precedent for allowing concurrent cancer treatment and psychiatric medications (antidepressants, pain meds), which enhances real-world applicability. The dose titration approach was specifically designed to mitigate anxiety risk observed in the prior MDLSD study.

## Limitations

- Protocol paper only (no results yet); trial commenced September 2023, anticipated completion late 2024
- Small sample (N=40) not powered for efficacy -- only feasibility
- Open question whether the mild deception adequately maintains blinding given LSD's perceptible effects at the dose range used
- Home dosing component introduces adherence monitoring challenges
- Generalisability may be limited to NZ healthcare context and the specific cancer population

## Claims to Verify

- The assertion that effect sizes of MCP vs. usual care are "only small to medium (d = 0.1 to 0.34)" (line 133) is attributed to Breitbart et al. 2018 and should be verified against the original source.
- Recruitment estimate of "one participant per week for 40 weeks" (line 668) is based on a similar study (Reynolds et al. 2017, ref 73); this may be optimistic for an advanced cancer population given their health burden.
