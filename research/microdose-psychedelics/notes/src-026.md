# src-026: LSDDEP2 Trial Protocol (Daldegan-Bueno et al. 2024)

## Core Findings

LSDDEP2 is the first randomised controlled trial designed to test LSD microdosing as a treatment for major depressive disorder (MDD) in participants' naturalistic (home) environment. The protocol describes a rigorously designed phase 2b trial using a double-dummy, triple-blind, active placebo-controlled design with titratable dosing (4-20 micrograms LSD). The trial targets N=90 participants with DSM-5 MDD (MADRS 18-35) and is powered to detect a minimum 4.2-point MADRS difference at 8 weeks.

## Trial Design

- **Phase:** 2b, superiority design, two-arm parallel groups
- **Registration:** ANZCTR ACTRN12624000128594 (prospectively registered 13 Feb 2024)
- **Venue:** Trials (2024), 14 citations, open access (CC-BY 4.0)
- **Sponsor:** University of Auckland, New Zealand
- **Recruitment:** Commenced March 2024, planned completion end of 2025
- **SPIRIT compliant** with CONSORT reporting planned

## Dosing Protocol

- **Formulation:** MB-22001 (LSD hemi-tartrate, manufactured by Biocell Corp NZ under GMP)
- **Route:** Sublingual administration
- **Schedule:** 2 doses/week on non-consecutive days, 8 weeks total (16 doses)
- **Starting dose:** 8 micrograms LSD free-base equivalent
- **Titration:** Adjusted by 1 or 2 micrograms per dosing session based on participant's 5-point Likert scale subjective assessment
- **Range:** 4 to 20 micrograms
- **Home administration:** 15/16 doses self-administered at home; 9th dose in-clinic for PK/EEG/biomarker collection
- **Safety:** Lock boxes provided; participants instructed not to drive for 6 hours post-dose; doses taken before 2 pm

## Active Placebo / Double-Dummy Design

This is a key methodological innovation. The double-dummy design addresses the core challenge in psychedelic microdosing trials: participants can often detect whether they received active drug.

- **LSD arm:** Active MB-22001 vial (LSD) + inactive placebo capsules (no caffeine/methylphenidate)
- **Placebo arm:** Inactive placebo vial (no LSD) + active placebo capsules (caffeine OR methylphenidate)
- **Caffeine:** Starting 100 mg (2 capsules), range 50-300 mg
- **Methylphenidate:** Starting 20 mg (2 capsules), range 10-60 mg
- **Allocation:** Within placebo group, 1:1 caffeine to methylphenidate
- **Rationale:** Caffeine and methylphenidate produce mild perceptual effects that mimic the subtle sensations of LSD microdoses, making it harder for participants to guess their allocation

## Triple-Blinding

- **Participants:** Blinded to whether they receive LSD + inactive capsules or placebo vial + active capsules
- **Investigators/staff:** All blinded except pharmacist (RP) and biostatistician (AC)
- **Outcome assessors:** Separate blinded raters conduct MADRS interviews; they do not interact with participants except for assessments; audio recordings cross-scored
- **Unblinding:** Only after database lock following 6-month follow-up

## Sample Size and Power

- N = 90 (45 per group), 1:1 allocation with variable block sizes
- Pragmatic sample size; sensitivity analysis via Monte Carlo simulations (10,000 iterations)
- Parameters: mean baseline MADRS = 30, 5 expected dropouts, alpha = 0.05, power = 0.8
- **Minimum detectable effect: approximately 4.2 MADRS points** at 8 weeks
- Random effects variance estimated from prior ketamine antidepressant trial in same lab

## Inclusion/Exclusion Highlights

- **Inclusion:** DSM-5 MDD, MADRS 18-35, age 21-65, any gender
- **Key exclusions:** Psychotic/schizophrenia/bipolar disorders (or first-degree relative), PTSD, eating disorders, C-SSRS items 3-5 positive, Stage II+ treatment-resistant depression, any lifetime psychedelic microdosing, psychedelic use within 3 months, lifetime self-medication with psychedelics for depression
- **Allowed:** Concurrent antidepressant therapy (if stable, no new treatments during trial)
- **Diversity target:** Minimum 25% Maori or Pasifika participants

## Compliance and Monitoring

- **MDOT (Mobile Directly Observed Therapy):** Custom mobile app guides participants through dosing; they record video of administration; staff review for compliance
- Previous trials (MDLSD) achieved 100% compliance with MDOT
- Capsule counting at return visits

## Psychotherapeutic Component

- Participants encouraged to set intentions and engage in self-selected psychologically beneficial activities during each microdosing session
- Journaling via the study app serves as a reflective integration component
- Based on MDLSD qualitative findings that positive experiences were associated with psychologically beneficial activities

## Additional Design Features

- **Extension period:** Optional 8-week open-label extension offered to all participants
- **Follow-up:** 1, 3, and 6 months post-treatment for durability assessment
- **DESS** measured at first follow-up for discontinuation symptoms
- **Expectancy:** CEQ measured at baseline and dosing sessions; semi-structured expectancy interview at baseline

## Limitations

- Sample size is pragmatic rather than formally powered; may be underpowered for smaller effect sizes
- No formal interim analysis planned for the primary outcome
- Self-titration introduces variability in actual doses across participants
- Naturalistic home setting increases ecological validity but reduces experimental control
- Concurrent antidepressant use may confound interpretation
- The paper is a protocol only; no results are reported

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q1 (Clinical trial design):** Directly relevant. This is a detailed protocol for a phase 2b microdosing RCT in MDD, with innovative design elements (double-dummy active placebo, home administration, MDOT compliance). Represents the state of the art in microdosing trial methodology.
- **Q3 (Methodological approaches - active placebo, triple-blind):** Highly relevant. The double-dummy active placebo design (using caffeine or methylphenidate to mimic LSD perceptual effects) is a direct methodological contribution. Triple-blinding with separate outcome assessors is described in detail.
- **Q7 (Dosing protocol details):** Highly relevant. Provides comprehensive dosing specifics: 8 microgram starting dose, 4-20 microgram range, 1-2 microgram titration steps, twice-weekly schedule, sublingual administration, MB-22001 GMP formulation.

## Claims to Verify

- Minimum detectable effect of 4.2 MADRS points: This is based on Monte Carlo simulations with variance estimates from a different drug (ketamine) trial. The applicability of these variance estimates to LSD microdosing is uncertain.
- "100% compliance with MDOT" in prior MDLSD trial: Reported in Discussion but refers to a different study; should be cross-referenced with the original publication.
- N = 19 in LSDDEP1 pilot: Mentioned parenthetically as "not yet published" at time of protocol writing.
