# src-194: Center-Based vs Home-Based Exercise on hs-CRP and IL-6 in HFrEF

**Citation:** Lawrence DS, Muzakkir AF, Patimang Y, et al. "Comparative Effects of 12 Weeks of Center-Based versus Home-Based Exercise Training on hs-CRP and IL-6 Levels in Patients with Heart Failure Reduced Ejection Fraction." *Ukrainian Journal of Cardiovascular Surgery*, 2025;33(4):184-191. DOI: 10.63181/ujcvs.2025.33(4).184-191

## Core Findings

Both center-based and home-based exercise training significantly reduced inflammatory markers IL-6 and hs-CRP after 12 weeks in heart failure patients with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF), with no statistically significant difference between the two modalities. Home-based exercise is non-inferior to center-based exercise for reducing systemic inflammation in this population.

## Study Design

- **Design:** Quasi-experimental (pre-post, non-randomized allocation)
- **Population:** 31 patients with HFrEF (LVEF <40%), NYHA class I-II, aged 18-70
- **Setting:** Integrated Cardiac Center, Dr. Wahidin Sudirohusodo Hospital, Makassar, Indonesia
- **Groups:** Center-based exercise (n=15) vs home-based exercise (n=16)
- **Intervention:** 12 weeks, light-intensity exercise, 5 sessions/week, 30 min/session
  - First 2 sessions (1 week) supervised for both groups
  - Center-based: 2 supervised hospital sessions + 3 home sessions/week
  - Home-based: 2 remote-supervised sessions + 3 independent sessions/week
- **Exercise kit provided:** 1 kg dumbbells, incentive spirometer, digital sphygmomanometer, pulse oximeter, logbook, instructional video flash drive
- **Primary outcomes:** Serum hs-CRP and IL-6 (pre/post blood draws)
- **Statistical tests:** Mann-Whitney U test (between groups), Wilcoxon signed-rank test (within groups), significance at p<0.05

## Key Results

### IL-6 Changes
| Group | Pre (pg/mL) | Post (pg/mL) | Median Delta | Within-group p | Between-group p |
|-------|-------------|--------------|--------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Home-based (n=16) | 11.8 | 4.6 | -3.8 | 0.001 | 0.906 |
| Center-based (n=15) | 17.4 | 5.5 | -3.9 | 0.002 | |
| Overall (n=31) | 13.1 | 4.8 | -3.9 | <0.001 | |

### hs-CRP Changes
| Group | Pre (mg/L) | Post (mg/L) | Median Delta | Within-group p | Between-group p |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Home-based (n=16) | 4.7 | 2.5 | -1.3 | 0.002 | 0.200 |
| Center-based (n=15) | 3.2 | 2.5 | -0.4 | 0.005 | |

Note: All values are medians with ranges reported in the source. The between-group comparisons for delta changes were non-significant for both markers.

## Clinical Outcome Correlations

This study did not report direct correlations between inflammatory marker changes and clinical outcomes (mortality, hospitalization, functional capacity). Functional capacity was estimated via 6-minute walk test (VO2 max calculation) at baseline, but no post-intervention functional outcomes were reported alongside the biomarker data.

The discussion cites supporting literature:
- IL-6 is a long-term predictor of mortality over 12-month follow-up, independent of VO2peak and LVEF (ref 7: Berger et al., 2024)
- hsCRP <2 mg/L is associated with reduced cardiovascular events over 2 years (ref 6: Zhang et al., 2023)
- A systematic review of 24 trials (3,046 participants) found no differences between home-based and center-based cardiac rehabilitation for mortality, exercise capacity, QoL, or costs at 12 months (ref 20: Taylor et al., 2023)

## Supporting Meta-Analysis Data (cited)
- Malandish et al. (2023) meta-analysis: exercise reduces hs-CRP with SMD = -0.441 (95% CI: -0.642 to -0.240; p=0.001) in HF patients

## Limitations

1. **Small sample size** (n=31) -- limits generalizability and statistical power
2. **Quasi-experimental design** -- non-randomized allocation; baseline IL-6 differed numerically (11.8 vs 17.4 pg/mL) though not statistically (p=0.268)
3. **No clinical outcome data** -- only biomarker endpoints; no mortality, hospitalization, or functional capacity outcomes reported post-intervention
4. **Short follow-up** -- 12 weeks only; no longer-term sustainability data
5. **Gender imbalance** -- center-based group was 100% male; only 3 females total (9.7%)
6. **Internet connectivity variability** affected home-based monitoring quality
7. **Age difference** between groups was significant (61.1 vs 54.9 years, p=0.032)

## Relevance to Research on CRP and Longevity

This study provides direct evidence that structured exercise (either center-based or home-based) can significantly reduce both hs-CRP and IL-6 in HFrEF patients over 12 weeks. The ~42% reduction in hs-CRP (4.3 to 2.5 mg/L) and ~63% reduction in IL-6 (13.1 to 4.8 pg/mL) are substantial, though the lack of randomization and small sample size limit causal inference. The study does not address whether these biomarker reductions translate to improved survival or longevity.
