# src-132: Prioritizing Health | hsCRP: A Promising Risk Assessment Tool

**Source:** ACC Cardiology Magazine, Dec 1, 2025
**URL:** https://www.acc.org/latest-in-cardiology/articles/2025/12/01/01/prioritizing-health-hscrp
**Authors:** Dairina Hernandez Ortega (Inova Schar), Kamil Faridi (Yale), Lily Dastmalchi (Inova Schar)
**Type:** Guideline summary / clinical review article (not peer-reviewed primary research)

## Core Findings

The ACC's 2025 Scientific Statement on Inflammation and Cardiovascular Disease now recommends **universal hsCRP screening** in both primary and secondary prevention populations. hsCRP is the most validated inflammatory biomarker and a stronger predictor of future CV events than LDL-C in secondary prevention. The threshold of >=2 mg/L is established as a risk-enhancing factor in primary prevention guidelines, and hsCRP predicts MACE over 20-30 year follow-up horizons.

## Key Evidence

### RCT Evidence Referenced
- **Ridker et al. (1997, 2000):** Original prospective studies showing hsCRP predicts CV events in men and women (NEJM)
- **CLEAR Outcomes (2024):** Among 13,970 patients with statin intolerance, inflammation and cholesterol were predictors of CV events; bempedoic acid lowered hsCRP by 23.2% in obese patients
- **Ridker et al. Lancet (2023):** Collaborative analysis of three RCTs showing inflammation and cholesterol predict events in statin-treated patients; hsCRP was a better predictor than LDL-C
- **PREDIMED:** Mediterranean diet + olive oil lowered CRP vs controls in high-risk patients
- **EVADE CAD:** Vegan diet reduced hsCRP by 32%
- **SELECT trial:** Semaglutide: 20% MACE risk reduction, 28% MI reduction, 19% all-cause mortality reduction; 43.5% inflammation reduction in HFpEF+obesity
- **Colchicine RCTs:** Meta-analyses show low-dose colchicine reduces MACE in stable CAD; but negative results in acute MI (Jolly 2025) and stroke (CONVINCE, CHANCE-3)

### Guideline Recommendations (ACC Position)
1. **Universal hsCRP screening** in primary and secondary prevention (2025 ACC Scientific Statement)
2. hsCRP >=2 mg/L = risk-enhancing factor (2018 AHA/ACC cholesterol guideline)
3. **Initiate/intensify statins** for persistently elevated hsCRP regardless of LDL-C
4. **Colchicine** has Class 2b recommendation for secondary prevention in CAD (specifically chronic stable CAD)
5. **GLP-1 RAs** should be considered for residual inflammatory risk when indicated

### Quantitative Lifestyle Modification Data
- Aerobic exercise: -0.59 mg/L (healthy), -0.34 mg/L (CVD patients)
- Mediterranean diet: -0.98 mg/L (meta-analysis of RCTs)
- Vegan diet: -0.54 mg/L vs mixed diet; 32% lower hsCRP in EVADE CAD
- Weight loss: -0.13 mg/L per kg; -0.25 mg/L per 6.4 kg
- Smoking cessation: -0.40 mg/L in CVD patients
- High-fiber diet: 28% reduction, comparable to lovastatin 20 mg/d

### Pharmacologic hsCRP Reduction
- Simvastatin 40 mg/d: -4.3 mg/L; Atorvastatin 80 mg/d: -3.7 mg/L
- Bempedoic acid: -23.2% hsCRP, -22.5% LDL-C (in obesity)
- Ezetimibe + BA: -26 to -38% hsCRP (ezetimibe alone does not reduce CRP)
- Colchicine: reduces hsCRP; FDA-approved for secondary prevention
- Semaglutide: reduces inflammation; 20% MACE reduction in SELECT

## Methodology

This is a clinical review/summary article published in ACC Cardiology Magazine. It synthesizes evidence from multiple RCTs, meta-analyses, and guideline documents. It is not itself a primary study. The article references 49 primary sources including landmark RCTs and guideline statements.

## Limitations

- **Not a primary research study** -- this is a clinical summary/guideline interpretation article
- **No original quantitative analysis** -- all numbers come from referenced studies
- **Published in a magazine format** (Cardiology Magazine), not a peer-reviewed journal
- Does not provide detailed critical appraisal of the cited RCTs
- Potential for publication bias in the cited meta-analyses is not discussed
- Does not address hsCRP limitations (e.g., non-specificity, acute-phase confounders, timing of measurement) in detail
- Does not discuss cost-effectiveness of universal screening

## Relevance to Research Questions

This source is highly relevant as an authoritative summary of the ACC's current position on hsCRP testing. It consolidates:
- The **guideline position** (universal screening recommended as of 2025 ACC Scientific Statement)
- **RCT evidence** for hsCRP as a risk predictor (cites landmark trials)
- **Quantitative data** on lifestyle and pharmacologic interventions to lower hsCRP
- **Long-term prediction** evidence (20-30 year follow-up studies)

## Claims to Verify

- **"hsCRP is a better predictor of future cardiovascular events and death than LDL-C"** (line 36-37): This is a strong claim attributed to references 14 and 15 (Ridker Lancet 2023 collaborative analysis; 2025 ACC Scientific Statement). Worth verifying that this is specifically in secondary prevention populations.
- **Universal screening recommendation**: This is attributed to the 2025 ACC Scientific Statement (ref 15). Should verify the actual wording of the Scientific Statement, as "universal screening" is a significant policy recommendation.
- **Simvastatin -4.3 mg/L, atorvastatin -3.7 mg/L reductions** (line 107-108): These are large absolute reductions. Verify from reference 32 (Zhang 2022 meta-analysis).
- **SELECT trial 20% MACE reduction**: Verify from reference 46 that this is the correct figure and population.
