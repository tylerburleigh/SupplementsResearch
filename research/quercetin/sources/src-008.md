Solubility

Quercetin’s solubility in aqueous solutions is low and pH-dependent. Supplement formulations that contain liposomes, nanoemulsions, and micelles can enhance quercetin’s water solubility.

Absorption

Quercetin is absorbed in the upper segment of the small intestine. Its absorption varies based on the type of formulation. Formulations containing quercetin-loaded nanoparticles display better absorption than other delivery methods.

Bioavailability

Enhancing quercetin’s water solubility also increases its bioavailability. Phospholipid complexes of quercetin increase the compound’s water solubility approximately 13-fold,
