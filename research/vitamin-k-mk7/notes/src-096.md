# Source src-096: High dp-ucMGP in Hemodialysis Patients -- Risk Factors and Response to MK-7

**Citation:** Aoun M, Makki M, Azar H, Matta H, Chelala DN. BMC Nephrology (2017) 18:191. DOI: 10.1186/s12882-017-0609-3
**Venue:** BMC Nephrology (open access, peer-reviewed)
**Citations:** 60
**Quality:** ok -- full text available, substantive content

---

## Core Findings

This pre-post intervention trial of 50 hemodialysis patients in Lebanon demonstrated profound vitamin K deficiency (median dp-ucMGP 3179 pM; 98% above the 500 pM deficiency threshold) that was significantly correlated with aortic calcification scores (Spearman rho = 0.43, P = 0.002). Daily supplementation with 360 mcg MK-7 for 4 weeks reduced dp-ucMGP by 86%, with 88% of patients reaching levels below 500 pM. No adverse events or thrombotic complications were observed.

## Key Evidence / Data Points

- **Baseline dp-ucMGP:** Median 3179.15 pM (IQR 1825.25-4339.50) -- extremely elevated above the 500 pM deficiency threshold
- **Correlation with calcification:** dp-ucMGP significantly correlated with AC-24 aortic calcification score (rho = 0.43, P = 0.002); association strongest at the 1000 pM cut-off (P = 0.02)
- **Prevalence of deficiency:** 98% of patients had dp-ucMGP > 500 pM at baseline
- **MK-7 intervention effect:** 86% median drop in dp-ucMGP after 4 weeks of daily 360 mcg MK-7; post-treatment median 294.50 pM (IQR 217.02-381.55)
- **Normalization rate:** 88% of patients achieved dp-ucMGP < 500 pM
- **Diabetes effect:** Diabetic patients had significantly lower dp-ucMGP drop rates (OR 0.17, 95% CI 0.05-0.62, P = 0.01 in multivariate analysis)
- **Risk factors for extreme deficiency (dp-ucMGP > 5000 pM):** Female sex (P = 0.02), recent fracture (P = 0.004), lower serum albumin (P = 0.046)
- **Safety:** Zero thrombotic events, zero adverse effects, zero withdrawals in 50 patients over 4 weeks
- **VKA washout:** In 6 patients previously on VKAs, dp-ucMGP dropped 70% after 2 weeks of withdrawal and stabilized before MK-7 initiation; no thrombotic events after switching to clopidogrel plus MK-7

## Methodology

- **Design:** Prospective pre-post intervention clinical trial (NCT02876354)
- **Population:** 50 hemodialysis patients at a Lebanese center; median age 71.5 years; 60% male; 36% diabetic
- **Intervention:** 360 mcg MK-7 daily (2 capsules of 180 mcg) for 4 weeks; administered post-dialysis on dialysis days and with lunch on non-dialysis days
- **Exclusions:** Patients on VKA who could not stop (heart valve replacements); those refusing consent
- **Measurements:** dp-ucMGP by ELISA (Immunodiagnostic Systems, UK) at baseline and 4 weeks; AC-24 aortic calcification score by lateral abdominal X-ray
- **Statistical methods:** Spearman correlation, Fisher's exact test, Mann-Whitney U test, bivariate and multivariate logistic regression

## Limitations

1. **Small sample size** (n = 50) limits generalizability and statistical power
2. **No control group** -- pre-post design cannot exclude time effects or placebo effects
3. **Short follow-up** (4 weeks) -- does not assess whether MK-7 reduces actual vascular calcification progression
4. **Single-center study** in Lebanon -- ethnic and dietary factors may limit generalizability
5. **Funded by Omicron (Lebanon)** who provided the MK-7 supplement (MenaQ7), though they had no role in design/analysis
6. The 86% drop is notably higher than prior European studies (61% in Westenfeld et al., 46% max in Caluwe et al.) -- the authors speculate absorption variability, capsule vs tablet formulation, and dosing with food may explain the difference, but this is not proven

## Relevance to Research Questions

### Q1: Vitamin K status and calcification markers
**Strongly relevant.** This study directly links dp-ucMGP (a vitamin K status marker) to vascular calcification scores in hemodialysis patients. The Spearman rho = 0.43 correlation (P = 0.002) between dp-ucMGP and AC-24 is a key data point. The study also demonstrates that MK-7 supplementation at 360 mcg/day dramatically corrects the biochemical marker of vitamin K deficiency within 4 weeks. The finding that 98% of HD patients are vitamin K deficient (by dp-ucMGP criteria) underscores the clinical relevance.

### Q4: Safety considerations in kidney disease
**Moderately relevant.** The study reports zero adverse events in 50 hemodialysis patients over 4 weeks at 360 mcg/day MK-7. The VKA-to-clopidogrel switch data (6 patients, no thrombotic events) provides additional safety context. However, the sample size is small, follow-up is short, and the study was not designed as a safety trial.

## Claims to Verify

- The 86% drop in dp-ucMGP is notably higher than prior studies (61% Westenfeld, 17-46% Caluwe). The authors suggest absorption or formulation differences but do not prove causation. This should be contextualized when cited.
- The n = 50 is reported consistently throughout the paper (Tables 1-6 all sum to 50). Verified.
- The Spearman rho = 0.43, P = 0.002 is reported in both the abstract and Figure 1 description. Consistent.
