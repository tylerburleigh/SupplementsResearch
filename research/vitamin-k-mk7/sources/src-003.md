How to Choose the Best Vitamin K2 MK-7 Supplement

The MK-7 form of vitamin K2 is preferred over MK-4 for most supplementation purposes because of its significantly longer half-life — MK-7 remains active in the body for approximately 72 hours compared to just a few hours for MK-4. This means daily dosing with MK-7 maintains more consistent blood levels. Look for supplements that specify “MK-7” or “menaquinone-7” rather than just “vitamin K2,” as the form makes a significant difference in effectiveness.

Most research supports doses between 100-200mcg of K2 MK-7 daily for bone and cardiovascular health. Higher doses up to 600mcg may be appropriate under practitioner guidance. If you’re taking vitamin D3, pairing it with K2 is considered essential by many nutrition experts to ensure proper calcium metabolism and prevent arterial calcification.

For fat-soluble vitamins like K2, absorption matters. Choose supplements that include a fat carrier (coconut oil, MCT oil, or olive oil) or take them with a fat-containing meal. Softgels generally absorb better than dry capsules for fat-soluble nutrients. Third-party testing for purity and potency adds an extra layer of quality assurance.

Consider whether you want a standalone K2 supplement (for maximum dosing flexibility) or a combination D3+K2 formula (for convenience). Both approaches are effective — it depends on whether you already take D3 separately and want to add K2, or prefer an all-in-one solution.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is vitamin K2 MK-7 and what does it do?

Vitamin K2 MK-7 (menaquinone-7) is a form of vitamin K2 primarily derived from natto, a traditional Japanese fermented soybean food. Its primary role is activating proteins that direct calcium into bones and teeth while preventing calcium from depositing in arteries and soft tissues. This makes it essential for both bone density and cardiovascular health. MK-7 has the longest half-life of all K2 forms, providing sustained activity throughout the body.

How much vitamin K2 MK-7 should I take daily?

Research supports 100-200mcg of K2 MK-7 daily for general bone and cardiovascular health. Most combination D3+K2 supplements provide 100-200mcg. Higher doses may be recommended by healthcare practitioners for specific conditions like osteoporosis or arterial calcification. There is no established upper limit for K2, and it is generally considered very safe even at higher doses.

Should I take K2 MK-7 with vitamin D3?

Yes. Vitamin D3 increases calcium absorption from the gut, but without adequate K2, that calcium may deposit in arteries rather than bones. K2 MK-7 activates the proteins (osteocalcin and matrix GLA protein) that direct calcium to its proper destinations. Most integrative health practitioners now recommend always pairing D3 with K2 for optimal calcium metabolism.

What is the difference between K2 MK-7 and K2 MK-4?

MK-7 is derived from bacterial fermentation (primarily natto) and has a half-life of about 72 hours, maintaining consistent blood levels with daily dosing. MK-4 is the form the body naturally synthesizes and has a very short half-life of just a few hours, requiring multiple daily doses for sustained effects. MK-7 is more practical for supplementation, while MK-4 has unique tissue-specific benefits in the brain and reproductive organs.

Can I take vitamin K2 if I’m on blood thinners?

Vitamin K2 can interfere with warfarin and other vitamin K-dependent blood thinners by counteracting their mechanism of action. If you take warfarin or similar anticoagulants, do not take K2 supplements without first consulting your prescribing physician. Newer anticoagulants (DOACs) like apixaban and rivarelbxaban work through different mechanisms and may not interact with K2, but medical consultation is still essential.

When is the best time to take vitamin K2 MK-7?

Take K2 MK-7 with a meal that contains dietary fat for optimal absorption. Since K2 is fat-soluble, it requires a fat carrier for proper uptake. If your supplement is in a softgel with an oil base (coconut oil or olive oil), absorption is less dependent on meal timing. Many people take their D3+K2 supplement with breakfast or dinner as part of their daily routine.
