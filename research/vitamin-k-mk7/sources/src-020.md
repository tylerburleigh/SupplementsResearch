Cholecalciferol (Vitamin D3) + Menaquinone-7 (Vitamin K2 MK-7) is renowned for its synergistic effects on bone and cardiovascular health, making it a top choice for researchers exploring optimal calcium utilization and arterial wellness. Vitamin D3 promotes calcium absorption in the intestines, while Vitamin K2 MK-7 ensures that calcium is directed to bones and teeth, preventing unwanted arterial calcification. This dynamic duo is essential for those seeking to enhance skeletal strength, vascular integrity, and overall metabolic balance.

Mécanisme d'action :

The combined action of Cholecalciferol and Menaquinone-7 centers on the regulation of calcium metabolism. Vitamin D3 increases the synthesis of calcium-binding proteins, boosting calcium uptake from the digestive tract. Vitamin K2 MK-7 activates matrix Gla-protein (MGP) and osteocalcin, which guide calcium into bone tissue and away from arteries, reducing the risk of calcification. This selective mechanism maximizes bone mineralization while protecting cardiovascular health, with minimal risk to non-target tissues.
