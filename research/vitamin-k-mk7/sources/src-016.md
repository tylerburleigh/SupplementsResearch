Vitamin K2

What Vitamin K2 Is and Why It Matters
Vitamin K2 is a fat-soluble vitamin that helps guide calcium to the places in the body where it’s needed — like bones — and away from places where it doesn’t belong. It plays an important role in bone strength by supporting how calcium is used and stored.
Without enough Vitamin K2, calcium can circulate in the bloodstream without being properly directed, which may limit its benefit to bone tissue over time.

Where Vitamin K2 Comes From
Vitamin K2 is found in certain fermented foods and is also produced in small amounts by gut bacteria. In supplements, it’s typically provided in a stable, bioavailable form that allows the body to absorb and use it efficiently.

How Vitamin K2 Works in the Body
Vitamin K2 activates specific proteins that help bind calcium into the bone matrix, where it contributes to bone density and strength. These proteins act like guides, ensuring calcium is deposited into bones rather than remaining unused.
By supporting this process, Vitamin K2 works alongside calcium and vitamin D to help maintain healthy bone structure.

Safety & Considerations
Vitamin K2 is generally well tolerated when used as directed. Individuals taking blood-thinning medications should consult a healthcare professional before use, as vitamin K can affect clotting pathways.
