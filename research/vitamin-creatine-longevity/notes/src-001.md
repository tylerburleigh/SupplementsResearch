## Core Findings

Mayo Clinic rates creatine as "generally safe" when taken at recommended doses, for up to five years. The primary established benefit is improved performance in short-burst high-intensity exercise (sprinting, weightlifting). Evidence for other uses (brain health, heart failure, bone density) is limited or preliminary.

## Key Evidence / Data Points

- **Safety duration:** Likely safe for up to 5 years at recommended doses (line 48)
- **Kidney function:** No harm to kidney function in healthy people at recommended doses; older reports suggested risk in those with pre-existing kidney disease, but current evidence is limited (lines 44, 54)
- **Weight gain:** Listed as a known side effect (line 52)
- **Caffeine interaction:** May reduce creatine's effectiveness; in a large Parkinson's study, >300 mg caffeine/day was associated with faster disease progression when combined with creatine (line 61)
- **Muscle cramps/injury:** No increased risk found (line 20)
- **Baseline dietary intake:** Typical diet provides 1-2 g/day; body replaces 1-3 g/day (line 6)
- **Population differences:** Vegetarians and others with low baseline creatine may see larger benefits (line 34)
