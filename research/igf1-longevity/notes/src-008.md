# Source Note: src-008

## Citation
Junnila RK, List EO, Berryman DE, Murrey JW, Kopchick JJ. "The GH/IGF-1 axis in ageing and longevity." *Nature Reviews Endocrinology* 9(6):366-376, 2013. DOI: 10.1038/nrendo.2013.67. PMCID: PMC4074016.

## Source Type
Comprehensive narrative review (peer-reviewed, high-impact journal). Not a primary study; synthesizes data from many mouse models and human cohorts.

## Core Findings

1. **Reduced GH/IGF-1 signalling consistently extends lifespan in mice.** Multiple independent genetic models -- including Ames dwarf (+68% female/+49% male), Snell dwarf (+42%), Ghr-/- (+21% female/+40% male), lit/lit (+25% female/+23% male), Pappa-/- (+38%), Igf1r+/- (+33% females only), and others -- demonstrate that disruption at various nodes of the GH/IGF-1 axis extends mouse lifespan. The longest-lived laboratory mouse on record was a Ghr-/- mouse that lived nearly 5 years.

2. **Human data are suggestive but not conclusive for lifespan extension.** IGF1R mutations (Ala37Thr, Arg407His) enriched in Ashkenazi Jewish centenarians suppress IGF-1 signalling. The IRS2 Gly1057Asp variant is associated with increased probability of reaching 96-104 years in an Italian population. Patients with Laron syndrome (Ecuadorian cohort) show near-complete cancer protection, but no definitive lifespan extension has been demonstrated due to confounding causes of death (accidents, alcohol) and small cohort sizes.

3. **rhGH as an anti-ageing therapy is not supported.** A meta-analysis of 18 trials in healthy elderly (mean age 69) found only minor body composition benefits but significant risks, including glucose intolerance or diabetes in >50% of males over 26 weeks. The Growth Hormone Society (2009) stated that anti-ageing rhGH therapy cannot be recommended.

4. **Pharmacological GH inhibition (rather than supplementation) may be the more promising anti-ageing strategy.** Pegvisomant (GH receptor antagonist) and somatostatin analogues normalize IGF-1 in acromegaly patients and could theoretically slow ageing, but long-term safety data in healthy individuals are lacking, and cost is prohibitive.

## Methodology

- **Design:** Narrative review. PubMed search (Aug-Oct 2012) using terms "GH", "IGF-1", "ageing", "longevity", "lifespan". Full-text English-language papers only (two exceptions).
- **Scope:** Comprehensive review of mouse genetic models (Table 1 summarizes 16+ strains with lifespan data), human cohorts with GH/IGF-1 axis mutations, and therapeutic interventions (rhGH, pegvisomant, somatostatin analogues).
- **No primary data** were generated; all findings are from cited primary studies.

### Human cohort data
- **Ecuadorian Laron syndrome (n=90):** Only 1 nonlethal cancer case reported; diabetes protection observed; no lifespan change vs. controls (after excluding childhood mortality).
- **Krk cohort, Croatia (n=25, PROP1 mutation):** Patients live to 80-90 years; delayed greying; no diabetes despite obesity.
- **Itabaianinha, Brazil (n=100+, GHRHR mutation):** Reduced mean lifespan (47.7 vs 63.3 years) due to early deaths in young women from diarrheal disease.
- **Swiss GH1 deletion cohort (n=11):** Shortened lifespan (females 47.4, males 57.4 vs. siblings 74.2, 70.9); possible confound from CD79B gene deletion affecting immunity.
- **Ashkenazi Jewish centenarians:** IGF1R mutations Ala37Thr and Arg407His enriched vs. controls; female carriers are IGF-1 resistant with slightly short stature.
- **Italian longevity study:** IRS2 Gly1057Asp variant (~34% population frequency) associated with higher probability of reaching 96-104 years.

### Intervention data
- **rhGH meta-analysis:** 18 trials, mean age 69; >50% males developed glucose intolerance/diabetes over 26 weeks.
- **Pegvisomant:** Elevated liver transaminases in ~2.5% of patients; normalizes IGF-1 better than somatostatin analogues.
- **Somatostatin analogues:** Normalize IGF-1 in ~45% of acromegaly patients; can worsen insulin resistance.

## Mechanisms Discussed

1. **Stress resistance:** Multiple long-lived mouse strains (Ames, Snell, Ghr-/-, Igf1r+/-, p66shc) show improved cellular stress resistance; FOXO transcription factors upregulated when IGF-1 signalling is reduced.
2. **Tumour resistance:** Consistent across mouse models and human Laron syndrome cohorts; linked to reduced mTOR signalling.
3. **Caloric restriction overlap:** CR does not further extend lifespan in Ghr-/- mice, suggesting shared mechanism (possibly via mTOR).
4. **Insulin sensitivity** is not a prerequisite for lifespan extension (some long-lived models are insulin-resistant).
5. **Body size** is not a reliable predictor: klotho and p66shc mice are normal-sized but long-lived; GHA mice are dwarfs but not long-lived.

## Limitations

1. **Review, not primary data.** All quantitative findings come from individual cited studies with varying methodologies and sample sizes.
2. **Mouse-to-human translatability is uncertain.** Human cohort sizes are small (25-100 individuals), confounded by comorbidities, and formal longevity studies have not been conducted.
3. **Swiss cohort contradicts the main thesis.** The GH1-deletion cohort had *shortened* lifespan, possibly due to the CD79B co-deletion, but this remains a confound.
4. **Publication date is 2013.** Over a decade of subsequent research on IGF-1, rapamycin, and senolytics is not covered.
5. **Competing interest:** Kopchick is inventor of US patent 5350836 on GH antagonists (the basis for pegvisomant).
6. **Some mouse findings are controversial.** Igf1r+/- lifespan extension was not replicated in C57BL/6 background; Irs2+/- lifespan findings disputed between research groups.

## Relevance to IGF-1 and Longevity Research

This review is **highly relevant** as a foundational reference for understanding:
- The breadth of mouse genetic evidence linking reduced GH/IGF-1 signalling to extended lifespan
- Human genetic evidence (IGF1R mutations in centenarians, Laron syndrome cancer protection)
- The argument that GH inhibition (not supplementation) may be the anti-ageing strategy
- Mechanistic pathways (mTOR, FOXO, stress resistance, tumour resistance)
- The somatopause concept and why declining GH/IGF-1 with age may be protective rather than pathological

## Claims to Verify

1. **Swiss cohort lifespan numbers** (females 47.4, males 57.4 vs. siblings 74.2, 70.9) -- cited from Besson et al. 2003 (ref 29). Important because this is the only human GH-deficiency cohort showing shortened lifespan, and the CD79B co-deletion confound should be tracked.
2. **">50% of male participants developed glucose intolerance"** on rhGH -- cited from Blackman et al. 2002 (ref 107). This is a critical adverse-effect claim that should be verified against the original study's exact numbers.
3. **Ames dwarf +68%/+49% lifespan increase** -- cited from Brown-Borg et al. 1996 (ref 17). Verify that these are mean (not median) lifespan changes and that control survival was not unusually short.
