# src-002: Ergothioneine promotes longevity and healthy aging in male mice

**Citation:** Katsube M, Ishimoto T, Fukushima Y, Kagami A, Shuto T, Kato Y. GeroScience. 2024;46(4):3889-3909. doi:10.1007/s11357-024-01111-5. PMCID: PMC11226696.

**Quality:** ok -- full text available from PMC, substantive peer-reviewed paper.

## Core Findings

Daily oral supplementation of ergothioneine (ERGO) at 4-5 mg/kg/day dissolved in drinking water (0.055 mg/mL) from 7 weeks of age significantly extended lifespan in male C57BL/6J mice (P < 0.001, log-rank test), with median survival increasing 16% (605 to 704 days) and average survival increasing 21% (590 to 715 days). The lifespan benefit was corroborated in C. elegans at 10 mM ERGO (median lifespan +31%, P < 0.01). Beyond longevity, ERGO attenuated multiple dimensions of age-related decline: frailty (weight loss, fat loss, locomotor slowing), systemic biomarkers of aging (renal function markers, kynurenine pathway metabolites), oxidative stress, cellular senescence, and cognitive impairment.

## Methodology

- **Subjects:** C57BL/6J male mice (n=36/group for survival; n=116 control, n=84 ERGO for frailty/cognition substudies). Housed 4/cage from 4 weeks until natural death.
- **Intervention:** ERGO (Tetrahedron, France) at 0.055 mg/mL in drinking water from 7 weeks of age to end of life. Estimated dose: 4-5 mg/kg/day.
- **Diet:** Basal diet 5755 (TestDiet) containing < 0.01 ug ERGO/g chow (intentionally low ERGO to isolate supplementation effect).
- **Survival:** Kaplan-Meier analysis, log-rank test. Mice checked daily; euthanized when unlikely to survive 24h.
- **Frailty:** Body composition (EchoMRI), open field test (OFT) at 7, 24, 48, 78, 88 weeks.
- **Biomarkers:** CE-TOF-MS plasma metabolomics (creatinine, SDMA, urea, ADMA, kynurenine pathway). ELISA for CXCL9. Western blot for p16, SIRT6. TBARS for oxidative stress.
- **Cognition:** Novel object recognition test (NORT) at 24 and 88 weeks.
- **Brain histology:** IHC for neurogenesis (Dcx/NeuN), microglial phenotype (CD86/CD206/Iba1/CD68), TDP43 aggregation.
- **Mechanism:** HNMT inhibition assay (brain cytosol and recombinant human), primary microglial culture qRT-PCR.
- **C. elegans:** IncuCyte-based lifespan and non-frailty span assay at 5 and 10 mM ERGO.
- **Statistics:** Log-rank test for survival, Welch t-test for two-group comparisons, two-way ANOVA with Tukey's post-hoc for multi-group, Dunnett's test vs control.

## Key Evidence

### Lifespan Extension
| Metric | Control | ERGO | Change |
|--------|---------|------|--------|
| Median survival | 605 days | 704 days | +16% |
| Average survival | 590 days | 715 days | +21% |
| P90 (90th percentile death age) | 672 days | 868 days | +29% |
| P value (log-rank) | -- | -- | < 0.001 |

### C. elegans Lifespan
- 10 mM ERGO: median lifespan 19.0 vs 14.5 days control (+31%, P < 0.01)
- 10 mM ERGO: non-frailty span 15.5 vs 12.5 days control (+24%, P < 0.05)

### Frailty Attenuation (at 88 weeks)
- Body weight decline: P < 0.05 vs control
- Fat mass decline: P < 0.01 vs control
- Average movement velocity: P < 0.05 vs control
- Maximum movement velocity: P < 0.05 vs control

### Systemic Aging Biomarkers (at 92 weeks)
- All renal function markers (creatinine, SDMA, urea, ADMA): P < 0.01 suppression vs control
- Kynurenine/tryptophan ratio: P < 0.01
- Quinolinic acid: P < 0.05; Kynurenine: P < 0.01

### Cellular Senescence and Oxidative Stress
- Hepatic TBARS (lipid peroxidation): P < 0.01 suppression at 92 weeks
- Plasma CXCL9 (inflammatory clock of aging): P < 0.05 suppression
- Liver p16 (senescence marker): P < 0.05 suppression
- Liver SIRT6 (longevity-associated deacetylase): P < 0.05 preservation

### Cognitive Enhancement
- NORT discrimination index: significantly higher at both 24 weeks (P < 0.01) and 88 weeks (P < 0.05)
- Hippocampal neurogenesis (Dcx+/NeuN+): P < 0.05 suppression of age-related decline at 28, 52, and 92 weeks
- Microglial M1 (CD86): P < 0.05 suppression of age-related increase
- Microglial M2 (CD206): P < 0.05 increase
- TDP43 aggregation in hippocampal hilus: P < 0.05 reduction

### Novel Mechanistic Finding: HNMT Inhibition
- ERGO competitively inhibits HNMT with IC50 of 83.8 uM (mouse brain cytosol)
- Plasma ERGO ~ 40 uM; estimated brain ERGO ~ 80 uM (brain-to-plasma ratio ~2)
- Both ERGO and HNMT inhibitor metoprine promoted M2 polarization in primary microglia (P < 0.05)
- No additive effect when combined, suggesting shared HNMT-dependent mechanism

## Limitations

1. **Male mice only** -- no data on female response
2. **Single cognitive test** (NORT) -- limited scope of cognitive assessment
3. **Senescence markers only in liver and blood** -- not measured in brain or other key organs
4. **Brain ERGO concentrations not directly measured** -- estimated from published brain-to-plasma ratio
5. **Control group had shorter-than-expected lifespan** -- average C57BL/6J lifespan is ~878 days; control here was 590 days average. Authors attribute this to the ERGO-poor basal diet (< 0.01 ug/g vs ~0.2 ug/g in typical diets) and higher fat content (22.1% of energy). This raises the possibility that ERGO's effect partly compensates for a deficient diet rather than extending normal lifespan.
6. **Sample sizes for some substudies were small** (n=4-6 for histology/biomarker endpoints)
7. **No dose-response data in mice** (single dose tested)

## Claims to Verify

- **Median survival figures (Table 2):** Control 605 days, ERGO 704 days -- cross-checked against text and table. Consistent.
- **n=36 per group for survival:** Stated in Methods (line 179) and figure caption. One mouse excluded from control for malformation, yielding n=35 control. Verified.
- **IC50 of HNMT inhibition:** Stated as 83.8 uM. Only mentioned in Discussion (line 1154), not shown in Results with formal curve-fitting. The figure (Fig 6a) shows dose-dependent inhibition but IC50 derivation is not shown.

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q1 (lifespan/longevity):** Strongest available preclinical evidence for ERGO lifespan extension in mammals. Large effect size (16-21% median/mean increase) with robust statistical significance.
- **Q2 (optimal dose):** Single dose tested (4-5 mg/kg/day). C. elegans data shows dose-response at 5 vs 10 mM.
- **Q4 (frailty/physical function):** Direct evidence for attenuation of age-related weight loss, fat loss, and locomotor slowing.
- **Q5 (biomarkers/safety):** Comprehensive plasma biomarker panel showing suppression of renal function decline and inflammaging markers. No adverse effects reported.
- **Q6 (cognitive/neurological):** Robust evidence for cognitive enhancement, hippocampal neurogenesis preservation, microglial M2 polarization, and reduced TDP43 aggregation. Novel HNMT inhibition mechanism proposed.
- **Q7 (human translation):** Discusses human relevance but provides no human data. Notes that lower plasma ERGO levels in humans are associated with cognitive decline, frailty, cardiovascular disease, and mortality.
