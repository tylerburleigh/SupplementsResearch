# src-293: Efficacy and Safety of Oral L-Ergothioneine Supplementation in Primary Dysmenorrhea

**Citation:** Guo C, Liu W, Ding W, Cao J, Tong T, Liu F, Xiao G. medRxiv preprint (2026). DOI: 10.64898/2026.03.26.26349375
**Trial Registration:** ChiCTR2500112557 (retrospectively registered 2025-11-17)
**Status:** Preprint (not peer-reviewed), posted March 27, 2026

## Core Findings

This is the first randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of oral L-ergothioneine (EGT) for primary dysmenorrhea (PD). EGT at 120 mg/day for 3 consecutive menstrual cycles significantly and progressively reduced menstrual pain and systemic symptoms compared to placebo, with no adverse events reported.

## Methodology

- **Design:** Single-center, prospective, randomized (1:1), double-blind, parallel, placebo-controlled pilot trial
- **Site:** Qingdao Central Hospital of Rehabilitation University (July-December 2025)
- **Population:** 40 nulliparous women aged 18-30 with PD (20 per group)
- **Intervention:** EGT capsules (30 mg each), 2 capsules morning + 2 evening = 120 mg/day total
- **Comparator:** Matching placebo capsules, identical dosing schedule
- **Duration:** 3 consecutive menstrual cycles
- **Primary outcome:** Peak pain intensity (Visual Analog Scale, VAS)
- **Secondary outcomes:** Dysmenorrhea Symptom Score (DSS), COX Menstrual Symptom Scale (CMSS)
- **Exploratory:** Serum inflammatory biomarkers (IL-6, TNF-alpha, IL-1-beta, PGF2alpha), breast ultrasound
- **Compliance:** >95% in both groups (diary cards + capsule counts)
- **Analysis:** Linear mixed model for longitudinal VAS trajectories; Wilcoxon signed-rank, Mann-Whitney U, Fisher's exact test

## Key Evidence

### Primary Outcome (VAS Peak Pain)
- EGT group: VAS decreased from 4.80 +/- 1.12 (baseline) to 2.32 +/- 1.59 (cycle 3), p < 0.001 vs baseline
- Placebo group: VAS changed from 4.10 +/- 1.30 to 3.45 +/- 1.69 (not significant)
- Between-group difference at cycle 3: p = 0.0052
- LMM Time x Group interaction: beta = -0.587, p < 0.001 (accelerated decline in EGT group)

### Secondary Outcomes
- DSS: 9.6 +/- 2.03 to 5.7 +/- 2.59 in EGT (p < 0.05 vs baseline; p = 0.0282 vs placebo)
- CMSS: 29.2 +/- 18.9 to 16.4 +/- 11.53 in EGT (p < 0.05 vs baseline); no significant change in placebo

### Responder Analysis
- >=50% VAS reduction: 84.2% EGT vs 35.0% placebo (p = 0.003)
- >=30% VAS reduction: 84.2% EGT vs 40.0% placebo (p = 0.008)
- NNT for >=50% VAS reduction: 2.0 (95% CI: 1.4-3.7)

### Mechanistic Findings
- No significant between-group differences in IL-6, TNF-alpha, IL-1-beta, PGF2alpha
- No correlation between biomarker changes and VAS improvement
- Suggests EGT acts via cytoprotective/antioxidant pathways (OCTN1-mediated mitochondrial accumulation) rather than classical inflammatory suppression

### Safety
- Zero adverse events or serious adverse events in either group
- No clinically meaningful changes in vital signs or laboratory parameters

## Limitations

1. **Small sample size:** N = 40 total (20 per group) -- pilot scale, not powered for definitive efficacy
2. **Single-center design:** Limits generalizability
3. **Short follow-up:** 3 months only; no long-term benefit or relapse data
4. **Preprint:** Not peer-reviewed; registered retrospectively
5. **Industry affiliation:** Several authors employed by Gene III Biotechnology Co., Ltd. (the supplement provider)
6. **Baseline imbalance:** VAS baseline marginally different (4.80 vs 4.10, p = 0.053); menstrual cycle length also differed (p = 0.048)
7. **Exploratory biomarkers:** Detailed mechanistic analysis deferred to a separate manuscript
8. **One missing data point:** Subject K007 missed cycle 3 assessment

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Ergothioneine supplementation efficacy:** Direct RCT evidence for 120 mg/day oral EGT producing clinically meaningful pain reduction in a specific condition (PD). NNT of 2.0 is strong for a nutritional intervention.
- **Safety profile:** Excellent tolerability at 120 mg/day over 3 months with zero adverse events, consistent with EGT's classification as a safe dietary compound.
- **Mechanism of action:** Data suggests EGT works through intracellular antioxidant/cytoprotective mechanisms (via OCTN1 transporter) rather than modulation of circulating inflammatory cytokines -- relevant for understanding EGT's broader therapeutic potential.
- **Longevity/aging relevance:** Indirect -- the study supports EGT's role as a cytoprotective agent with cumulative benefits over time, but does not directly address aging or longevity endpoints.

## Claims to Verify

- **N=40 sample size:** Confirmed in text (20 per group, all completed). One subject (K007) missed final assessment, so responder analysis used n=19 for EGT.
- **VAS baseline difference (p = 0.053):** The near-significant baseline imbalance in pain severity is a methodological concern. The EGT group started with higher pain (4.80 vs 4.10), which could amplify within-group improvement due to regression to the mean. The LMM analysis partially addresses this, but it remains a limitation.
- **84% responder rate:** Note this is 16/19 (not 16/20), as one subject was excluded from cycle 3 analysis. The abstract states "84%" which matches 16/19 = 84.2%, but the denominator should be clarified.
