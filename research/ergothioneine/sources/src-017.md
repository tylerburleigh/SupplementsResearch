L-Ergothioneine
is a naturally occurring amino acid and potent antioxidant that safeguards your body against oxidative stress—a key contributor to aging and chronic diseases. Notably, your body cannot synthesize L-Ergothioneine; it must be obtained through diet, primarily from mushrooms or specific supplements.

In this article, we delve into why L-Ergothioneine is garnering attention as a potential "longevity vitamin," exploring its role in supporting long-lived cells, brain function, skin health, and immune resilience, all backed by recent scientific research.

An Old Discovery, a New Purpose

Discovered in 1909, L-Ergothioneine remained enigmatic for decades. The breakthrough came with the identification of a specific cellular transporter,
SLC22A4 (OCTN1)
, which actively absorbs and distributes L-Ergothioneine throughout the body. This unique transport mechanism underscores its significance in human biology, particularly during mealtimes when absorption peaks.

Could L-Ergothioneine Be the Next Vitamin?

Some researchers propose that L-Ergothioneine fulfills the criteria for a new essential vitamin:

It cannot be synthesized by the body

It has a dedicated transport system

Its deficiency is linked to cognitive decline and chronic illnesses

If recognized, it would be the first new vitamin identified since B12 in 1948.

Why L-Ergothioneine Supports Healthy Aging

A standout feature of L-Ergothioneine is its ability to protect long-lived cells—those that regenerate slowly or not at all, such as neurons in the brain, retinal cells in the eye, and fibroblasts in the skin. These cells are susceptible to oxidative damage over time, necessitating robust protection.

Brain Health & Cognitive Protection

L-Ergothioneine is one of the few antioxidants capable of crossing the blood-brain barrier, accumulating in memory-related areas like the hippocampus. This property makes it particularly valuable in protecting against neurodegenerative conditions.

A 2021 study published in
Free Radical Biology and Medicine
found that individuals with dementia had significantly lower blood levels of L-Ergothioneine. Lower levels were associated with brain shrinkage and impaired blood vessels, suggesting that supplementation may help delay cognitive decline and support brain longevity.

Eye Protection Against Oxidative Stress

Your eyes are constantly exposed to UV light, blue light, and pollution, all of which can cause oxidative damage. L-Ergothioneine naturally concentrates in the lens and cornea, providing protection against degeneration.

Research indicates that the body actively transports L-Ergothioneine to these eye tissues, where it functions as a potent antioxidant, potentially reducing the risk of UV-induced damage and supporting overall eye health.

Clinically Studied Skin Benefits

A 2023 clinical trial investigated the effects of daily oral supplementation with L-Ergothioneine on skin health. Over a four-week period, 19 healthy participants took 25 mg of pure L-Ergothioneine per day. Skin changes were evaluated using the VISIA skin analysis system, focusing on key parameters such as pores, wrinkles, UV spots, brown spots, and porphyrins.

The results showed measurable improvements, including reduced pore size, fewer wrinkles, reduction in UV spots, improved brown spot scores, and better porphyrin balance, indicating reduced bacterial activity and sebum regulation.

Immune System Support from the Inside Out

L-Ergothioneine is absorbed by key immune cells such as monocytes and macrophages, where it helps reduce inflammation and oxidative stress. It also accumulates in the bone marrow, where immune cells are produced. This dual action may reduce inflammatory cytokines like IL-8, improve immune resilience during stress or illness, and help balance immune function, especially in autoimmune conditions like Crohn’s disease.

Liver Health & Metabolic Balance

A 2024 study published in
Lipids in Health and Disease
found that L-Ergothioneine can help protect the liver in cases of MASLD—a common form of fatty liver disease linked to obesity and insulin resistance. In both mice and human liver cells, L-Ergothioneine reduced fat accumulation, inflammation, and oxidative stress, while also improving insulin sensitivity. These benefits were associated with increased autophagy—a natural cellular process that helps clear out damaged components and restore balance.

Kidney Health & Oxidative Protection

The kidneys filter over 50 gallons of blood daily and are constantly exposed to oxidative stress. A recent mouse study on cystinuria found that L-Ergothioneine reduced the formation of kidney stones, restored glutathione levels, and improved mitochondrial energy production, suggesting it may help maintain kidney function in aging populations and those with chronic stress or illness.
