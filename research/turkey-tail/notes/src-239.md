## Core Findings

1. **PSP prolongs endotoxin fever via IL-6**: Pre-treatment with PSP (100 mg/kg i.p.) 2 hours before LPS administration (50 ug/kg i.p.) significantly extended the duration of endotoxin fever in male Wistar rats. The fever in PSP/LPS-treated rats lasted approximately 9 hours longer than in saline/LPS controls (returning to baseline at 21h vs. 12h post-PSP injection). This effect was mediated by IL-6, as anti-IL-6 antibody (30 ug/rat) completely prevented the fever extension.

2. **Differential cytokine modulation by PSP**: PSP produced an additive effect on IL-6 production during LPS-induced fever (5.4-fold increase at 3h, 11.5-fold at 14h) while paradoxically decreasing TNF-alpha levels (76% reduction at 3h). PSP alone induced TNF-alpha but not IL-6, suggesting different signaling pathway engagement.

3. **TLR4 signaling mechanism proposed**: The authors propose PSP acts as a pathogen-associated molecular pattern (PAMP) recognized by TLR4, activating NF-kB via the TLR4-TIRAP/MAL-MyD88 pathway. PSP alone activates TLR4/p38 MAPK (producing TNF-alpha), while combined PSP+LPS additionally engages STAT3 signaling (producing IL-6).

## Key Evidence / Data Points

### Study Design
- **Model**: Male Wistar rats (250-300g), housed individually at 23 +/- 1 C, 12:12 light:dark cycle
- **Temperature measurement**: Biotelemetry (PhysioTel TA10TA-F40, Data Sciences International), accuracy +/- 0.1 C
- **PSP source**: Coriolus versicolor strain COV-1 (MycoMedica, Czech Republic)
- **PSP dose**: 100 mg/kg i.p. (lower dose of 50 mg/kg tested but showed no significant effect on LPS fever)
- **LPS source**: E. coli 0111:B4 (Sigma Chemicals), 50 ug/kg i.p.
- **Timing**: PSP injected at 7:00 a.m., LPS at 9:00 a.m. (2h interval)
- **Cytokine measurement**: Sandwich ELISA (R&D Systems), IL-6 detection limit 21 pg/ml, TNF-alpha 5 pg/ml
- **Statistics**: ANOVA followed by Student's t-test, p<0.05 significance
- **Ethics**: Approved by Local Bioethical Committee for Animal Care, Bydgoszcz, Poland (permission 17/2013)

### Quantitative Results

**Fever Duration (Section 3.1)**
- PSP/LPS group: average Tb 38.3 +/- 0.1 C from 19:00-04:00
- Saline/LPS group: average Tb 37.8 +/- 0.2 C from 19:00-04:00
- p < 0.01 for the difference
- Saline/LPS returned to baseline 12h post-injection; PSP/LPS at 21h post-injection

**IL-6 Levels (Section 3.2)**
- 3h post-injection: PSP/LPS = 1694.2 +/- 80 pg/ml vs. saline/LPS = 315.9 +/- 20 pg/ml (p<0.001)
- 14h post-injection: PSP/LPS = 379.7 +/- 7 pg/ml vs. saline/LPS = 32.9 +/- 9 pg/ml (p<0.001)
- PSP alone and saline controls: IL-6 below ELISA detection (62.5 pg/ml lowest standard)

**TNF-alpha Levels (Section 3.3)**
- 3h post-injection: PSP/LPS = 317.3 +/- 40 pg/ml vs. saline/LPS = 1342.9 +/- 310 pg/ml (p<0.001)
- PSP/LPS was still significantly higher than PSP/saline (225.9 +/- 4 pg/ml; p<0.01)
- At 14h: all groups below detection limit (5 pg/ml)

**Anti-IL-6 Antibody Blockade (Section 3.4)**
- Anti-IL-6 (30 ug/rat) at 17:00 prevented fever extension
- Tb in anti-IL-6 group: 37.8 +/- 0.1 C vs. PSP/LPS group: 38.3 +/- 0.1 C (p<0.01)
- Anti-IL-6 alone did not affect normal Tb or circadian rhythm

## Methodology

This was an in vivo animal study using biotelemetry for continuous body temperature monitoring. The experimental design involved four main treatment groups: (1) PSP/LPS, (2) saline/LPS (positive control), (3) PSP/saline, and (4) saline/saline (negative control). Cytokine levels were measured at two time points (3h and 14h post-PSP injection) using standardized sandwich ELISA kits. The anti-IL-6 antibody experiment provided mechanistic confirmation of IL-6 dependency. Statistical analysis used ANOVA with post-hoc Student's t-test.

## Limitations

- **Animal model only**: All experiments in male Wistar rats; direct clinical translation requires caution
- **Single PSP dose**: Only 100 mg/kg i.p. showed significant effects; 50 mg/kg did not. No dose-response curve reported for the fever interaction
- **Intraperitoneal administration**: Clinically, PSP is taken orally; pharmacokinetics and bioavailability may differ substantially
- **No in vitro PBMC data**: Authors note that in vitro PBMC reactivity studies from PSP-pretreated rats are still needed
- **TLR4 mechanism is inferred**: Not directly demonstrated; based on literature review of similar compounds (CVP, PSK) and gene expression studies by other groups
- **Sample sizes not explicitly stated**: The n per group is not clearly reported in the text (likely 6-8 based on similar biotelemetry studies)
