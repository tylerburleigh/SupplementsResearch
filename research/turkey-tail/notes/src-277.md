# Source Note: src-277

**Title:** Efficacy of adjuvant Immunochemotherapy with polysaccharide K for patients with curatively resected colorectal cancer: a meta-analysis of centrally randomized controlled clinical trials

**Authors:** Junichi Sakamoto, Satoshi Morita, Koji Oba, Takanori Matsui, Michiya Kobayashi, Hiroaki Nakazato, Yasuo Ohashi

**Journal:** Cancer Immunology, Immunotherapy (2006) 55:404-411

**Year:** 2005 (published online August 2005)

**Quality:** ok -- full text available, substantive paper with methods, results, discussion, and references.

---

## Core Findings

This meta-analysis pooled data from 3 centrally randomized controlled clinical trials (N=1,094 patients) evaluating PSK (polysaccharide K, derived from Coriolus versicolor) as adjuvant immunochemotherapy for curatively resected colorectal cancer. Adding PSK to standard chemotherapy (mitomycin C + oral fluorinated pyrimidines) significantly improved both overall survival (OR 0.71, 95% CI 0.55-0.90, P=0.006) and disease-free survival (OR 0.72, 95% CI 0.58-0.90, P=0.003) compared to chemotherapy alone. This represents a 29% reduction in death rate and 28% reduction in recurrence.

## Key Evidence/Data Points

- **Total patients:** 1,094 across 3 trials (578 immunochemotherapy, 516 chemotherapy alone)
- **5-year overall survival:** 79.0% (PSK+chemo) vs 72.2% (chemo alone); 121 vs 143 deaths
- **5-year disease-free survival:** 72.2% (PSK+chemo) vs 65.9% (chemo alone); 161 vs 176 recurrences
- **Heterogeneity tests:** P=0.50 (OS), P=0.22 (DFS) -- supporting homogeneity
- **Only 1 of 3 individual trials** showed statistically significant survival advantage on its own (Mitomi et al. 1992); significance emerged in pooled analysis
- **Treatment regimen:** MMC induction + long-term oral fluorinated pyrimidines + PSK; minimum 5-year follow-up

## Methodology

- **Design:** Meta-analysis of published and unpublished randomized controlled trials
- **Search:** MEDLINE (December 2004) + pharmaceutical industry inquiries; identified 48 potentially relevant papers, screened down to 3 qualifying trials
- **Eligibility criteria:** (1) evaluation of adjuvant chemo +/- PSK; (2) randomized with central assignment; (3) after curative tumor resection; (4) same chemo in control arm
- **Statistical method:** Weighted average of individual log hazard ratios; indirect log hazard ratio and variance estimation method (Parmar et al. 1998); SAS 8.02
- **Analysis type:** Intent-to-treat without patient exclusion
- **Endpoints:** Overall survival and disease-free survival
- **Exclusions from meta-analysis:** Trials comparing with surgery alone, using suppository tegafur, PSK monotherapy without chemo, or not truly randomized

## PSK Description

- Extracted from mycelia of Coriolus versicolor strain CM-101 (basidiomycetes)
- Consists of proteins and polysaccharides; major glycoside portion is beta-1,4-glucan
- Average molecular weight: ~100 kDa
- Mechanism: induces IL-2, interferon-gamma; stimulates LAK cells; enhances NK cells; activates leukocyte chemotactic locomotion and phagocytic activity
- Widely prescribed in Japan for oral ingestion

## Limitations

1. **Small number of trials (n=3)** included in the meta-analysis -- only 3 of 48 identified studies met inclusion criteria
2. **Individual trials underpowered** -- only 1/3 showed significance alone; effect detectable only in pooled analysis
3. **All trials conducted in Japan** with Japanese patient populations -- generalizability to other populations uncertain
4. **Chemotherapy regimens varied** slightly across the three trials (different oral fluorinated pyrimidines)
5. **Randomization ratios differed** (1:1 in two trials, 1:2 in one trial)
6. **Publication bias possible** -- authors actively searched for unpublished trials, but cannot exclude bias
7. **No individual patient data meta-analysis** -- published summary statistics used; authors note an IPD meta-analysis was in progress at time of publication

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q1 (Efficacy):** Strong evidence for PSK efficacy in colorectal cancer adjuvant setting; significant OS and DFS improvements
- **Q2 (Clinical trial design/dosing):** Details treatment regimens across 3 RCTs; MMC induction + oral fluoropyrimidines + PSK; 1:1 or 1:2 randomization; intent-to-treat analysis; minimum 5-year follow-up
- **Q3 (Dosing specifics):** PSK dosing not individually detailed in this meta-analysis; references original trials for specifics
- **Q4 (PSK vs other preparations):** Describes PSK as protein-bound polysaccharide from Coriolus versicolor CM-101; composition details (beta-1,4-glucan, ~100 kDa); distinguishes from other immunotherapies (OK-432, levamisole)
- **Q5 (Mechanism of action):** Mechanisms described: IL-2 and IFN-gamma induction, LAK cell stimulation, NK cell enhancement, leukocyte activation, phagocytic activity
- **Q7 (Safety/side effects):** Not directly addressed; PSK described as having favorable effects on immune function; long-term oral administration noted as feasible
