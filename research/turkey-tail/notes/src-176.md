# Source src-176: Lentinan and PD-L1 Downregulation in Gastric Cancer

**Citation:** Ina H, Yoneda M, Kanda M, et al. (2016) Lentinan, A Shiitake Mushroom beta-Glucan, Stimulates Tumor-Specific Adaptive Immunity through PD-L1 Down-Regulation in Gastric Cancer Cells. *Med Chem (Los Angeles)* 6: 710-714. DOI: 10.4172/2161-0444.1000419

**Note:** This paper is about **lentinan** (from shiitake mushroom, *Lentinula edodes*), NOT about PSK (from turkey tail, *Trametes versicolor*). However, both are beta-glucan biological response modifiers used in cancer immunotherapy, so mechanistic findings are transferable.

## Core Findings

1. **Clinical survival benefit:** Lentinan combined with S-1 based chemotherapy significantly prolonged median overall survival in stage-4 gastric cancer patients (401 days vs. 148 days, P=0.000243, N=34 lentinan + 18 control). Matched pair analysis confirmed benefit (OR 8.0 for 1-year survival, P=0.0455).

2. **PD-L1 downregulation mechanism:** Lentinan at clinically relevant concentrations (1-10 ng/mL) reduced PD-L1 mRNA expression in all three gastric cancer cell lines tested (MKN1, MKN45, NUGC3), primarily through suppression of the MAPK signaling pathway. AKT pathway was not significantly affected.

3. **Immunomodulatory rather than cytotoxic:** Lentinan showed minimal direct anti-proliferative activity at clinically achievable concentrations (<10 ng/mL), indicating its therapeutic effect is primarily through immune modulation rather than direct tumor cell killing.

## Key Evidence and Data Points

- **Retrospective cohort:** 52 stage-4 gastric cancer patients (34 lentinan + chemo, 18 chemo alone) over 5 years (2011-2015) at Nagoya Memorial Hospital
- **Survival:** Median OS 401 days (95% CI 257-747) with lentinan vs. 148 days (95% CI 79-225) without (log-rank P=0.000243)
- **Matched pair analysis:** 15 pairs matched for age, gender, and regimen; OR 8.0 for 1-year survival (McNemar's P=0.0455)
- **Pharmacokinetics:** Serum beta-glucan 0.24-16.7 ng/mL after 2 mg IV lentinan; median 13.4 ng/mL immediately post-infusion declining to 2.20 ng/mL by day 21
- **MAPK pathway:** MAPK mRNA significantly reduced at 1 ng/mL lentinan in NUGC3 cells (P=0.036); AKT not significantly changed (P=0.313)
- **NF-kB and STAT3:** Showed non-significant decreasing trends at 1-10 ng/mL lentinan
- **Paradoxical at high dose:** 100 ng/mL lentinan increased PD-L1 expression in NUGC3 cells, suggesting a biphasic or dose-dependent response

## Methodology

- **Clinical:** Retrospective chart review; Kaplan-Meier survival analysis; matched pair design with McNemar's test
- **In vitro:** WST-1 proliferation assay; real-time qPCR (delta-delta Ct method) on 3 human gastric cancer cell lines
- **Concentrations tested:** 0, 0.1, 1, 10, 100 ng/mL lentinan
- **Signaling molecules examined:** PD-L1, MAPK, AKT, NF-kB, STAT3

## Limitations

- **Not about PSK/turkey tail** -- the beta-glucan studied is lentinan from shiitake mushroom
- **Small sample size:** Only 52 stage-4 patients total (34 lentinan, 18 control); 15 matched pairs
- **Retrospective, non-randomized** design introduces potential selection bias
- **Chemotherapy regimens differed** between groups (S-1 alone, S-1/cisplatin, PSC triple therapy)
- **mRNA only:** PD-L1 protein expression was not measured
- **Single institution** (Nagoya Memorial Hospital)
- **Specific to gastric cancer** -- generalizability to other cancers unknown

## Relevance to Turkey Tail Research

While this study examines lentinan (shiitake) rather than PSK (turkey tail), the findings are relevant because:
- Both are beta-glucan biological response modifiers
- Both modulate immune checkpoint pathways (PD-L1/PD-1 axis)
- Both are used as adjuncts to chemotherapy in gastric cancer
- The MAPK-mediated PD-L1 suppression mechanism may be a shared property of medicinal mushroom beta-glucans
- The clinical pharmacokinetic data (serum levels 0.24-16.7 ng/mL) provides a reference for beta-glucan bioavailability

## Claims to Verify

- **N=52 patients:** 81 eligible total, 52 classified as stage 4. Verified in text (line 157-158). Numbers are plausible for a single-institution retrospective study.
- **Matched pairs N=15:** Only 15 matched pairs from 34+18=52 patients seems low. Likely reflects difficulty matching on regimen (only S-1 and S-1/cisplatin pairs were matchable; PSC triple therapy had 9 vs 0).
- **Odds ratio 8.0:** Based on only 15 pairs; with this small sample, the confidence interval would be extremely wide. The P=0.0455 is barely significant.
