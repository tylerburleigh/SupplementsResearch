# src-095: Benson et al. (2019) - Mycelium vs. Fermented Substrate Immune Activation

## Core Findings

This in vitro study demonstrated that Trametes versicolor (Turkey tail) mycelium and its fermented rice substrate have **distinct and complementary** immune-activating properties on human PBMC cultures. The mycelium was potent at triggering CD69 immune-cell activation (especially NKT cells), while the fermented substrate was the primary driver of cytokine induction (IL-2, IL-6, IFN-gamma, IL-1ra, IL-10, G-CSF, IL-8, MIP-1alpha). The initial unfermented rice substrate was largely bioinactive, confirming that fungal fermentation transforms the substrate into an immunologically active material.

## Key Evidence

1. **CD69 activation**: TvM aqueous fraction robustly induced CD69 on lymphocytes and monocytes (comparable to LPS positive control), while FS only minorly induced CD69. The aqueous fraction was 10x less material by dry weight than the solid fraction yet more potent for lymphocyte activation (P < 0.02).

2. **Cytokine induction**: FS drove large, dose-dependent increases in 8+ cytokines spanning pro-inflammatory (IL-2, IL-6), anti-inflammatory (IL-1ra, IL-10), anti-viral (IFN-gamma, MIP-1alpha), and regenerative (G-CSF, IL-8) categories. TvM showed more modest cytokine effects.

3. **Beta-glucans and water-soluble components**: Both insoluble (solid fraction) and water-soluble (aqueous fraction) components showed bioactivity. Monocytes were more strongly activated by solid fractions (consistent with TLR-2/TLR-4 beta-glucan signaling), while lymphocytes responded more to aqueous fractions.

4. **Dry weight analysis**: Aqueous extract yields were 4.5% (IS), 11% (FS), 12% (TvM) w/w, meaning the aqueous fractions contained substantially less material yet drove significant bioactivity.

5. **PSK/PSP mentioned**: Tv contains precursors to PSK and PSP proteoglycans; PSK is used clinically in Japan for gastric cancer. The lipid fraction of PSK is instrumental to TLR-2 induction.

## Methodology

- **Study type**: In vitro PBMC culture from 3 healthy human donors
- **Test materials**: Tv mycelium (TvM), fermented rice substrate (FS), initial rice substrate (IS) -- each split into aqueous (0.22 micron filtered) and solid (homogenized, unfiltered) fractions
- **Culture conditions**: 24h incubation at 37C, 5% CO2; triplicate treatments at 0.08, 0.4, and 2 mg/mL
- **Assays**: Flow cytometry (CD3, CD56, CD69) on lymphocyte/monocyte subsets; Luminex multiplex cytokine arrays (10 analytes)
- **Cultivation**: Organic rice flour, autoclaved, inoculated with Tv spawn, 42 days at 20-24C in clean room
- **Extraction**: Cold-water (PBS) extraction, no heat/pressure applied
- **Positive controls**: LPS (10 ng/mL) and IL-2 (100 IU/mL)
- **Statistics**: 2-tailed independent t-test; P < 0.05 significant, P < 0.01 highly significant

## Limitations

- **In vitro only**: No human clinical outcomes measured; 3 donors is very small
- **No cognitive, neuroprotective, or longevity endpoints**: Entirely immune-cell focused
- **Industry funded**: Fungi Perfecti, LLC (Paul Stamets' company) sponsored the study; multiple authors are employees
- **Paul Stamets holds related patents** and is founder/owner of the sponsoring company
- **No pharmacokinetics**: No absorption, distribution, metabolism, or excretion data
- **No safety/adverse event data**: In vitro only; no dosing in humans assessed
- **Ongoing clinical trial mentioned** but results not yet available

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q1 (cognitive/neuroprotective clinical trials)**: Not addressed. Purely in vitro immune study.
- **Q2 (longevity biomarkers)**: Not directly addressed. Anti-inflammatory cytokine induction (IL-1ra, IL-10) and regenerative markers (G-CSF, IL-8) are mentioned but as in vitro cytokine measures, not clinical longevity endpoints.
- **Q3 (bioactive compounds, pathways)**: Directly relevant. Documents that immune activation involves both beta-glucans (solid fraction, TLR-2/TLR-4 mediated) and water-soluble components. Identifies FS as rich in cytokine-inducing compounds. Mentions PSK/PSP precursors and TLR-2 pathway.
- **Q4 (dosing, extraction methods)**: Relevant for extraction methodology. Cold-water extraction preserved bioactivity. Doses tested in vitro (0.08-2 mg/mL of starting material) provide reference points but are not human doses. Cultivation protocol detailed (42 days on rice flour).
- **Q5 (direct evidence vs. extrapolation)**: Strongly relevant as an example of extrapolation -- the immune effects documented here are the mechanistic basis that gets extrapolated to claims about cognition and longevity, but the study itself provides zero direct evidence for those outcomes.
- **Q6 (risks, adverse events)**: Not addressed.
- **Q7 (null results, critical reviews)**: No null results or critical reviews reported.
