# src-177: Adjuvant immunochemotherapy with oral Tegafur/Uracil plus PSK in stage II/III colorectal cancer

**Citation:** Ohwada S, Ikeya T, Yokomori T, et al. *Br J Cancer* 90, 1003-1010 (2004). DOI: 10.1038/sj.bjc.6601619

**Quality:** Full text available. Peer-reviewed RCT published in British Journal of Cancer.

## Core Findings

1. **PSK + UFT significantly improved 5-year disease-free survival** in stage II/III colorectal cancer patients (73.0% vs 58.8%, P=0.016), with an even stronger effect in stage III patients (DFS 60.0% vs 32.1%, P=0.002; OS 74.6% vs 46.4%, P=0.003).

2. **PSK reduced recurrence by 43.6% overall and by 63.4% in stage III**, with a particularly notable reduction in lung metastases (OR 0.27, P=0.02). No significant benefit was observed in stage II subgroup analysis.

3. **Treatment was well-tolerated** with only grade 1-2 toxicity in 15.1% of patients, no grade 3-4 toxicity, and 89% compliance over 2 years.

## Dosing Protocol

- **PSK:** 3.0 g/day orally (KRESTIN, Kureha Chemical Industry Co., Japan)
- **UFT (Tegafur/Uracil):** 300 mg/day orally
- **MMC (Mitomycin C):** IV bolus 12 mg/m2 on postop day 1, 8 mg/m2 on postop day 2
- **Duration:** PSK + UFT or UFT alone for 2 years, starting 2 weeks post-surgery
- **PSK source:** Protein-bound polysaccharide extracted from mycelia of *Coriolus versicolor* (same as turkey tail mushroom)

## Key Evidence

| Endpoint | PSK+UFT | UFT alone | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5-yr DFS (all) | 73.0% | 58.8% | 0.016 |
| 5-yr OS (all) | 81.8% | 72.1% | 0.056 |
| 5-yr DFS (stage III) | 60.0% | 32.1% | 0.002 |
| 5-yr OS (stage III) | 74.6% | 46.4% | 0.003 |
| Recurrence rate (all) | 23.3% | 36.5% | 0.06 |

## Multivariate Analysis (Cox Model)

Independent predictors of recurrence:
- Regional metastases: RR 2.973 (P<0.001)
- Omission of PSK: RR 2.109 (P=0.007)
- Higher primary tumor: RR 4.398 (P=0.047)

## Safety Profile

- 15.1% any toxicity (all grade 1-2); no grade 3-4 toxicity
- GI toxicity: 5.8%; hematological: 4.9%; hepatotoxic: 2.4%
- Only 6.3% temporarily discontinued due to adverse effects
- 89% overall drug compliance

## Mechanism of Action

PSK induces IL-2, IFN-gamma, IL-1, IL-6, IL-8, TNF production; stimulates LAK and NK cell activity; downregulates TGF-beta1, uPA, MMP-2, MMP-9; activates programmed cell death. The marked reduction in lung metastases may reflect immunological and biochemical modulation.

## Study Design Details

- **Design:** Randomized controlled trial, 2:1 allocation (PSK:control)
- **Enrollment:** October 1994 - March 1997, 19 affiliate hospitals in Gunma Oncology Study Group
- **Sample:** 207 registered, 205 analyzed (137 PSK, 68 control); 123 stage II, 82 stage III
- **Planned sample:** 240 (80% power to detect 15% DFS difference, alpha=0.05)
- **Surgery:** Colon resection or tumor-specific mesorectal excision with D2/D3 lymphadenectomy
- **Follow-up:** Every 2 weeks first month, monthly for 2 years, then every 2-3 months to 5 years
- **Analysis:** Intention-to-treat, Kaplan-Meier, log-rank test, Cox proportional-hazards

## Limitations

1. **Small sample size:** Only 207 enrolled (target 240), with uneven allocation (2:1). Control arm only 68 patients.
2. **Control arm not standard of care:** UFT alone was not the standard 5-FU/LV comparator, limiting comparison to established regimens.
3. **Stage II subgroup underpowered:** No DFS or OS benefit detected in stage II, but subgroup was small.
4. **No blinding:** Open-label design (not placebo-controlled).
5. **UFT monotherapy performed poorly in stage III** (OS 46.4% at 5 years, comparable to untreated historical controls), raising questions about whether UFT alone was an appropriate control.

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q1 (Dosing/Protocols):** Strong - detailed dosing protocol for PSK 3g/day + UFT 300mg/day for 2 years
- **Q2 (Clinical Efficacy):** Strong - primary endpoint data with 5-year follow-up, significant DFS benefit, especially in stage III
- **Q3 (Bioavailability):** Indirect - urinary tegafur concentration measured in subset; PSK oral bioavailability not directly assessed
- **Q4 (Mechanism):** Moderate - discusses immunomodulatory mechanisms (IL-2, IFN-gamma, NK cells, MMPs) and notes lung metastasis reduction as evidence
- **Q5 (Comparison to other treatments):** Moderate - compares outcomes to historical 5-FU/LV and 5-FU/levamisole data; PSK+UFT comparable or superior in stage III
- **Q6 (Safety):** Strong - detailed adverse effect data; only grade 1-2 toxicity, no grade 3-4
- **Q7 (Study design):** Strong - RCT with clear methodology, intention-to-treat analysis, multivariate models

## Claims to Verify

- The 2:1 allocation ratio is unusual; verify that the statistical power calculation accounts for this (it does - protocol specified 160 PSK, 80 control).
- Stage III control group OS of 46.4% seems notably poor compared to historical 5-FU/LV data; the authors acknowledge UFT dose intensity may have been lower than standard.
- Compliance verification relied on interviews and urinary tegafur in only 24 randomly selected patients, not pill counts or diaries.
