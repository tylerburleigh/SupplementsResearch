# src-086: Immunomodulatory Properties of Coriolus versicolor: The Role of Polysaccharopeptide

**Authors:** Saleh MH, Rashedi I, Keating A (2017)
**Venue:** Frontiers in Immunology
**Citations:** 84
**Type:** Review article (peer-reviewed)

## Core Findings

This is a comprehensive review of PSP (polysaccharopeptide) from Coriolus versicolor, focused almost exclusively on immunomodulatory mechanisms in the context of cancer adjunct therapy. PSP induces a predominantly pro-inflammatory cytokine profile (TNF-alpha, IL-12, IFN-gamma, IL-1beta, IL-2, IL-6) and activates immune cells (T cells, B cells, NK cells, macrophages) via multiple pattern-recognition receptors (Dectin-1, CR3, TLR-2, TLR-4). Clinical data are limited to cancer patients and a few healthy-volunteer studies showing immunostimulatory effects; there are no cognitive, neuroprotective, or longevity outcomes reported.

## Key Evidence

### Bioactive Compounds and Mechanisms (Q3)
- **PSP and PSK** are the two main polysaccharopeptides from CV. Both ~100 kDa. PSP (COV-1 strain, China) has 90-10% polysaccharide-to-peptide ratio; PSK (CM-101 strain, Japan) has 60-40% ratio.
- PSP polysaccharide moiety: beta-(1->3)-D-glucan branching at 4' and 6'. Contains mannose, xylose, galactose, fructose. PSK contains arabinose and rhamnose instead of fructose.
- **Extraction:** PSP isolated by boiling COV-1 mycelia/fruiting bodies in water, ethanol precipitation.
- **Receptor engagement:** Dectin-1, CR3, TLR-2, TLR-4, TLR-5, TLR-6, TLR-7. Signaling via p38 MAPK and NF-kappaB pathways.
- PSK mediates DC and T cell activation via TLR-2 signaling (demonstrated with neu transgenic mice).

### Cytokine Profile (Q2, Q3)
- TNF-alpha: >3.5-fold increase in human PBMCs; also elevated in mouse peritoneal macrophages, healthy rats.
- IL-12: threefold increase in murine splenic lymphocytes with CV extract.
- IFN-gamma: increased in healthy donor and breast cancer patient PBMCs.
- IL-2: almost eightfold increase; IL-18 about twofold increase in murine splenic lymphocytes.
- IL-1alpha: threefold increase in human PBMCs.
- IL-6: dose-dependent increase in HL-60 cells; increased in PBMCs from breast cancer patients and healthy donors.
- GM-CSF, G-CSF, M-CSF: all elevated.

### Antioxidant Effects (Q2)
- PSP induces SOD activity in lymphocytes; partially rescues SOD activity reduced by tumor implantation or irradiation.
- PSK mimics SOD radical scavenging activity in vitro (extent depends on PSK peptide content).
- SOD induction rescues NK cell cytolytic activity impaired by reactive oxygen species.
- i.p. PSK treatment in tumor-bearing rats slowed tumor progression, decreased oxidative stress, restored NK killing/binding.

### Clinical Data (Q4, Q5, Q6)
- **Healthy volunteers:** Double-blind crossover study (10 months); PSP + Danshen increased T helper cell counts, elevated IL-2R expression, increased IFN-γ production (ref 88).
- **Gut microbiome:** Randomized trial showed PSP had prebiotic-like effects comparable to amoxicillin modulation (ref 89).
- **HPV clearance:** ~90% of oral HPV-positive patients cleared virus after 2 months with CV extract + Ganoderma lucidum (ref 90).
- **Breast cancer:** Phase 1 dose-escalation (n=9); increased NK cytotoxic function and lymphocyte counts after radiotherapy (ref 91). PSP/Danshen for 6 months in 80+ patients: increased T-helper and B cell counts, decreased serum IL-2R (ref 92).
- **NSCLC:** PSP increased leukocyte, neutrophil counts, serum IgG/IgM; less disease progression withdrawal but no disease-parameter improvements (ref 93).
- **Nasopharyngeal carcinoma:** PSP/Danshen alleviated radiation-induced lymphopenia in RCT (ref 96).
- **Survival:** PSP extended 5-year survival in esophageal cancer (double-blind); improved survival in lung, gastric, esophageal carcinoma with radiotherapy (refs 97, 98).

### Limitations and Concerns (Q6, Q7)
- **Endotoxin contamination:** Many preclinical studies did not report endotoxin testing -- results may be confounded.
- **Route of administration:** Most animal studies used i.p. or s.c. injection, not clinically relevant oral route.
- **Standardization:** Antitumor effects varied significantly based on fermentation duration, extraction method, and preparation.
- **Contradictory results:** PSP showed no direct cytotoxicity on hepatoma, sarcoma, melanoma, breast cancer lines; contradictory apoptosis results even for HL-60.
- **Potential harm:** IL-1beta and IL-6 induction could promote MDSC expansion and tumor progression in some contexts.
- **Conflict of interest:** AK received funding from Purapharm Inc. to investigate Yun Zhi effects on NK cell activity.
- **No cognitive or neuroprotective data at all** -- the paper is entirely focused on cancer immunotherapy.

## Methodology
This is a narrative review synthesizing English-language literature on CV immunomodulatory effects. No systematic search strategy described. Covers in vitro, in vivo animal, and clinical studies through 2017. Focuses on PSP (COV-1 strain) rather than PSK (CM-101 strain), though PSK data are included where relevant.

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q1 (cognitive/neuroprotective):** No evidence. This paper does not address cognitive or neuroprotective outcomes.
- **Q2 (longevity biomarkers):** Limited relevance. PSP induces pro-inflammatory cytokines and SOD activity (oxidative stress), but only in cancer/immune contexts, not longevity. No telomere, senescence, or mortality data in healthy populations.
- **Q3 (bioactive compounds, pharmacokinetics, pathways):** Strong evidence for compound characterization (PSP/PSK structure, molecular weight), receptor engagement (Dectin-1, CR3, TLRs), and signaling pathways (p38 MAPK, NF-kappaB). No pharmacokinetic data. No brain/aging pathway data.
- **Q4 (dosing, formulations):** Limited. Extraction method described (water boil + ethanol precipitation). Clinical doses not specified in detail.
- **Q5 (cognition/longevity claims as extrapolation):** Strongly supports the view that current claims are extrapolation. This review, the most comprehensive on CV immunomodulation, contains zero cognitive or longevity data. Any such claims would be extrapolation from immune effects.
- **Q6 (risks, drug interactions):** Notes potential for IL-1beta/IL-6 to promote MDSC expansion. No specific adverse event data in healthy individuals. Conflicts of interest noted.
- **Q7 (null results, critical reviews):** Documents contradictory cytotoxicity results, lack of endotoxin testing in many studies, and methodological standardization problems.

## Claims to Verify
- The 5-year survival extension in esophageal cancer (ref 97, Kidd 2000) is cited from a secondary review, not a primary trial report -- the original source should be verified.
- The claim that "almost 90%" of HPV-positive patients cleared the virus (ref 90) comes from a preliminary clinical trial by Donatini (2014) in the International Journal of Medicinal Mushrooms -- sample size and study quality should be verified.
- The "more than 80" breast cancer patients figure (ref 92) needs verification -- the actual sample size in Wong et al. 2005 should be confirmed.
