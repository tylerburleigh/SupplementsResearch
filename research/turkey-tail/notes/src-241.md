# Source src-241: Phytomedicine Article (2014)

## Citation
Luo KW, Yue GGL, Ko CH, Lee JKM, Gao S, Li LF, Li G, Fung KP, Leung PC, Lau CBS. "In vivo and in vitro anti-tumor and anti-metastasis effects of Coriolus versicolor aqueous extract on mouse mammary 4T1 carcinoma." *Phytomedicine* 21 (2014): 1078-1087. DOI: 10.1016/j.phymed.2014.04.020

## Core Findings

This study is the first to demonstrate that **Coriolus versicolor (CV) aqueous extract** (hot-water decoction of dried fruiting bodies) exhibits anti-tumor, anti-metastasis, immunomodulatory, and bone-protective effects in a metastatic breast cancer mouse model (4T1 orthotopic in BALB/c mice). The extract worked through non-cytotoxic mechanisms: it inhibited cancer cell migration/invasion via MMP-9 suppression, reduced lung metastasis by ~71%, decreased primary tumor weight by 36%, and enhanced pro-inflammatory cytokine production from spleen lymphocytes.

## Key Evidence

### In Vitro Results
- **No direct cytotoxicity**: CV aqueous extract at 0.125-2 mg/ml did not inhibit 4T1 cell proliferation after 24 or 48h; cell viability remained ~85% even at 2 mg/ml after 72h.
- **Migration inhibition**: Non-cytotoxic doses (1-2 mg/ml) significantly inhibited cell migration in scratch wound assay at 9h and 18h (p < 0.05).
- **Invasion inhibition**: 0.5, 1, and 2 mg/ml CV inhibited 4T1 cell invasion by 40.7%, 49.8%, and 61.5% respectively (p < 0.05).
- **MMP-9 suppression**: Gelatin zymography and Western blot confirmed dose-dependent suppression of MMP-9 enzyme activity and protein levels; MMP-2 was unaffected. No change in MMP-2 or MMP-9 mRNA expression was observed, suggesting post-transcriptional regulation.

### In Vivo Results (BALB/c mice, 4T1 orthotopic, n=10/group)
- **Tumor weight reduction**: CV (1 g/kg oral gavage daily x 4 weeks) decreased tumor weight by 36.0% vs. control (p < 0.01).
- **Lung metastasis reduction**: Tumor burden in lungs decreased by 70.7% (from 6.19% to 1.81%) vs. control (p < 0.001).
- **Liver metastasis**: No significant effect on liver metastasis.
- **Bone protection**: Micro-CT showed CV-treated mice had 10.3% higher bone volume vs. control (control lost ~30% BV vs. naive), indicating protection against breast cancer-induced osteolysis (p < 0.05).
- **Immunomodulation**: Spleen lymphocytes from CV-treated mice showed significantly higher proliferative responses and increased production of IL-2, IL-6, IL-12, TNF-alpha, and IFN-gamma. IL-10 was unchanged.
- **Safety**: No body weight loss; no significant changes in plasma enzymes (AST, ALT, ALP, CK). In contrast, doxorubicin (positive control) caused significant body weight loss.

### Preparation and Composition
- **Extract**: 1 kg dried CV fruiting bodies (Yunnan, China), hot water reflux extraction, yield 15.7% (w/w).
- **Polysaccharide content**: 8.34% (w/w); amino acid content 38.4% (w/w).
- **UPLC molecular weight fractions**: Four fractions -- (a) 270-2000 kDa (peak 376 kDa), (b) 50-270 kDa (peak 169 kDa, likely containing PSK/PSP), (c) 12-50 kDa (peak 41.9 kDa), (d) <12 kDa (peak 1.1 kDa).

## Methodology

- **In vitro**: MTT cytotoxicity assay, scratch wound healing assay, transwell migration/invasion assay, gelatin zymography, Western blot.
- **In vivo**: BALB/c mice (female, 6-8 weeks), 4T1 cells (4x10^5) inoculated at mammary fat pad. Treatment started 1 week post-inoculation: CV (1 g/kg oral gavage daily), doxorubicin (2 mg/kg i.p. twice/week), or distilled water control. Duration: 4 weeks. n=10/group.
- **Assessments**: Body weight, tumor weight, H&E histology of lungs/livers for metastasis burden, micro-CT for bone volume, spleen lymphocyte proliferation and cytokine ELISA.
- **Statistics**: One-way ANOVA, p < 0.05 significant.

## Limitations

1. **Single dose in vivo**: Only one oral dose (1 g/kg) was tested; no dose-response in animals.
2. **No mechanistic molecular pathway data**: The study did not identify specific signaling pathways; MMP-9 mRNA was unchanged but protein was suppressed, suggesting translational regulation, but the mechanism was not further explored.
3. **No liver metastasis effect**: CV did not reduce liver metastasis, which remains unexplained.
4. **Whole extract, not purified**: The aqueous extract is a complex mixture; specific bioactive components (PSK, PSP, or others) responsible for each effect were not isolated.
5. **Mouse model only**: Results in immunocompetent BALB/c mice; human clinical relevance not directly established.
6. **No direct anti-osteoclast data**: Bone protection may be indirect (via reduced tumor burden) rather than direct osteoclast inhibition.

## Relevance to Research Questions

This source is highly relevant to understanding the bioactivity, anti-cancer mechanisms, preparation methods, safety profile, and immunomodulatory effects of Turkey Tail (Coriolus versicolor) aqueous extract in the context of metastatic breast cancer.
