# src-101: Induction of cell cycle changes by PSP (I'm-Yunity)

**Citation:** Hsieh TC, Wu P, Park S, Wu JM. BMC Complement Altern Med. 2006;6:30. DOI: 10.1186/1472-6882-6-30. 47 citations.

**Quality:** ok. Full text available (open access BMC). Raw PDF-to-text conversion, some formatting artifacts but content is substantive.

## Core Findings

This is an **in vitro mechanistic study** examining how water extracts of I'm-Yunity (PSP, a polysaccharo-peptide from *Coriolus versicolor* Cov-1 strain mycelia) suppress proliferation and induce apoptosis in two human leukemia cell lines (HL-60 and U-937). The study identifies multiple molecular targets: downregulation of Rb, NF-kB (p65/p50), COX2, bcl-2, survivin, and phospho-ERK; upregulation of bax, cytochrome c, and STAT1; and PARP cleavage. These are cancer-relevant pathways, not brain/aging pathways.

## Key Evidence

- **Dose range:** 0.1, 0.5, 1.0 mg/ml water extracts, 72h treatment
- **Cell cycle:** G1/S arrest in HL-60, G2/M arrest in U-937 (cell-type dependent)
- **Apoptosis markers:** Increased bax/bcl-2 ratio, cytochrome c release from mitochondria, PARP cleavage (112 kDa to 89 kDa)
- **NF-kB suppression:** p65 decreased more than p50; COX2 reduced 65-85%
- **STAT1 increase:** Up to 8.14-fold at 1.0 mg/ml; phospho-ERK decreased to 0.49-fold
- **Extraction:** Hot water at 95C for 4-5h, ethanol precipitation, fractionation retaining >40 kDa polysaccharo-peptides; commercial capsules contain 340 mg powder

## Methodology

- **Design:** In vitro cell culture study (no animal or human subjects)
- **Cell lines:** Human promyelocytic leukemia HL-60 and monocytic leukemia U-937 (both from ATCC)
- **Assays:** Flow cytometry (cell cycle, sub-G1 apoptosis), Western blot (protein expression), real-time PCR (COX2 mRNA), subcellular fractionation (mitochondrial vs. cytosolic)
- **Replication:** Results represent averages of 2-4 experiments

## Limitations

- **In vitro only** -- no animal models, no human clinical data
- No pharmacokinetic data (absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination)
- Uses immortalized cancer cell lines, not normal cells (though authors note PSP had no effect on normal human lymphocytes in prior work)
- No dose-response quantification yielding IC50 values
- Funding from Hong Kong Healthcare Center Ltd. (unrestricted grant) -- potential COI though authors declare none
- I'm-Yunity is a specific commercial product (ICM Holdings Ltd., Hong Kong)

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q1 (Human clinical trials, cognitive/neuroprotective):** Not relevant. In vitro leukemia cell study only.
- **Q2 (Human longevity biomarkers):** Not relevant. No human data.
- **Q3 (Bioactive compounds, pathways):** Moderately relevant. Identifies PSP composition (polysaccharo-peptide, >40 kDa, 2:1 polysaccharide:peptide ratio) and molecular mechanisms (NF-kB, COX2, STAT1, ERK, bcl-2/bax pathways). These pathways are relevant to cancer biology but not directly to brain aging or neuroprotection.
- **Q4 (Dosing, extraction, formulation):** Relevant for extraction method details (hot water 95C, 4-5h, ethanol precipitation) and capsule content (340 mg). In vitro doses (0.1-1.0 mg/ml) cannot be directly translated to human dosing.
- **Q5 (Cognition/longevity claims -- direct evidence vs. extrapolation):** The conclusion states findings "may contribute to the reported clinical attributes and overall health benefits" of PSP. This is explicitly speculative. Any cognition/longevity claims derived from this paper would be extrapolation.
- **Q6 (Risks, drug interactions):** Not addressed.
- **Q7 (Null results, critical reviews):** Not relevant.

## Claims to Verify

- COX2 reduction of 65-85% is reported in the text (line ~716) but the figure data show fold changes of 0.16 and 0.35 (84% and 65% reduction respectively), which is consistent.
- STAT1 fold increase of 8.14 at 1.0 mg/ml is from Figure 7 legend data and appears reliable as a single data point.
- No participant counts or sample sizes in the clinical sense -- this is a cell culture study with 2-4 experimental replicates.
