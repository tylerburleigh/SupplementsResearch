# src-178: Biological mechanism and clinical effect of PSK (KRESTIN)

## Source Metadata
- **Title:** Biological mechanism and clinical effect of protein-bound polysaccharide K (KRESTIN): review of development and future perspectives
- **DOI:** 10.1007/s00595-011-0075-7
- **Venue:** Surgery Today (Springer)
- **Quality:** ok -- full text available, substantive peer-reviewed review article

## Core Findings

This is a comprehensive review of PSK (Krestin), a protein-bound polysaccharide extracted from Coriolus versicolor (turkey tail mushroom) mycelium, covering its biological mechanisms and clinical efficacy as a cancer immunotherapy adjuvant. PSK operates through three mechanisms: (1) neutralizing immunosuppressive factors (TGF-beta, IL-10, PGE2), (2) activating immune cells (DC maturation, Th1/Th2 correction, IL-15 induction), and (3) direct anti-tumor actions (apoptosis, anti-metastasis, anti-angiogenesis). Clinical efficacy is established in gastric and colorectal cancer through multiple RCTs and meta-analyses. The paper has **no relevance to cognitive or neuroprotective outcomes, longevity biomarkers, or brain aging** -- it is exclusively an oncology review.

## Key Evidence

### Bioactive compound and pharmacokinetics (Q3)
- PSK: average MW ~100,000, contains 18-38% protein, isolated from Coriolus versicolor cultured mycelium
- Oral bioavailability sufficient for systemic immune effects at 3 g/day dosing
- Intracellular signaling: activates PKC-delta/epsilon, ERK2/3 in NK cells; LAT, ERK1/2, NFAT, AP-1 in CD4+ T cells
- Antioxidant activity demonstrated; counteracts chemotherapy-induced reactive oxygen species
- Inhibits NF-kB activation (via blocking LPS-LBP binding), suggesting anti-inflammatory properties

### Dosing protocols (Q4)
- Standard clinical dose: 3 g/day orally
- Duration: typically 1 year as adjuvant post-surgery, sometimes longer (up to 3 years in some trials)
- Administered in combination with fluoropyrimidine chemotherapy (tegafur, UFT, S-1, 5-FU)
- Alternating regimens also used (e.g., 4 weeks PSK + 4 weeks 5-FU, repeated for 10 courses)
- Preoperative use also studied (1 week before surgery)
- Monotherapy: 1-3 g/day studied in some early trials

### Safety profile (Q6)
- Few adverse reactions reported; mostly GI symptoms
- No bone marrow suppression, liver, or renal toxicity reported
- PSK attenuates chemotherapy-induced adverse reactions and immunosuppression
- Specifically prevents T-cell apoptosis induced by S-1 chemotherapy (via antioxidant mechanism)
- No grade 3/4 adverse events in some trials comparing UFT+PSK vs UFT alone

### Clinical trial evidence -- oncology only
- **Gastric cancer meta-analysis** (Oba et al., 8 RCTs): HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.79-0.98, P=0.018) for 5-year survival
- **Colorectal cancer meta-analysis** (Sakamoto et al., 3 RCTs): OS risk ratio 0.71 (95% CI 0.55-0.90, P=0.006)
- **Small-cell lung cancer** (Konno et al.): median response duration 25 weeks (PSK) vs 13 weeks (control), P=0.042
- **15-year follow-up** (Maehara et al., 225 patients): 56.9% vs 45.7% survival (P=0.0351) for PLCC+PSK vs surgery alone

### Null/negative results (Q7)
- JFMC05 (651 pts): tegafur+PSK 52.8% vs tegafur alone 47.0% 5-year survival, NOT significant
- Ogawa et al. (111 pts): carmofur+PSK vs carmofur alone, no difference in 5-year survival
- JFMC11 (228 pts): pre+postop PSK vs surgery alone, no survival difference for T1/T2 gastric cancer
- Author acknowledges: "it is undeniable that the main mechanism of action is unclear"

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q1 (cognitive/neuroprotective):** Not addressed. No cognitive endpoints in any trial.
- **Q2 (longevity biomarkers):** Not addressed. Survival endpoints are cancer-specific, not general longevity.
- **Q3 (bioactive compounds/pathways):** HIGHLY relevant. Detailed mechanistic data on PSK composition, signaling pathways, immune modulation, antioxidant activity, and anti-inflammatory effects. The immunomodulatory and anti-inflammatory mechanisms could theoretically support extrapolation to neuro/aging contexts but this paper provides no such evidence.
- **Q4 (dosing/formulations):** Relevant. Standard dose 3 g/day oral, multiple dosing schedules documented across RCTs.
- **Q5 (direct vs extrapolation):** Critical clarity -- all claims in this paper are oncology-specific. Any cognition/longevity claims would be pure extrapolation.
- **Q6 (risks/adverse events):** Relevant. Good safety profile documented with GI symptoms as main side effect, no serious organ toxicity.
- **Q7 (null results):** Relevant. Several RCTs with null results documented; author acknowledges unclear primary mechanism.

## Limitations
- Review article, not primary data; however, it synthesizes a large body of primary research
- All clinical data is in cancer patients receiving chemotherapy -- not applicable to healthy populations
- No pharmacokinetic data (absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination) provided for PSK
- Published ~2011; does not cover more recent developments

## Claims to Verify
- MW ~100,000 and 18-38% protein content: stated without primary citation in this review, attributed to earlier characterization
- "Few adverse reactions" claim should be verified against original trial reports for systematic underreporting
- The JFMC01 trial enrolled 7,637 patients -- this is a very large number and should be cross-checked
