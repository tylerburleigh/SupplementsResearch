What is Organic Turkey Tail Mushroom Extract?

Organic turkey tail mushroom extract is derived from the fruiting bodies of Trametes versicolor, cultivated under certified organic conditions. The mushrooms are grown without synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or chemical fertilizers, ensuring a clean and traceable raw material suitable for high-quality formulations.

Undersun
supplies bulk organic turkey tail extract, produced using a dual extraction technology. Polysaccharides ≥45%, β-glucan ≥40%. Our special processing makes this extract easily soluble in water.
