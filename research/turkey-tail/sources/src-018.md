Turkey Tail
Trametes versicolor, Coriolus versicolor, Polyporus versicolor

Active Ingredients:

polysaccharopeptide (PSP), polysaccharide-K (PSK), linoleic acid, oleic acid, palmitic acid, stearic acid, linolenic acid, p-hydroxy benzoic, protocatechuic, vanillic, homogentisic acids, leucine, isoleucine, methionine, tyrosine, glutamine, asparagine

Taste:
Slightly bitter, earthy and mild in flavor when compared to reishi or shiitake.

Key Applications:
Turkey Tail is a very popular addition to quality ingredient mixes included in supplements, functional foods and beverages.

Dietary supplements for digestion and immunity

Addition to tea and coffee as a functional enhancement

Addition to ready to eat and grab-n-go foods (soups, shakes and prepared meals) as a functional ingredient

Top Benefits Include:

Relieves chronic fatigue symptoms

Promotes healthy digestion

Supports healthy liver function

Turkey Tail mushrooms’ name comes from their eye-catching stripes. Their anti-microbial properties help protect the digestive system. Other benefits include immunological enhancement, liver protection, and oxidation resistance.
