# src-038: Nicotinamide riboside supplementation alters body composition and skeletal muscle acetylcarnitine concentrations in healthy obese humans

**Citation:** Remie et al. (2020), American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 112(2):413-426. DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/nqaa072
**Citation count:** 149
**Quality:** ok
**Trial registration:** NCT02835664

## Core Findings

This randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study found that NR supplementation (1000 mg/d for 6 weeks) in 13 healthy overweight/obese adults increased skeletal muscle NAD+ metabolic markers (NAAD, MeNAM) without increasing steady-state NAD+ levels, produced minor improvements in body composition (increased fat-free mass, decreased fat mass), and altered skeletal muscle acetylcarnitine metabolism. However, NR had no effect on the primary outcomes of insulin sensitivity or mitochondrial function, nor on hepatic/intramyocellular lipid, cardiac function, blood pressure, or inflammatory markers.

## Study Design and Methodology

- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover
- **Participants:** 13 healthy overweight or obese men and women (mean age 59 +/- 5 y, BMI 30.2 +/- 2.6; 7 women, 6 men); 15 recruited, 2 dropped out
- **Intervention:** NR (Niagen) 1000 mg/d vs placebo, each for 6 weeks, with 4-7 week washout between arms
- **Primary outcomes:** Insulin sensitivity (2-step hyperinsulinemic-euglycemic clamp), skeletal muscle mitochondrial function (high-resolution respirometry in permeabilized fibers)
- **Secondary outcomes:** NAD+ metabolites, body composition (BodPod), sleeping metabolic rate (respiration chamber), muscle acetylcarnitine (MRS + biopsy), hepatic/intramyocellular lipid (MRS), cardiac function (31P-MRS, MRI), ambulatory BP (36-h), plasma biochemistry, inflammatory markers
- **Compliance:** 99.3% NR, 99.1% placebo (by pill count)
- **Sample size calculation:** Based on detecting 3.25 umol/kg/min difference in insulin-stimulated glucose disposal, 80% power, alpha=0.05, n=12 + 20% dropout buffer
- **Analysis:** Paired t-test or Wilcoxon; no correction for multiple comparisons (acknowledged by authors as a limitation)
- **Funding:** Dutch Heart Foundation, EU Horizon 2020; ChromaDex provided Niagen and placebo capsules but had no role in study design, analysis, or manuscript

## NAD+ Elevation Data

- **Enzymatic NAD+ assay (n=8):** NAD+ content not different between NR and placebo (1.019 vs 1.125 nmol/mg dry weight, P=0.34)
- **Metabolomics (n=12):** NAD+ content unchanged (P=0.91)
- **NAAD:** Increased 677 +/- 155% with NR vs placebo (P < 0.01) -- a highly sensitive biomarker of NR supplementation
- **MeNAM:** Increased 299 +/- 62% with NR vs placebo (P < 0.01) -- marker of increased NAD+ flux/degradation
- **Other metabolites unchanged:** NADH, NADP, NADPH, NAM, NMN (all P > 0.25)
- **Interpretation:** NR increases NAD+ turnover rate without elevating steady-state NAD+ concentrations in skeletal muscle

## Body Composition Changes

- **Fat-free mass (%):** NR 62.65% vs placebo 61.32%; change +1.34% +/- 0.50%, P=0.02
- **Fat mass (%):** NR 37.35% vs placebo 38.68%; change -1.34% +/- 0.50%, P=0.02
- **Body weight:** Unchanged (NR 85.2 kg vs placebo 85.5 kg, P=0.55)
- **Sex-specific signal:** 6/7 women showed increased FFM and reduced FM; only 1/6 men showed this pattern. This sex difference is exploratory and not statistically tested.
- **Sleeping metabolic rate:** Higher with NR (6.68 vs 6.49 MJ/d, P=0.05), but explained by increased FFM (SMR/FFM P=0.48)

## Muscle Metabolite Changes (Acetylcarnitine)

- **Biopsy acetylcarnitine (C2, morning fasted):** NR 4558 vs placebo 3025 pmol/mg dry weight; change +1533 +/- 683, P=0.04
- **MRS resting acetylcarnitine (evening, 3h postprandial):** Lower with NR (1.30 vs 1.80 mmol/kg, P=0.02) -- paradoxical finding possibly related to meal-induced lowering
- **Exercise-stimulated acetylcarnitine (MRS):** Maximal levels unchanged (P=0.67)
- **Capacity to form acetylcarnitine (exercise - rest):** Higher with NR (2.99 vs 2.40 mmol/kg, P=0.01)
- **Other acylcarnitines:** Free carnitine (C0), short-chain (C3-C5), medium-chain (C6-C12), long-chain (C13-C20) all unchanged (P > 0.25)

## Null Results (Insulin Sensitivity, Mitochondrial Function, Other)

- **Insulin sensitivity:** No effect on whole-body glucose disposal (delta Rd P=0.98), hepatic insulin sensitivity (EGP suppression P=0.30), metabolic flexibility (P=0.70), or NOGD
- **Mitochondrial respiration (ex vivo):** All states unchanged -- state 2, state 3 (lipid and CI substrates), combined CI+II, uncoupled (state u), and state 4o (proton leak) all P > 0.05
- **OXPHOS protein content:** Complexes I-V all unchanged (P > 0.24)
- **Ectopic lipid:** IHL unchanged (P=0.85), IMCL unchanged (P=0.50)
- **Cardiac function:** PCr:ATP ratio (P=0.90), EF (P=0.24), cardiac volumes all NS
- **Blood pressure:** 36-h ambulatory SBP, DBP, MAP, HR all NS (daytime and nighttime separately also NS)
- **Plasma biochemistry:** Glucose, triglycerides, total/HDL/LDL cholesterol all unchanged
- **Inflammatory markers (n=7 subset):** All NS; IL-1alpha trended lower (P=0.06)

## Safety and Adverse Events

- NR at 1000 mg/d for 6 weeks was well tolerated
- No adverse events or side effects reported
- ChromaDex provided capsules but had no role in study design/analysis

## Limitations

1. **Small sample size:** n=13 completors; even smaller subsets for some measures (NAD+ enzymatic n=8, inflammatory markers n=7, IMCL n=9, cardiac PCr:ATP n=11)
2. **No multiple-comparison correction:** Authors explicitly note this increases risk of false positives, which is relevant for the significant body composition and acetylcarnitine findings
3. **Short duration:** 6 weeks may be insufficient for metabolic remodeling (mouse studies used 8-15 weeks)
4. **Healthy population:** Participants were metabolically healthy despite being overweight/obese; effects may differ in insulin-resistant or older populations
5. **No steady-state NAD+ increase:** Only markers of NAD+ flux increased, not NAD+ itself, raising questions about biological significance
6. **Sex-specific body composition finding:** Exploratory observation not designed or powered to detect sex differences

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **NAD+ elevation in humans:** Confirms NR increases NAD+ metabolic markers (NAAD, MeNAM) in skeletal muscle but does not increase total NAD+ content. Important for understanding the gap between NR supplementation and metabolic outcomes.
- **Metabolic health effects:** Adds to accumulating evidence that NR does not improve insulin sensitivity or mitochondrial function in humans at 1000 mg/d over 6 weeks, despite promising preclinical data. The body composition and acetylcarnitine findings are novel but modest and uncorrected for multiple comparisons.
- **Safety:** Reinforces NR safety profile at 1000 mg/d for 6 weeks with zero adverse events.

## Claims to Verify

- **FFM change of 1.34%:** This is a small absolute difference. With n=13 and no multiple-comparison correction across the many outcomes tested, this could be a chance finding. The sex-specific subgroup analysis (6/7 women vs 1/6 men) was post hoc and not powered.
- **Sleeping metabolic rate P=0.05:** Borderline significance that disappears when normalized to FFM (P=0.48), suggesting the effect is driven by the small FFM change rather than a direct NR effect on metabolic rate.
- **Acetylcarnitine biopsy finding (P=0.04):** The paradoxical direction difference between morning biopsy (higher with NR) and evening MRS (lower with NR) is unexplained and could reflect diurnal variation rather than a treatment effect.
