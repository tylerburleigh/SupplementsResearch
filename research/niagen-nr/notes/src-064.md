# src-064: Repeat Dose NRPT Increases NAD+ Levels in Humans Safely and Sustainably

**Citation:** Dellinger RW, Santos SR, Morris M, Evans M, Alminana D, Guarente L, Marcotulli E. (2017). *npj Aging and Mechanisms of Disease*, 3:17. doi:10.1038/s41514-017-0016-9

**Venue:** npj Aging and Mechanisms of Disease (Nature Partner Journals) | **Citations:** 164 | **Quality:** ok

## Core Findings

This is the first randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial of NRPT (nicotinamide riboside + pterostilbene, commercially known as Basis by Elysium Health) in 120 healthy older adults (age 60-80). NRPT produced a dose-dependent increase in whole-blood NAD+ that was sustained over 8 weeks with a favorable safety profile.

## Study Design

- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group trial (NCT02678611)
- **Population:** 120 healthy adults, age 60-80, BMI 18-35; 87% Western European white, 68% female
- **Arms (n=40 each):**
  - Placebo: 4 capsules/day of microcrystalline cellulose
  - NRPT 1X: 250 mg NR + 50 mg pterostilbene daily (2 NRPT capsules + 2 placebo capsules)
  - NRPT 2X: 500 mg NR + 100 mg pterostilbene daily (4 NRPT capsules)
- **Duration:** 8 weeks of daily supplementation + 30-day follow-up
- **Blood draws:** Baseline (day 0), day 30, day 60 (12-hour fasted)
- **Analysis populations:** ITT (n=118), Per Protocol (n=113); NAD+ analyzed in PP population
- **Sites:** London ON, Orlando FL, Irvine CA; conducted by KGK Synergize (CRO)
- **Capsule composition:** Each NRPT capsule contained 125 mg NR (as Niagen) + 25 mg pterostilbene

## NAD+ Elevation Data (Dose-Response)

| Group | Day 30 increase | Day 60 increase |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| Placebo | No change | No change |
| NRPT 1X (250/50 mg) | ~40% over baseline | ~40% (sustained) |
| NRPT 2X (500/100 mg) | ~90% over baseline | ~55% (partial decline) |

- NAD+ measured by LC-MS/MS from whole-blood lysates collected in perchloric acid
- Within-group increases significant at both time points for both NRPT groups
- Between-group differences significant at both time points
- NRPT 2X still significantly higher than NRPT 1X at day 60
- Authors speculate the partial decline at day 60 in the 2X group may reflect homeostatic mechanisms (e.g., CD38 induction)

## Safety Outcomes

- **Total AEs:** 66 events in 45 participants across all groups
  - Placebo: 18 AEs / 13 participants
  - NRPT 1X: 25 AEs / 15 participants
  - NRPT 2X: 23 AEs / 17 participants
- **Between-group p-value for related AEs:** 0.088 (not significant)
- **No serious adverse events** reported
- Treatment-related AEs in NRPT 2X: moderate fatigue, mild headache, moderate dyspepsia, moderate abdominal discomfort, diarrhea (1 probably related, 4 possibly related)
- GI disorders were the most common treatment-related AEs (3 in NRPT 2X vs 1 in NRPT 1X vs 0 in placebo)
- Compliance exceeded 94% in all groups

## Exploratory Findings

### Liver enzymes
- ALT significantly decreased in NRPT 1X group at day 30 and day 60 vs. baseline (within-group)
- AST showed same trend but did not reach significance
- Suggests possible improvement in liver function at the standard dose

### Blood pressure
- Diastolic blood pressure significantly reduced at day 60 in NRPT 1X group
- Consistent with prior pterostilbene data (Riche et al. 2014)

### Mobility
- NRPT 2X group showed significant within-group increases in both 30-second chair stand test and 6-minute walk test at day 60
- No changes in placebo or NRPT 1X groups
- Authors caution this was exploratory and underpowered

### Lipids
- Small increases in total cholesterol and LDL in NRPT groups
- LDL increase in NRPT 1X was ~3% at day 30, ~3.5% at day 60 vs placebo
- When stratified by BMI: normal BMI subjects in NRPT 1X showed no significant LDL increases; increases confined to overweight subgroup
- Baseline LDL/total cholesterol differed significantly between groups (confound)
- Authors note that individual LDL biological variation is ~9%, so these changes may be natural variation
- Possible mechanisms discussed: SIRT1-mediated reverse cholesterol transport, or healthier liver producing more cholesterol

## Pterostilbene Rationale

Pterostilbene was selected over resveratrol as the SIRT1-activating component due to:
- Greater oral bioavailability (two methoxy groups increase lipophilicity)
- Longer half-life (reduced oxidation)
- The combination is theorized to synergistically support metabolic health: NR supplies NAD+ to all seven sirtuins, PT provides additional SIRT1 activation

## Key Strengths

- Rigorous RCT design with placebo control and double-blinding
- Independent CRO conducted the trial
- Dose-response relationship demonstrated
- 8-week duration showing sustained effect

## Limitations

- Only older adults (60-80) studied; generalizability to younger populations unclear
- Secondary endpoints (liver, mobility, lipids) underpowered
- Significant baseline imbalances in lipid parameters between groups
- Industry-funded (Elysium Health); all authors are employees and shareholders
- NAD+ measured in whole blood, not tissue-specific
- NR and PT cannot be individually disentangled (no NR-only arm)

## Relevance to Research Questions

This study directly demonstrates that daily oral NR (at 250 mg or 500 mg) combined with pterostilbene produces significant, sustained, dose-dependent increases in whole-blood NAD+ in healthy older adults over 8 weeks, with an acceptable safety profile. It is one of the earliest and most cited RCTs of NR supplementation in humans.

## Claims to Verify

- The NRPT 2X group ITT population is reported as n=38 (not 40) in line 159, suggesting 2 participants were excluded from ITT; this discrepancy from the stated n=40 randomization should be confirmed.
