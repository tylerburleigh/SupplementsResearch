# src-035: NR Combined with Exercise for Hypertension in Older Adults

**Citation:** Lin Y, Zeidan RS, et al. "Nicotinamide riboside combined with exercise to treat hypertension in middle-aged and older adults: a pilot randomized clinical trial." GeroScience (2025) 47:6895-6908. DOI: 10.1007/s11357-025-01815-2

**Quality:** ok (full paper text extracted, 54K chars, 515 sentences)

---

## Core Findings

NR (1000 mg/day) combined with supervised walking exercise for 6 weeks did **not** reduce daytime systolic blood pressure (SBP) more than exercise alone in hypertensive middle-aged and older adults. In fact, the NR+Exercise group trended toward *increased* daytime SBP (+5.19 mmHg) while the Placebo+Exercise group trended toward decreased SBP (-2.71 mmHg). However, a hypothesis-generating post hoc analysis in participants not on antihypertensive medications (n=18) suggested NR+Exercise may reduce nighttime SBP (-9.6 mmHg), which the authors consider worthy of further investigation.

---

## Study Design

- **Design:** Double-blinded, Phase IIa pilot RCT (NCT04112043)
- **Three arms:**
  1. NR (1000 mg/day) + supervised walking exercise 3x/week, 30 min (NR+Ex, n=17 randomized, n=15 completed)
  2. Placebo + same exercise regimen (PL+Ex, n=18 randomized, n=16 completed)
  3. NR alone (1000 mg/day), no exercise (NR, n=19 randomized, n=18 completed)
- **Duration:** 6 weeks
- **Population:** 54 sedentary adults >=55 years with daytime SBP 130-160 mmHg
- **Mean age:** 67 years; 61% female; ~28% Black
- **Primary comparison:** NR+Ex vs PL+Ex
- **Funding:** NIH/NIA grant R21AG064282; NR product provided by Niagen Bioscience (formerly ChromaDex)
- **Competing interests:** Authors declare no competing interests

## Primary Outcome: Daytime SBP

| Group | Mean Change (SD) | Cohen's d |
|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| NR+Ex | +5.19 +/- 13.2 mmHg | 0.391 (trend toward increase) |
| PL+Ex | -2.71 +/- 10.5 mmHg | -0.259 (trend toward decrease) |
| NR alone | -0.48 +/- 8.49 mmHg | -0.057 (minimal change) |

**No statistically significant differences** between groups. The primary hypothesis was not supported -- NR+Ex was not superior to PL+Ex for daytime SBP reduction.

Notable: The NR+Ex group started with a lower average SBP at baseline, which may have limited the observable intervention effect.

## Secondary Outcomes

### Arterial Stiffness (PWV)
| Group | Mean Change (SD) | Cohen's d |
|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| NR+Ex | -0.31 +/- 0.77 m/s | -0.403 |
| PL+Ex | -0.11 +/- 0.78 m/s | -0.143 |
| NR alone | +0.03 +/- 0.9 m/s | 0.029 |

NR+Ex showed the largest trend toward PWV reduction but differences were not statistically significant.

### NAD Urine Catabolites
NR supplementation (both arms) confirmed bioactivity through increased Me-NAM, PY, and Me-PY levels:
- Me-NAM: NR+Ex increased 0.43 uM/uM (d=1.31); NR alone increased 0.65 uM/uM (d=0.747); PL+Ex decreased 0.02 uM/uM
- Me-PY: NR+Ex increased 9.18 uM/uM (d=1.14); NR alone increased 6.93 uM/uM (d=1.36)

## Post Hoc Analysis: Non-Antihypertensive Medication Subgroup (n=18)

PCA identified antihypertensive medication use as the primary confounding factor (PC1 loading=0.51). Among 18 participants NOT on antihypertensive medications:

- **Nighttime SBP:** NR+Ex showed -9.6 +/- 9.22 mmHg (d=-1.04) vs PL+Ex -1.17 +/- 28.2 mmHg (d=-0.041)
- **Nighttime DBP:** NR+Ex showed -4.51 +/- 7.12 mmHg (d=-0.633) vs PL+Ex -1.06 +/- 9.7 mmHg
- **Daytime SBP:** PL+Ex actually showed greater reduction (-11.6 +/- 5.08 mmHg, d=-2.29) vs NR+Ex (-3.16 +/- 11.7 mmHg)

**Caution:** Very small subgroups (NR+Ex n=6, PL+Ex n=4, NR n=8). Large standard deviations. Post hoc and hypothesis-generating only.

## Key Findings: NR Alone vs NR+Exercise vs Exercise Alone

1. **Exercise alone (PL+Ex)** showed the most consistent trend toward daytime SBP reduction in the full cohort.
2. **NR alone** showed minimal BP effect (SBP change -0.48 mmHg).
3. **NR+Exercise** paradoxically trended toward increased daytime SBP in the full cohort but showed the largest nighttime SBP reduction in unmedicated participants.
4. No additive or synergistic effect of combining NR with exercise was demonstrated for the primary outcome.
5. NR confirmed metabolic bioactivity through elevated urinary NAD catabolites in both NR arms.

## Safety Data

- 91% retention rate; 90% supplement adherence; 93% exercise adherence
- No abnormal blood laboratory results in any group
- 1 serious adverse event (NR-only group, determined unrelated to study)
- Mild adverse events: dizziness, shortness of breath, fatigue, low BP after exercise -- comparable across groups
- No withdrawals due to intervention-related adverse effects
- NR well-tolerated at 1000 mg/day for 6 weeks

## Effect Sizes and Clinical Significance

- The primary outcome (daytime SBP) showed **no clinically meaningful benefit** for NR+Ex over PL+Ex. The direction actually favored PL+Ex.
- PWV reduction with NR+Ex (d=-0.403) was small and non-significant.
- Post hoc nighttime SBP effect in unmedicated subgroup (d=-1.04) was a large effect size but based on n=6 in NR+Ex arm with high variability (SD=9.22 mmHg).
- The authors conclude these trends "warrant further investigation in a Phase IIb RCT."

## Limitations

- **Small sample size** (49 completed across 3 arms) -- pilot study not powered for statistical significance
- **Short duration** (6 weeks) -- authors note longer intervention may be needed
- **Post hoc analyses** are hypothesis-generating only; subgroup sizes extremely small
- **Baseline imbalance:** NR+Ex group had lower baseline SBP, potentially limiting observable effect
- **Antihypertensive medication** used by most participants (36/54), confounding BP responses
- Missing data imputed using the mean for primary/secondary outcomes
- NR alone arm was not the primary comparator and was included for exploratory purposes

## Context for Research Question

This study provides direct evidence that NR (Niagen brand, 1000 mg/day, 6 weeks) does NOT enhance the BP-lowering effect of aerobic exercise in hypertensive older adults taking antihypertensive medications. The negative primary finding is important for assessing NR's cardiovascular efficacy claims. The post hoc nighttime BP signal in unmedicated participants is provocative but very preliminary.

## Claims to Verify

- Sample size of 54 randomized is confirmed in the text (line 464). Completed n=49 also confirmed.
- The mean daytime SBP change values (NR+Ex: +5.19, PL+Ex: -2.71) are consistently reported in abstract and results.
- Subgroup sizes for the post hoc analysis (n=18 total; NR+Ex n=6, PL+Ex n=4, NR n=8) are reported but very small -- effect sizes from n=4 should be treated with extreme caution.
- The PL+Ex daytime SBP Cohen's d of -2.29 in the unmedicated subgroup (n=4) is implausibly large and driven by the very small SD of 5.08 mmHg in 4 participants.
