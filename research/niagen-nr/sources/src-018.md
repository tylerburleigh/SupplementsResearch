What is Nicotinamide Riboside Chloride?

Nicotinamide Riboside Chloride
is a form of vitamin B3 and a direct precursor to NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide), a vital coenzyme in cellular metabolism. NAD+ levels naturally decline with age, leading to reduced energy production and cellular repair efficiency. Supplementing with NR can help support cellular energy, brain function, and overall metabolic health.

How Does Nicotinamide Riboside Boost NAD+ Levels?

NR enters the body and is converted into
NAD+
, which is crucial for cellular processes like DNA repair, mitochondrial function, and energy metabolism. Unlike other NAD+ precursors, NR offers a direct and efficient pathway to increase NAD+ levels without significant side effects.

NRK Pathway Conversion

Once absorbed, NR is phosphorylated by NRK1/NRK2 into NMN, which then converts quickly into NAD+: NR → NMN → NAD+.

Kinetic studies show that NRK1 has a high affinity for NR (Km ~0.2–0.5 μM). NAMPT (for nicotinamide) has lower affinity (Km ~20–30 μM).

This means NR enters the NAD+ cycle 40–100 times more efficiently than nicotinamide in certain tissues.

What Are the Key Specifications and Purity Standards for NR?

As a NAD+ precursor, NR requires high-level quality control to prevent degradation and ensure consistent performance in finished formulations. Key specifications include:

●
Purity (≥98%):
Verified by HPLC or UPLC

●
Moisture Content:
NR is sensitive to humidity. Low moisture is critical to maintain stability during storage and to prevent clumping, discoloration, or premature degradation.

●
Heavy Metal Limits:
NR must meet strict heavy-metal limits:

Lead (Pb): ≤0.5–1.0 ppm

Arsenic (As): ≤1.0 ppm

Cadmium (Cd): ≤0.5 ppm

Mercury (Hg): ≤0.1 ppm

●
Microbiological Standards:
Microbial contamination can impact both safety and shelf life.

Total Plate Count: <1000 CFU/g

Yeast & Mold: <100 CFU/g

E. coli, Salmonella, Staphylococcus aureus: Negative

●
Residual Solvent Compliance:
Common limits include:

Class 2 solvents: ≤320 ppm total

Class 3 solvents: ≤5000 ppm

●
Appearance & Physical Characteristics:
A consistent physical profile indicates good production control:

Appearance: White or off-white crystalline powder

Odor: Neutral, no foreign smell

Particle size: Suitable for capsule, tablet, or beverage applications

Bulk density: Important for formulation flowability

●
Chromatographic Profile:
HPLC or UPLC. A clean profile with minimal secondary peaks indicates good control of by-products and intermediates.

● Stability Requirements

NR should demonstrate stability under both ambient and accelerated conditions (40°C, 75% RH).

≥95% active content at 24 months (under recommended storage conditions)
