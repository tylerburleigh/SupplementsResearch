Nicotinamide Riboside
Nicotinamide Riboside (NR) is an advanced form of vitamin B3 and a powerful precursor to NAD+, a key molecule responsible for energy production, cellular repair, and longevity. By supporting and sustaining healthy NAD+ levels, NR promotes optimal mitochondrial function and helps protect against age-related cellular decline. Research suggests that NR supplementation can enhance energy metabolism, support brain and muscle performance, and contribute to overall cellular resilience.

• Enhanced ATP (cellular energy) production
• Optimal metabolism and enzyme function
• Support for lipid and hormone synthesis
• DNA repair and cellular renewal
• Maintenance of muscle tone and neurological function
