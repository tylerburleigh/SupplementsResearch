Why is NAD+ so important?

NAD+ is a crucial coenzyme that powers your cells, converting nutrients into energy while activating key enzymes for DNA repair and cellular resilience. After decades of research, scientists now recognize it as fundamental to maintaining healthy cellular function throughout your body.
Research shows that NAD+ levels naturally decline with age and metabolic stress, a shift closely linked to aging itself. Because NAD+ precursors are found only in trace amounts in nature, the body cannot rely on direct dietary sources to replenish it. Instead, it requires a precursor. Niagen®, a highly bioavailable and nature-identical form of nicotinamide riboside (NR), is clinically proven to efficiently raise NAD+ levels, helping to support cellular function.
