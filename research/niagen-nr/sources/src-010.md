Product Description:

Nicotinamide Riboside is an orally available form of vitamin B3 and a precursor to nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+), an essential coenzyme involved in cellular energy production and metabolic processes. It plays an important role in maintaining mitochondrial function and supporting cellular repair mechanisms. After oral administration, nicotinamide riboside is converted in the body into nicotinamide mononucleotide through the action of specific enzymes known as nicotinamide riboside kinases (NRK1 and NRK2). This compound is then further converted into NAD+, a molecule that is vital for redox reactions and energy metabolism within cells.

Nicotinamide Riboside has gained attention for its potential therapeutic role in managing chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy (CIPN). By increasing and maintaining NAD+ levels in the body, it may help protect nerve cells from damage caused by neurotoxic chemotherapy agents. Elevated NAD+ levels are associated with improved mitochondrial function and enhanced cellular resilience, which may help reduce axonal injury and peripheral nerve degeneration.

Due to its ability to support cellular energy metabolism and nerve health, Nicotinamide Riboside is also widely studied for applications in healthy aging, metabolic health, and mitochondrial disorders, making it a promising nutraceutical ingredient in advanced health supplements.

Nicotinamide Riboside is used as a dietary supplement to support cellular energy production, healthy aging, and metabolic health. It works by increasing the levels of NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide), an essential coenzyme involved in energy metabolism and cell repair. It is often used to support mitochondrial function, brain health, and overall vitality.

Nicotinamide Riboside is a naturally occurring form of vitamin B3. It is typically produced through chemical synthesis or biotechnological fermentation processes to ensure purity and stability for supplement formulations. Once ingested, it is converted in the body into NAD+.
