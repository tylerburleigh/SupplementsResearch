Description

Nicotinamide Riboside is a supplement which research suggests supports enhanced NAD levels which may lead to enhanced mitochondrial biogenesis. Nicotinamide riboside chloride is a highly efficient oral precursor to boost cellular NAD+ (an essential and limiting co-factor for energy metabolism) levels and has been shown to significantly increase cellular NAD+ levels in a preliminary human clinical trial. Levels of NAD+ within cells decline precipitously with aging as well as with high caloric intake, which are both associated with compromised metabolic function and many degenerations. Increasing cellular NAD+ such as by caloric restriction, fasting, and exercise, promotes healthy mitochondrial function and energy expenditure.*
