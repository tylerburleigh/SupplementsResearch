## Abstract Summary
This review examines emerging features of autophagy and their links to aging and disease progression. It discusses preclinical evidence for autophagy modulators as suppressors of age-related pathologies (especially neurodegenerative diseases) and proposes novel research avenues linking autophagy to hallmarks of aging. The review covers autophagy mechanisms broadly (macroautophagy, mitophagy, lipophagy, chaperone-mediated autophagy) and their roles in healthspan and longevity.
