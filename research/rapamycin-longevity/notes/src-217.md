## Content Quality Note

This file is **supplemental data only**, not the main paper. It contains supplemental experimental procedures, fly strain descriptions, weight/lipid/starvation measurement methods, X-gal staining protocols, fly food composition, supplemental references, figure legends, and Table S1 with physiological measurements. The main findings (lifespan curves, statistical analysis of longevity effects) are in the primary paper, which is not included. Consequently, this source has limited standalone evidentiary value.

## Core Findings

1. The TOR signaling pathway (TSC1/TSC2, dTOR, S6K) regulates lifespan in Drosophila, with the TSC-TOR pathway interacting with the insulin/IGF-PI3K-AKT pathway to balance growth and longevity.
2. No statistically significant differences in weight, lipid-to-protein ratio, or starvation resistance were found among TOR pathway-modified Drosophila strains compared to controls (Table S1).
3. High yeast extract diet increases fecundity and body weight while decreasing stress resistance, consistent with nutrient-TOR pathway tradeoffs.

## Key Evidence / Data Points

- **Table S1:** Weight, lipid/protein ratio, and starvation resistance measured in 4 replicates of 10 male flies each for genotypes: da-GAL4/UAS-dTsc2, da-GAL4/UAS-dTORFRB, da-GAL4/UAS-dS6kKQ, plus controls. No significant differences found.
- **Tissue-specific drivers:** gmr-GAL4 (eye), appl-GAL4 (nervous system), 24B-GAL4 and PO188-GAL4 (muscle + fat), DJ634-GAL4 and PO163-GAL4 (fat only) -- used for targeted TOR pathway manipulation.
- **Fly food composition:** Standard lab food with 82% water, 0.45% agar, 5% dextrose, 2.5% sucrose, 8.3% cornmeal, 1.5% dried yeast.
- **Lifespan measurement protocol:** Flies transferred every 3-4 days, deaths recorded, p values calculated with GraphPad Prism.

## Methodology

- **Organism:** Drosophila melanogaster (various GAL4-UAS transgenic lines)
- **Genes studied:** dTsc1, dTsc2 (tumor suppressors), dTOR (FRB and TED domains), dS6k (kinase dead KQ and STDETE mutants)
- **Design:** GAL4-UAS system for tissue-specific and ubiquitous overexpression/inhibition of TOR pathway components
- **Measures:** Weight per fly (mg), lipid-to-protein ratio (relative), starvation resistance (hours), lifespan (survival curves)
- **Statistics:** GraphPad Prism for p-value calculation

## Limitations

- **Supplemental material only:** The main lifespan data (survival curves, median/maximum lifespan extension statistics) are in the primary paper, not this file.
- **No effect on physiology:** The supplemental Table S1 shows no significant differences in weight, lipid ratio, or starvation resistance, suggesting TOR pathway modulation affects lifespan through mechanisms not captured by these gross physiological measures.
- **Drosophila-specific:** Findings in fruit flies may not translate directly to mammals.
