## Core Findings (from abstract)

This book chapter reviews pharmacological agents (resveratrol, rapamycin, metformin, aspirin) reported to extend lifespan in model organisms from worms to rodents. These drugs target master regulators of aging — mTOR, FOXO, and PGC1alpha — affecting autophagy, inflammation, and oxidative stress. In humans, these drugs were reported to reduce inflammation, prevent CVD, and slow functional decline in certain organs, and inhibit carcinogenesis.
