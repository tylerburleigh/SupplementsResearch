NMN Powder β-Nicotinamide Mononucleotide Powder

NMN is the abbreviation of Nicotinamide Mononucleotide, and it is also called NAMN, β-NMN, β-Nicotinamide Mononucleotide or Beta Nicotinamide Mononucleotide.

NMN is white powder and it is a kind of nucleotide. In human body, the NMN is used by enzymes to generate nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD). NAD plays an important role in promoting cells metabolism. NMN powder has potential neuroprotective and anti-aging properties.Nicotinamide mononucleotide powder is mainly used in supplements.
