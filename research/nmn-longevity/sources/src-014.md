What is Nicotinamide Mononucleotide (NMN)?

NMN is a molecule that plays a crucial role in the energy production of our cells. It’s a key player in creating NAD+, a vital resource that keeps our bodily functions running smoothly. NAD+ levels naturally decline as we age, leading to various age-related health issues. This is where NMN steps in. By boosting NAD+ levels, NMN promises to reinvigorate the cellular processes that keep us healthy, helping delay some aspects of the aging process. Here are the roles NMN play in the body:

What is the role of NMN in cellular metabolism and energy production?

Our bodies constantly convert food into energy, which is essential for everything we do, from moving and thinking to growing. At the heart of this energy production are the mitochondria, known as the powerhouses of the cells. NMN has a special role here. It helps build NAD+, which, in turn, supports the mitochondria in producing energy. Maintaining a good supply of NMN helps keep our body’s powerhouses efficient and resilient.

What is the role of NMN in DNA repair and the maintenance of genomic stability?

Our DNA is the blueprint of our existence. However, it’s not invincible and can get damaged. When our body senses harmed DNA, it’s like an alarm bell ringing, signaling repair crews to fix it. NMN helps fuel these repair processes. How?

NMN is involved in a specific process that increases the production of NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide).

NAD+ is essential for the proper functioning of cellular repair mechanisms.

The increased presence of NAD+ through NMN supplementation supports the stability and resilience of our DNA.

This DNA stability is a critical factor in the overall process of healthy aging, as it helps maintain the integrity of genetic information and cellular function.

NAD+ synthesis and the significance of this coenzyme in body functions

NAD+ performs many vital functions in the body, from converting food into energy to keeping our internal organs working perfectly. However, we tend to produce less NAD+ as we age. NMN steps in like a valuable assistant, providing the raw materials needed to produce more NAD+. This process is essential because having enough NAD+ ensures our cells have the energy and resources necessary to operate smoothly.

NMN does not stop the natural aging process. It may impact certain aspects of aging, such as cellular health, but it does not halt or reverse the aging process.
