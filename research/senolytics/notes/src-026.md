## Significance and context

This is widely recognized as one of the first pilot clinical trials of senolytic drugs (Dasatinib + Quercetin) in humans, targeting individuals with diabetic kidney disease. The original paper reported decreases in adipose tissue senescent cell burden and circulating SASP factors following D+Q administration. The existence of the corrigendum is itself important: it indicates that re-analysis of raw data changed some conclusions. Any systematic review or meta-analysis MUST account for this corrigendum when citing the original findings.
