## Core Findings

This is a consumer-facing review article evaluating 8 commercial senolytic supplement products across ingredients, dosing, pricing, bioavailability, and formulation quality. It is not a scientific study but aggregates research references and provides comparative analysis of available products. The author recommends multi-ingredient complexes over single compounds because senescent cells are difficult to kill selectively -- "more difficult than killing cancer cells."

## Key Evidence on Dosing Protocols

- Most research favors **intermittent/pulse dosing** (weekly or monthly) over daily dosing
- Direct quote from study: "our data suggest that [senolytic cell]-reducing interventions need not entail continuous treatment; rather, intermittent treatments may provide substantial benefits"
- Products recommending daily dosing (BioTE, Supersmart) are criticized by the author as contradicting research consensus
- Author personally spaces senolytics away from workouts and takes during weekly 24-hour fast

## Key Evidence on Efficacy

- **Fisetin in old mice:** ~10% lifespan increase (analogous to 75-year-old human living extra 7.5 years)
- **Senolytics broadly:** "alleviate disease in numerous organs, improve physical function and resilience, and suppress all causes of mortality, even if administered to the aged" (2021 research)
- **Healthgevity Rejuvenate 2.0:** 63% decrease in SA-beta-Gal senescence marker in muscle tissue
- **MitoPrime (L-ergothioneine):** Swedish study of 3,200 adults showed strongest correlation with reduced all-cause mortality; lengthened telomeres after 8 weeks
- Most existing research performed on animals; human clinical trials still emerging

## Safety Considerations

- Human safety data for high-dose senolytic stacks is lacking
- Standard guidelines for dosages, timing, and administration routes not established
- Author recommends sticking to natural compounds (fisetin, quercetin) at reasonable intermittent doses
- Dasatinib (chemotherapy drug) causes far more side effects than natural bioflavonoids
- Avoid senolytics on workout days -- ingredients like fisetin and quercetin inhibit mTOR pathway, reducing exercise benefits
