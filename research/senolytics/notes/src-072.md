# src-072: Xu et al. (2018) "Senolytics Improve Physical Function and Increase Lifespan in Old Age"

**Citation:** Xu M, Pirtskhalava T, Farr JN, et al. Nature Medicine 24(8):1246-1256. DOI: 10.1038/s41591-018-0092-9
**PMID:** 29988130 | **PMCID:** PMC6082705 | **Venue:** Nature Medicine (2018)

---

## Core Findings

This landmark study provides proof-of-concept that senescent cells are sufficient to cause physical dysfunction and shortened lifespan, and that the senolytic cocktail dasatinib plus quercetin (D+Q) can selectively eliminate senescent cells, alleviate physical dysfunction, and extend both healthspan and remaining lifespan -- even when initiated in very old age (24-27 months in mice, equivalent to human age ~75-90 years).

## Key Results

### 1. Senescent Cell Transplantation Causes Physical Dysfunction (Causality Proof)
- Transplanting as few as 0.5x10^6 senescent (SEN) preadipocytes (just 0.007% of total body cells) into 17-month-old mice caused significant reductions in walking speed, hanging endurance, and grip strength within 1 month
- In young (6-month-old) mice, 10^6 SEN cells caused dysfunction; 0.5x10^6 impaired grip strength and walking speed; 0.2x10^6 had no detectable effect (dose-dependent)
- SEN cell transplantation into 17-month-old mice reduced survival over the following year: **5.2-fold higher mortality hazard** (P=0.006; n=23 SEN, n=24 CON)
- Results replicated with autologous ear fibroblasts and with human SEN cells in SCID-beige immunodeficient mice, ruling out immune rejection as primary mechanism
- High-fat diet exacerbated SEN cell-induced dysfunction

### 2. Senescence Spreading Mechanism
- Transplanted SEN cells survived only ~40 days in vivo, but physical dysfunction persisted for up to 6 months
- At 2 months post-transplant, host (LUC-negative) tissues showed elevated SA-beta-gal+, p16Ink4a, and TAF+ cells -- indicating senescence spread to host cells
- Spread occurred both locally (visceral adipose) and to distant tissues (quadriceps muscle, where no transplanted cells were detected)
- SASP factors (TNF-alpha, IL-6) were elevated in distant muscle tissue

### 3. D+Q Eliminates Human Senescent Cells
- Human omental adipose tissue explants from obese subjects (BMI 45.5+/-9.1; age 45.7+/-8.3 years; n=8)
- 48h treatment with D+Q (1 uM D + 20 uM Q) significantly reduced:
  - TAF+ cells, p16INK4A-high cells, SA-beta-gal+ cells
  - Increased cleaved caspase-3+ cells (apoptosis)
- Reduced secretion of SASP factors: IL-6, IL-8, MCP-1, PAI-1, GM-CSF
- Did NOT reduce adiponectin or adipsin secretion (ruling out non-specific toxicity)
- D+Q had little direct effect on macrophages (p16INK4A+;F4/80+ cells unaffected)
- D+Q increased PPAR-gamma and CEBP-alpha expression (adipogenic markers)

### 4. D+Q Improves Physical Function in Aged Mice
- 20-month-old wild-type C57BL/6 mice treated with D+Q bi-weekly for 4 months
- Significant improvements in: maximal walking speed, hanging endurance, grip strength, treadmill endurance, daily activity (n=20 D+Q vs. n=13 vehicle)
- SASP component expression reduced in visceral adipose tissue of D+Q-treated mice
- Testosterone levels were NOT altered by D+Q (ruling out hormonal mechanism)
- INK-ATTAC transgenic mice with genetic clearance of p16Ink4a+ cells showed similar functional improvements, confirming senescence-dependent mechanism

### 5. D+Q Extends Lifespan Starting at Very Old Age
- **Critical lifespan result:** Bi-weekly D+Q starting at 24-27 months of age
  - 36% increase in median post-treatment lifespan
  - Mortality hazard reduced to 64.9% (P=0.01)
  - n=71 D+Q (40 males, 31 females); n=76 vehicle (41 males, 35 females)
- **No extended morbidity:** Despite living longer, D+Q-treated mice showed no worse physical function in their last 2 months of life compared to controls
- Disease burden, tumor burden, and causes of death at autopsy were NOT significantly different between groups
- AP20187 (INK-ATTAC genetic clearance) starting at 24-27 months did NOT enhance late-life survival (contrast with Baker et al. 2016 finding at 12 months)

## D+Q Dosing Regimen Details

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Dasatinib dose | 5 mg/kg body weight |
| Quercetin dose | 50 mg/kg body weight |
| Route | Oral gavage |
| Vehicle | 10% PEG400 (100-150 uL) |
| Schedule (physical function) | Bi-weekly for 4 months |
| Schedule (lifespan study) | 3 consecutive days every 2 weeks |
| Drug half-lives | Both <12 hours |
| Start age (lifespan) | 24-27 months |
| Start age (physical function) | 20 months |

## Hit-and-Run Protocol Evidence

- A single 3-day course of D+Q at time of SEN cell transplantation prevented dysfunction 1 month later
- A single 5-day course given 5 weeks post-transplantation alleviated existing dysfunction
- Benefits lasted for several months despite drugs being cleared within hours
- This "hit-and-run" property distinguishes senolytics from drugs requiring continuous occupancy

## Methodology

- **Mouse strains:** C57BL/6 (NIA), LUC-transgenic (JAX 025854), SCID-beige, INK-ATTAC
- **Senescence induction:** 10 Gy radiation or 0.2 mM doxorubicin (24h) in preadipocytes; >85% senescence confirmed
- **Physical function battery:** RotaRod (walking speed), hanging endurance, grip strength, treadmill endurance, daily activity (CLAMS), food intake, body weight
- **Senescence markers:** SA-beta-gal, TAF (telomere-associated foci), p16Ink4a mRNA (qPCR), p16INK4A immunostaining
- **Survival analysis:** Cox proportional hazard model with sex and age as fixed effects; cohorts and cage assignment as random effects
- **Pathology:** Blinded postmortem examination of tumor burden, disease burden, inflammation
- **Human tissue:** Omental fat from bariatric surgery patients; D+Q or vehicle for 48h ex vivo
- **Statistics:** Two-tailed t-tests, ANOVA with Tukey's post-hoc, Pearson correlation; P<0.05

## Limitations

1. Human tissue explants were from obese subjects (BMI ~45), not aged lean individuals; generalizability to normal aging is unclear
2. Mouse lifespan study used oral gavage, which is stressful for very old mice -- potential confound
3. No formal statistical power analysis was used for sample size determination
4. Mice housed 3-5 per cage; some moved mid-study to reduce single-housing stress (minor cage confound)
5. D+Q's senolytic specificity was inferred from in vitro work and indirect evidence (no single-cell resolution in vivo killing confirmation)
6. INK-ATTAC treatment starting at 24-27 months did NOT replicate lifespan extension seen at 12 months (Baker et al. 2016), suggesting timing of intervention matters
7. Mechanism of senescence "spreading" was demonstrated but specific SASP mediators responsible were not identified
8. Patents held by Mayo Clinic; multiple authors have financial conflicts of interest

## Translational Implications

- Intermittent dosing reduces potential for side effects (notably, dasatinib can cause pulmonary edema with continuous daily dosing)
- Starting treatment at age equivalent to human 75-90 years still extended lifespan -- highly relevant for clinical translation
- Ongoing clinical trial referenced: NCT02848131
- First demonstration that senolytics work in human tissue ex vivo
