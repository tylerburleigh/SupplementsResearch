The senescence associated secretory phenotype, or SASP, is a critical concept in understanding cellular senescence. Essentially, it refers to a collection of proteins and molecules secreted by cells that have stopped dividing due to aging—a state known as senescence. These secreted elements include pro-inflammatory cytokines and various proteins crucial for cellular communication.
