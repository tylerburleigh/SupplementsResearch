## Core Findings

This white paper reviews treatment options for patients with the MTHFR C677T mutation, focusing on L-methylfolate and folic acid supplementation for depression. The key takeaway is that L-methylfolate bypasses the MTHFR conversion step and may improve depression outcomes both as monotherapy and as an adjunct to antidepressants. However, genotype-stratified evidence is extremely limited (only one small study), and no dosing guidelines exist.

## Key Evidence

### MTHFR C677T Functional Impact
- Heterozygous (C/T): 35% reduction in MTHFR enzyme activity
- Homozygous (T/T): 70% reduction in MTHFR enzyme activity
- Result: reduced capacity to convert folic acid/dietary folate into active L-methylfolate
- Sources: Frosst et al. 1995 (Nat. Genet.); Chango et al. 2000 (Br. J. Nutr.)

### L-Methylfolate for Depression
- **Monotherapy studies (cited):**
  - Guaraldi et al. 1993 (Ann. Clin. Psychiatry) -- open trial in elderly depressed patients
  - Passeri et al. 1993 (Aging Clin. Exp. Res.) -- double-blind multicenter study in senile organic mental disorders
  - Di Palma et al. 1994 (Curr. Ther. Res.) -- methylfolate in depressed alcoholics
  - Shelton et al. 2013 (Prim Care Companion CNS Disord) -- real-world patient experience trial
- **Adjunctive therapy studies (cited):**
  - Godfrey et al. 1990 (Lancet) -- methylfolate enhancement of psychiatric recovery
  - Papakostas et al. 2012 (Am. J. Psychiatry) -- L-methylfolate as adjunctive therapy for SSRI-resistant major depression; two randomized, double-blind, parallel-sequential trials
  - Papakostas et al. 2014 (J. Clin. Psychiatry) -- L-methylfolate 15 mg adjunct in SSRI-inadequate responders, stratified by biomarker levels and genotype

### Genotype-Stratified Evidence (Critical Gap)
- **Only one study** has evaluated MTHFR C677T genotype in depressed patients taking L-methylfolate (Papakostas et al. 2014)
- Found **no significant improvement** on HDRS-28 for any MTHFR genotype
- However, sample was small and there was a **trend** for CT/TT patients benefiting more
- **No studies** have evaluated MTHFR C677T genotype in depressed patients taking folic acid supplementation

### Folic Acid for Depression
- Monotherapy studies: mixed results
- Adjunctive studies: mixed -- some show improvement (Coppen & Bailey 2000; Resler et al. 2008; Venkatasubramanian et al. 2013), others including a meta-analysis show no clinical benefit (Basoglu et al. 2009; Bedson et al. 2014; Sarris et al. 2016)
- Risks: high-dose folic acid may mask vitamin B12 deficiency and exacerbate neurological effects of B12 deficiency

### Dosing
- No guidelines exist for folic acid or L-methylfolate dosing in MTHFR C677T patients
- Standard: 400 mcg folic acid in multivitamin, 800 mcg in prenatal vitamin
- L-methylfolate available OTC or as prescription-only product
