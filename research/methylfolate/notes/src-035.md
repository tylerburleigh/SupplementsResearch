## Core Findings

1. This is the authoritative BOND expert panel review covering all aspects of folate biology and biomarkers. It identifies three priority biomarkers for folate status assessment: serum folate (short-term status), RBC folate (long-term status), and plasma homocysteine (functional but non-specific). The review provides detailed analytical considerations, cutoff values, and clinical interpretation guidance for each.

2. The MTHFR enzyme catalyzes the irreversible generation of 5-methyltetrahydrofolate (5-methyl-THF, i.e., methylfolate), the primary circulating folate form. The MTHFR 677C>T polymorphism reduces the variant enzyme's affinity for its FAD cofactor, leading to low serum/RBC folate, elevated homocysteine, and altered folate vitamer distribution (formyl-folates replace a portion of 5-methyl-THF polyglutamates in RBCs). Riboflavin supplementation can stabilize the variant enzyme.

3. Folic acid (synthetic form) must be reduced by DHFR to THF before entering one-carbon metabolism. The first reduction step is slow and rate-limiting. Intake exceeding ~200 mcg/meal leads to unmetabolized folic acid in circulation. The main circulating folate vitamer is 5-methyl-THF. Both folic acid and 5-methyl-THF are poor substrates for conversion to retainable polyglutamates.

## Key Evidence and Data Points

### Folate Metabolism and MTHFR
- THF polyglutamates are the form of the vitamin in cells and food from natural sources
- Folic acid is a chemically stable, fully oxidized pro-vitamin that must be reduced to THF by DHFR
- MTHFR catalyzes irreversible generation of 5-methyl-THF in vivo
- The "methyl trap" occurs when 5-methyl-THF accumulates in vitamin B-12 deficiency due to lack of MTR activity
- MTHFR is allosterically inhibited by SAM
- 5-Methyl-THF (in polyglutamyl form) inhibits GNMT and is part of homeostatic regulation

### MTHFR 677C>T Polymorphism
- Reduced affinity for FAD cofactor; decreased activity of the variant enzyme
- When folate or riboflavin status is low, TT genotype individuals have low serum/RBC folate and elevated homocysteine
- Riboflavin supplementation at 1.6 mg/d can stabilize the variant enzyme in vivo
- Hypertensive patients with TT genotype are responsive to blood pressure lowering with riboflavin intervention
- In RBCs, formyl-folates replace a portion of 5-methyl-THF polyglutamates in 677C>T carriers
- Confirmed NTD risk factor in most studies and a large meta-analysis
- Men with TT genotype use more choline as a methyl donor

### Folate Forms and Bioavailability
- 5-Methyl-THF is the main circulating folate vitamer in serum
- Folic acid and 5-methyl-THF are poor substrates for conversion to retainable polyglutamates
- Unmetabolized folic acid in fasting plasma is very low (0-2 nmol/L), poorly correlated with total folate
- Folic acid has very high affinity for folate-binding protein (FBP), which may explain persistence in plasma
- DFE multiplier of 1.7 accounts for differential bioavailability (folic acid ~85% vs food folate ~50%)
- Unmetabolized folic acid appears in serum from intake exceeding ~200 mcg/meal

### Biomarker Details
- Serum folate: earliest indicator of altered folate exposure, short-term status, highly responsive to folic acid intervention
- RBC folate: reflects preceding 120 days, parallels liver concentrations (~50% total body folate), most robust long-term biomarker
- Plasma homocysteine: functional but non-specific biomarker, responds within 3-4 weeks of depletion/repletion
- Serum folate deficiency cutoff: <10 nmol/L; RBC folate deficiency cutoff: <340 nmol/L (WHO 2005)
- Optimal NTD prevention: RBC folate ~1000-1300 nmol/L; WHO recommends >906 nmol/L (>400 mcg/L) for women of reproductive age
- Post-fortification: prevalence of low serum or RBC folate <=1% in the US
- Plasma folate peaks ~2h after oral folic acid dose, mean residence time ~10 min

### Nutrient Interactions
- Vitamin B-12: required for methionine synthase; deficiency causes methyl trap and falsely elevated serum folate
- Riboflavin: FAD cofactor for MTHFR; effects of low status most prominent in TT genotype
- Vitamin B-6: weakly affects plasma homocysteine; cystathionine is more sensitive functional indicator
- Choline: can partially compensate for folate insufficiency via betaine-dependent remethylation
- Iron: PCFT is shared transporter; ferritin catalyzes folate turnover

## Methodology

- Expert panel review (BOND Folate Expert Panel) convened by NIH NICHD
- Systematic evaluation of published literature on folate biology and biomarkers
- Organized by biomarker type with detailed analytical considerations (MBA, PBA, LC-MS/MS)
- Provided guidance on clinical and population-level interpretation
- Covered preanalytical factors, quality assessment, and standardization

---

## Limitations

- Review published in 2015; does not cover developments after that date
- Primarily focused on folic acid rather than methylfolate supplementation
- Acknowledges that methods for serum/RBC folate "have not yet reached the point where they produce sufficiently comparable results across methods or laboratories"
- No formal standardization program existed for folate measurements at time of publication
- Most NTD prevention evidence is based on folic acid, not 5-methyl-THF supplementation
- Limited discussion of direct methylfolate supplementation outcomes
