# src-026: L-methylfolate supplementation for sleep disturbance in MTHFR polymorphism patients

## Core Findings

In a retrospective cohort of 10 patients with MTHFR polymorphisms (C667T and/or A1298C), supplementation with 5 mg/day L-methylfolate produced statistically significant improvement in PROMIS Sleep Disturbance scores at 2 weeks (-6.94 points, p = 0.005) and 8 weeks (-14.34 points, p = 0.001). By 8 weeks, all patients showed improvement across both low and intermediate MTHFR function phenotypes.

## Key Evidence / Data Points

- **Sample:** n = 10 (6 male, 4 female), mean age 43 +/- 16 years
- **MTHFR polymorphisms:** C667T and/or A1298C
- **Intervention:** 5 mg L-methylfolate daily (oral supplementation)
- **Outcome measure:** PROMIS (Patient-Reported Outcomes Measurement Information System) Sleep Disturbance
- **Assessment timepoints:** Baseline, 2 weeks, 4 weeks, 8 weeks
- **Results at week 2:** -6.94 point change, p = 0.005
- **Results at week 8:** -14.34 point change, p = 0.001; all patients improved
- **Phenotypes:** Improvement seen in both low and intermediate MTHFR function phenotypes
- **Statistical methods:** ANOVA and T-test comparisons

## Methodology

- **Design:** Retrospective cohort study
- **Setting:** Single concierge medical clinic
- **Patient selection:** Patients presenting with sleep complaints who had known MTHFR polymorphisms
- **Assessment:** PROMIS Sleep Disturbance at baseline, 2, 4, and 8 weeks
- **Analysis:** ANOVA and T-test comparisons

## Limitations

1. **Very small sample size (n=10):** Limits statistical power and generalizability
2. **No control group:** Retrospective uncontrolled design cannot rule out placebo or time effects
3. **Single clinic setting (concierge medicine):** Patient population may not be representative of general clinical populations
4. **Abstract only:** Full text not accessible; cannot verify detailed methods, inclusion/exclusion criteria, adverse events, or dropout rates
5. **No reporting of 4-week results:** Although patients were evaluated at 4 weeks, results for this timepoint are not reported in the abstract

## Relevance

Directly relevant to L-methylfolate supplementation in MTHFR polymorphism carriers, specifically for sleep disturbance as an outcome. Provides clinical outcome data supporting efficacy of 5 mg/day dosing. The PROMIS instrument is a validated patient-reported outcome. However, the evidence is limited by small sample size and lack of a control group.
