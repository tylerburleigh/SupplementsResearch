L-Methylfolate: The Active Folate for Brain and Mood Support!

L-Methylfolate is the active, body-ready form of folate, an essential B vitamin (B9). Unlike standard folic acid, which must be converted by the body before it can be used, L-Methylfolate bypasses this entire process. This makes it a highly bioavailable nutrient, ready to support critical functions like neurotransmitter production, DNA synthesis, and cellular health from the moment it’s absorbed.

This direct availability is particularly important for the significant portion of the population with variations in the MTHFR gene, which can impair the conversion of folic acid to its active form. L-Methylfolate FAQs

1. What is the difference between L-Methylfolate and Folic Acid?

Folic acid is a synthetic form of folate that requires a multi-step conversion process in the body to become active. L-Methylfolate (5-MTHF) is the active form itself, meaning it is ‘body-ready’ and can be used immediately without conversion.

2. Why is this form better if I have an MTHFR gene variant?

The MTHFR enzyme is crucial for converting folic acid into active L-Methylfolate. If you have a common gene variant, this enzyme works less efficiently. Supplementing with L-Methylfolate bypasses this faulty enzyme, delivering the active form your body needs directly.

3. Can I get L-Methylfolate from food?

Natural folate is found in foods like leafy greens and legumes, but it is often unstable and easily destroyed by cooking. Fortified foods contain folic acid, not L-Methylfolate. Supplementation provides a stable, concentrated, and active dose that is difficult to achieve from diet alone.
