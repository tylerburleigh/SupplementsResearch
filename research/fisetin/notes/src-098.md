# src-098: Krishnakumar et al. (2022) -- Enhanced bioavailability of fisetin via Hybrid-FENUMAT formulation

## Core Findings

This is the first human pharmacokinetic study of fisetin. A novel hybrid-hydrogel formulation (FF-20) encapsulating fisetin micelles within fenugreek galactomannan hydrogel scaffold achieved a **26.9-fold increase in AUC0-12h** and **23.9-fold increase in Cmax** compared to unformulated fisetin, despite delivering only 192 mg fisetin versus 982 mg in the unformulated arm. No significant adverse events were reported.

## Study Design

- **Design**: Randomized, double-blinded, comparative crossover study
- **Participants**: 15 healthy volunteers (12 males, 3 females), aged 22-55 years, BMI 18-25 kg/m2; 21 screened, 15 enrolled
- **Arms**: Single-dose 1000 mg unformulated fisetin (UF, 982 mg fisetin) vs 1000 mg FF-20 (192 mg fisetin)
- **Washout**: 10 days between treatments
- **Blood sampling**: 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, and 12 h post-dose
- **Analysis**: UPLC-ESI-MS/MS (triple quadrupole QTRAP); free (unconjugated) fisetin and geraldol measured
- **Ethics**: CTRI/2020/07/026748, registered ethical committee, Helsinki/ICH-GCP compliant
- **Registration**: CTRI/2020/07/026748, dated 23/07/2020
- **Funding**: Akay Natural Ingredients (manufacturer of FF-20); Life Extension sells FF-20 as "Bio-Fisetin"

## Key Pharmacokinetic Data (Table 1)

| Parameter | UF (982 mg fisetin) | FF-20 (192 mg fisetin) | Fold change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cmax (ng/ml) | 9.97 +/- 3.97 | 238.2 +/- 87.26 | 23.9x (p<0.0001) |
| Tmax (h) | 0.88 +/- 0.18 | 1.24 +/- 0.35 | -- (p<0.05) |
| t1/2 (h) | 1.14 +/- 0.09 | 1.51 +/- 0.05 | -- (p<0.05) |
| AUC0-12 (ng*h/ml) | 12.67 +/- 4.86 | 341.4 +/- 130.05 | 26.9x (p<0.0001) |

Note: Results were normalized for the difference in fisetin content between arms.

## Geraldol (Active Metabolite) Pharmacokinetics

| Parameter | UF | FF-20 |
|---|---|---|
| Cmax (ng/ml) | 24.0 +/- 7.69 | 239.23 +/- 94.09 |
| Tmax (h) | 0.90 +/- 0.12 | 1.13 +/- 0.25 |
| t1/2 (h) | 1.18 +/- 0.08 | 1.59 +/- 0.1 |
| AUC0-12 (ng*h/ml) | 20.48 +/- 6.19 | 227.14 +/- 89.71 |
| Geraldol/Fiseton AUC ratio | 1.62 | 0.67 |

The lower geraldol-to-fisetin ratio with FF-20 indicates reduced metabolic conversion of fisetin to geraldol, possibly due to hydrogel protection from presystemic biotransformation.

## Detection Window

- UF: Fisetin quantifiable only up to **2 h** post-dose
- FF-20: Fisetin quantifiable up to **8 h** post-dose
- UF at doses below 1000 mg: generally not quantifiable (500 mg detected only at 1 h time point)

## Formulation Details

- **FF-20**: Fisetin encapsulated as lecithin-based micelles within fenugreek galactomannan hydrogel matrix
- **Technology**: Hybrid-FENUMAT (proprietary, patented by Akay Natural Ingredients)
- **Encapsulation efficiency**: 93.3 +/- 0.78%
- **Fisetin content**: 19.2% w/w in FF-20 (vs 98.2% in UF)
- **Particle size**: Micelles ~50 nm; dissolved FF-20 particles ~151.6 nm
- **Mechanism**: Hydrogel swells in GI fluid, leaching galactomannan-coated nanomicelles for mucoadhesive intestinal delivery

## Safety

- No significant adverse events reported
- Two mild GI events: bloating sensation (1 case), decreased appetite (1 case)
- GI events were identical with both UF and FF-20
- Prior animal data: no toxicity up to 2000 mg/kg single oral dose in mice; 90-day dietary study showed no toxic manifestations

## Limitations

1. **Glucuronides and sulphates not measured** -- only free (unconjugated) fisetin and geraldol were quantified. Conjugated metabolites may represent significant additional bioavailability.
2. **Small sample size** (N=15), all healthy volunteers -- may not generalize to patient populations
3. **Single-dose study** -- no data on steady-state pharmacokinetics or accumulation
4. **Industry-funded**: Study funded by Akay Natural Ingredients (manufacturer of FF-20); the formulation is sold commercially as "Bio-Fisetin" by Life Extension
5. **Bioanalytical range**: Plasma calibration 1-1000 ng/ml; extraction efficiency >84% for fisetin, 81% for geraldol
6. **Normalization concern**: Results were normalized for fisetin content difference (192 mg vs 982 mg), which involves a mathematical adjustment rather than a direct dose-matched comparison

## Claims to Verify

- The fold-improvement figures (26.9x AUC, 23.9x Cmax) are after normalization for the ~5x difference in actual fisetin content between arms. Without normalization, the raw fold-change would be even larger. The normalization methodology is not explicitly detailed in the text.
- The paper states "no adverse events were reported" in the abstract but the body text reports two GI events. This minor inconsistency should be noted.

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q3 (pharmacokinetics)**: Strongly relevant. First human PK data for fisetin. Provides concrete Cmax, AUC, Tmax, t1/2 values for both UF and formulated fisetin. Demonstrates extremely poor baseline bioavailability of unformulated fisetin (AUC 12.67 ng*h/ml at ~1 g dose) and dramatic improvement with hydrogel formulation.
- **Q6 (dosing)**: Relevant. Shows that 1000 mg unformulated fisetin yields barely detectable plasma levels, suggesting much higher doses or enhanced formulations are needed for therapeutic effect. The 192 mg fisetin in FF-20 achieved far superior PK, suggesting lower effective doses may be possible with proper formulation.
