They secrete a mix of inflammatory cytokines, growth factors, and proteases known as the senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP). The SASP creates a pro-inflammatory environment that damages surrounding tissues and promotes the development of age-related diseases. Fisetin has been shown to selectively induce apoptosis in senescent cells, effectively reducing the burden of these harmful cells in the body.
