What are natural senolytics?

Natural senolytics are compounds found in plants that help the body clear aging, dysfunctional cells (senescent cells). These compounds work by targeting specific cellular pathways involved in cellular aging. Common natural senolytics include flavonoids like quercetin and fisetin, which are found in various fruits and vegetables but in much smaller quantities than what’s provided in supplemental form.

What is the best natural senolytic?

Based on current research, fisetin is considered the most potent senolytic among tested flavonoids. However, the most effective approach combines multiple senolytic compounds, as different senolytics can target different types of senescent cells through various mechanisms. This is why SenoAid™ combines both fisetin and quercetin, along with supporting ingredients, for comprehensive senolytic support.

Do natural senolytics help eliminate senescent cells?

Natural senolytics have been shown in research to help manage senescent cells. These compounds work by targeting specific pathways that senescent cells rely on for survival, while leaving healthy cells unaffected. Quercetin and fisetin, the primary ingredients in SenoAid™, are both backed by extensive research demonstrating their effectiveness in targeting and clearing senescent cells.

How long does it take senolytics dietary supplements to work?

The timeframe for experiencing benefits from senolytics can vary depending on individual factors such as age, lifestyle, and overall health status. Some people report feeling initial benefits like reduced inflammation and improved energy within a few weeks, while the full range of benefits may develop over several months of consistent use. For best results, SenoAid™ should be taken regularly as part of a long-term health maintenance strategy.

SenoAid™ may also provide acute relief of allergy symptoms thanks to the natural antihistamine properties of quercetin and the anti-inflammatory effects of bromelain, with many users reporting improvements within hours to days of starting supplementation.

Which is better, quercetin or fisetin?

Rather than considering one better than the other, quercetin and fisetin are complementary compounds that work best together. Fisetin is recognized as the most potent natural senolytic currently known, while quercetin offers additional benefits including natural antihistamine properties and uric acid regulation.

SenoAid™ combines both ingredients because they target different types of senescent cells and provide distinct but complementary health benefits. This synergistic approach offers more comprehensive support than either compound alone.
