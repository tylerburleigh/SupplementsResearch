Exploring the Disadvantages of Fisetin: Understanding Potential Drawbacks

Although preclinical studies have demonstrated its safety and tolerability, comprehensive clinical trials are needed to assess the potential adverse effects of prolonged fisetin supplementation. Additionally, interactions with other medications and potential contraindications need to be thoroughly investigated to ensure the safe use of fisetin as a dietary supplement for anti-aging purposes.
