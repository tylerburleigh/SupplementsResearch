Details

Thorne Iron Bisgylcinate is formulated for enhanced iron absorption. This formulation is NSF Certified for Sport to ensure the absence of banned substances. Athletes need approximately 1.5 times as much Iron as non-athletes. Those who follow a vegetarian or lower protein diet may have needs as high as 1.8 times as much dietary iron. Iron is essential for the formation of red blood cells. Untreated chronic low levels of iron can lead to iron-deficient anemia. Thorne's Iron Bisglycinate contains elemental iron that has been reacted with glycine for optimal absorption in the gastrointestinal tract help to decrease typical gastrointestinal side effects. One bottle of Thorne Iron Bisgylcinate contains 90 capsules. The recommended dose is 1 capsule a day.
