Ingredients
NatureWise Iron Bisglycinate Plus is specially formulated with Ferrochel® to provide a gentle yet effective iron supplement that maximizes absorption with the least amount of gastrointestinal side effects.*
To further support iron absorption, this supplement contains vitamin C, vitamin B12, and folate.* Vitamin C plays a key role in increasing iron uptake, ensuring the body can effectively utilize it. B12 and folate help support healthy red blood cell formation and energy production, working alongside iron.*
NatureWise Iron Bisglycinate Plus goes beyond basic iron supplementation by incorporating a blend of organic whole foods, including quinoa, tomato, spinach, kale, carrots, broccoli, broccoli sprout, and beetroot. Supplement Facts

Ferrous Bisglycinate Chelate

Ferrochel® is a fully chelated iron that is known to cause significantly fewer instances of GI upset compared to ferrous sulfate and has better bioavailability than traditional iron salt forms.* This type of iron is bound to the amino acid glycine, making it less prone to interference from anti-nutrients and improving absorption compared to iron salts.*

Vitamin C

Vitamin C is an important antioxidant that supports collagen synthesis and overall immune health.*

Methylated B12

Methylcobalamin is methylated form of vitamin B12 that easier to absorb and supports energy, metabolism and neurological function.*

Methylated Folate

Methylated Folate, as calcium L-5-Methyltetrahydrofolate is a methylated form of vitamin B9 that is naturally occurring.* Since it is the active form of folate, it is readily usable by the body, whereas folic acid must be converted which can be problematic for some people.* Methylated folate supports the production of new cells and can support heart health.*

Organic Beet Root

Beet root or beta vulgaris is a nutritious root vegetable that contains minerals like iron, as well as vitamin C, natural nitrates, and antioxidants like betalains*

Organic Whole Food Blend

This blend of organic whole foods contains quinoa, tomato, spinach, kale, carrots, broccoli, and broccoli sprout for complimentary nutrients, natural iron and antioxidants.*
