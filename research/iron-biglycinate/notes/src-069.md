# src-069: Low-Dose Liquid Fermented Iron-Bisglycinate for IDWA in Premenopausal Women

**Source:** PubMed PMID 37299583 (Nutrients journal, MDPI)
**URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37299583
**Year:** 2023 (inferred from PMID)
**Type:** Randomized, placebo-controlled trial (abstract only; full text behind PMC/MDPI)
**Quality:** Abstract-level content only; no access to full methods/results.

## Core Findings

A low-dose (27 mg/day) liquid fermented iron-bisglycinate supplement improved blood-iron status in premenopausal women with iron deficiency without anemia (IDWA) over 8 weeks, significantly increasing serum ferritin (p=0.03), total serum iron (p=0.03), and mean corpuscular volume (p=0.02) versus placebo, with no increase in gastrointestinal distress.

## Key Evidence

- **N=85** premenopausal women with IDWA (ferritin < 70 ng/dL, hemoglobin > 11.0 g/dL)
- Randomized to LIS (27 mg iron-bisglycinate) or placebo for 8 weeks
- Serum ferritin increased significantly (p=0.03)
- Total serum iron increased significantly (p=0.03)
- Mean corpuscular volume (MCV) increased significantly (p=0.02)
- No significant GI distress vs placebo (p > 0.05) at Wk0, Wk4, Wk8 assessments
- No significant effect on Profile of Mood States (POMS) (p > 0.05)
- IDWA diagnostic criteria used: ferritin < 70 ng/dL AND hemoglobin > 11.0 g/dL

## Methodology

- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
- 85 premenopausal women with IDWA
- Daily supplementation: 27 mg iron (as liquid fermented iron-bisglycinate) or placebo
- Duration: 8 weeks
- Blood draws at Week 0 and Week 8 for serum iron markers
- GI distress surveys at Week 0, 4, and 8
- POMS survey at Week 0 and Week 8

## Limitations

- **Industry-funded:** MegaFood (the supplement manufacturer) funded the study; 3 authors + medical advisor are MegaFood employees/affiliates. They were excluded from data collection and analysis.
- **Abstract only:** Full text not available; cannot verify methodological details, effect sizes, confidence intervals, or completeness of reporting.
- **Specific population:** Results limited to premenopausal women with IDWA; generalizability to other populations (men, postmenopausal women, healthy individuals) is unknown.
- **No alternate-day protocol:** Used daily dosing only; cannot speak to alternate-day efficacy.
- **Short duration:** 8 weeks may be insufficient to assess long-term safety or sustained ferritin improvements.

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q1 (health consequences of low-normal iron):** Indirectly relevant -- treats IDWA as a clinically meaningful condition worth treating, using ferritin < 70 ng/dL as the threshold. Does not directly assess health consequences.
- **Q2 (iron-longevity U-curve):** Not directly relevant. No longevity outcomes measured.
- **Q3 (biglycinate vs other forms):** Highly relevant. Demonstrates iron-bisglycinate efficacy at low dose (27 mg) with good GI tolerability. Supports bisglycinate as a well-tolerated form.
- **Q4 (low-dose/alternate-day protocols):** Relevant. 27 mg/day is a low dose (vs typical 65-325 mg prescriptions), and it worked. No alternate-day arm, however.
- **Q5 (risks of supplementing non-deficient):** Partially relevant -- shows GI safety in IDWA population, but participants were iron-deficient, not iron-replete. Cannot inform risks for non-deficient individuals.
- **Q6 (absorption optimization):** Marginally relevant. Liquid fermented formulation may enhance absorption/tolerability, but no absorption kinetics were measured.
- **Q7 (clinical guidelines):** Supports low-dose bisglycinate as a clinical approach for IDWA, using ferritin < 70 ng/dL as treatment threshold.

## Claims to Verify

- Exact effect sizes for ferritin, serum iron, and MCV increases are not reported in the abstract -- only p-values. The magnitude of change is important for clinical significance assessment.
- The ferritin threshold of < 70 ng/dL for IDWA is notably higher than some guidelines (which use 15-30 ng/dL). Verify if this was justified in the full text.
- N=85 total -- unclear how many per group (likely ~42-43 each), but randomization ratio not stated in abstract.
