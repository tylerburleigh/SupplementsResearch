# src-087: Erinacine A-enriched Hericium erinaceus mycelium ameliorates AD pathologies in APP/PS1 mice

**Citation:** Tsai-Teng T, et al. Journal of Biomedical Science (2016) 23:49. DOI: 10.1186/s12929-016-0266-z
**Type:** Open-access peer-reviewed research article
**Quality:** ok (full text extracted, substantive)

## Core Findings

Oral administration of erinacine A-enriched Hericium erinaceus mycelia (HE-My) and its ethanol extract (HE-Et) at 300 mg/kg/day for 30 days significantly reduced amyloid plaque burden in APPswe/PS1dE9 transgenic mice, primarily through upregulation of insulin-degrading enzyme (IDE) and reduction of non-compact plaque structures. The treatments also increased NGF/proNGF ratio, promoted hippocampal neurogenesis, reduced plaque-associated glial activation, and improved nesting behavior (ADL proxy) after 81 days.

## Key Evidence and Data Points

### Plaque Reduction
- **HE-My**: plaque burden reduced 38.6 +/- 9.3%, plaque number reduced 34.8 +/- 13.4%
- **HE-Et**: plaque burden reduced 55.8 +/- 15.2%, plaque number reduced 43.5 +/- 15.6%
- Reduction was specific to the **non-compact (diffuse/filamentary)** portion of plaques; the ThS-positive compact core was unchanged
- HE-My reduced soluble Abeta(1-42) by ~40% (significant); HE-Et did not significantly affect soluble or insoluble Abeta levels

### Mechanisms (IDE Upregulation)
- IDE expression increased by 127.7 +/- 81.4% (HE-Et) and 103.9 +/- 30.9% (HE-My) in cortex
- No effect on neprilysin levels
- No effect on APP processing (APP, CTF-alpha, CTF-beta unchanged)
- Plaque-associated microglial clusters reduced by 25.2% (HE-Et) and 19.4% (HE-My)
- Plaque-associated astrocyte clusters reduced by 40.0% (HE-Et) and 43.3% (HE-My)
- Iba-1 (microglial activation marker) reduced to 47.9% (HE-Et) and 37.9% (HE-My) of control
- GFAP (astrocyte reactivity) reduced to 64.3% (HE-Et) and 55.7% (HE-My) of control

### NGF and Neurogenesis
- NGF/proNGF ratio increased by 124.7 +/- 54.1% (HE-Et) and 109.9 +/- 25.7% (HE-My)
- Both DCX-positive (newly born neurons) and BrdU-positive (proliferating progenitors) cells in dentate gyrus subgranular zone were restored toward WT levels

### Behavioral/Cognitive Improvement
- Nesting behavior (ADL proxy) improved after 81-day HE-My treatment at 300 mg/kg/day
- APP/PS1 mice had lower nesting scores and less nestlet shredding vs WT; HE-My recovered both deficits

### Dosing and Composition
- **Dose**: 300 mg/kg/day oral gavage
- Pathological assessment: 30-day treatment
- Behavioral assessment: 81-day treatment
- **HE-My**: 19 mg/g erinacine A content
- **HE-Et**: 104.4 mg/g erinacine A content (ethanol extract concentrates the active compounds ~5.5x)
- Also contains erinacine C and erinacine S (identified by NMR and LC-MS-MS)
- Safe dose established at 3000 mg/kg in 28-day rat study (10x therapeutic dose)

## Methodology

- **Model**: APPswe/PS1dE9 double transgenic female mice (Jackson Lab #005864), 5 months old at treatment start
- **Sample sizes**: Vehicle n=6, HE-Et n=5-6, HE-My n=5-6, WT n=7-8
- **Route**: Oral gavage dissolved in 3% DMSO/10% cremophor EL/87.5% D5W
- **Plaque assessment**: Double staining with Thioflavin S (compact core) and AB10 antibody (whole plaque), quantified with ImageJ
- **Abeta measurement**: Sandwich ELISA for Abeta(1-42) in SDS-soluble and formic acid-insoluble fractions
- **IDE, NGF**: Western blot quantification
- **Neurogenesis**: BrdU injection (50 mg/kg/day, last 7 days) + DCX immunohistochemistry in dentate gyrus SGZ
- **Behavior**: Nesting test (nest score 1-5 scale, nestlet shredding weight)
- **Statistics**: ANOVA with post-hoc Bonferroni

## Limitations

- **Female mice only** -- authors note female APP/PS1 mice have more plaque deposition; generalizability to males unclear
- **No dose-response** -- only single dose (300 mg/kg) tested; ceiling effect acknowledged
- **Small sample sizes** (n=5-6 per group)
- **Behavioral assessment limited** -- only nesting behavior; no spatial memory tests (Morris water maze, Y-maze, etc.)
- **No direct human dosing translation** -- 300 mg/kg in mice is much higher than typical human supplement doses (~1-3 g/day)
- HE-Et paradoxically showed no significant effect on soluble/insoluble Abeta levels despite greater plaque burden reduction, suggesting mechanisms beyond simple Abeta level reduction
- Study supported by Grape King Bio Ltd (industry funder); patent application ongoing (data not shared)

## Relevance to Research Questions

**Q7 (AD model results, plaque reduction, cognitive improvement, dosing):** Strong relevance. Direct evidence of plaque burden reduction (38-56%) and behavioral improvement (nesting) in a well-established AD transgenic mouse model at 300 mg/kg/day. Detailed dosing and composition data provided.

**Q2 (Mechanisms, erinacine A, plaque clearance pathways):** Strong relevance. Identifies IDE upregulation as the primary mechanism for enhanced Abeta clearance, along with reduced glial activation and increased NGF/proNGF ratio. Erinacine A is the named active compound.

**Q3 (Dose levels and human dosing relevance):** Moderate relevance. Provides animal dose (300 mg/kg/day), composition (19 mg/g erinacine A in HE-My), and safety data (3000 mg/kg NOAEL in rats). No direct human dose equivalence calculation, but the data allow for allometric scaling.

## Claims to Verify

- **Soluble Abeta reduction by ~40% with HE-My** (mentioned in Discussion, line ~558): The text states "HE-My-treated APP/PS1 mice showed a 40% reduction" but the actual figure (Fig 3c) data are not numerically reported in Results -- confirm this matches the ELISA data shown.
- **Sample sizes**: Vehicle n=6, HE-Et n=5, HE-My n=6 for pathological tests; n=6 for behavioral tests. The n=5 for HE-Et is notably small and the large SD on IDE increase (127.7 +/- 81.4%) suggests high variability that may not reach significance with n=5 -- verify p-values reported.
