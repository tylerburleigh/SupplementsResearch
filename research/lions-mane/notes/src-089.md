# src-089: Erinacine A-Enriched HE Mycelium - Antidepressant Effects via BDNF/PI3K/Akt/GSK-3-beta in Mice

**Citation:** Chiu et al. (2018). *Int J Mol Sci*, 19(2):341. DOI: 10.3390/ijms19020341. PMCID: PMC5855563, PMID: 29364170.

## Core Findings

Erinacine A-enriched *Hericium erinaceus* (HE) mycelium (100, 200, 400 mg/kg/day, oral, 4 weeks) produced significant antidepressant-like effects in male ICR mice subjected to 14 days of repeated restraint stress. The effects were mediated through restoration of hippocampal monoamine neurotransmitters (NE, DA, 5-HT), suppression of pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-alpha), activation of the BDNF/TrkB/PI3K/Akt/GSK-3-beta signaling pathway, and inhibition of NF-kB nuclear translocation. The medium (200 mg/kg) and high (400 mg/kg) doses were consistently more effective than the low dose (100 mg/kg).

## Key Evidence

### Behavioral Results (Q7)
- **TST**: HE at 200 and 400 mg/kg significantly reduced immobility time (p < 0.01 vs. RS group)
- **FST**: HE at 200 and 400 mg/kg reduced immobility (p < 0.01); all doses increased swimming time (100: p < 0.01; 200/400: p < 0.001)
- **EPM**: HE at 200 and 400 mg/kg increased POAE by 21-24% and PTOA by ~22% (p < 0.01); no change in closed arm entries
- **OFT**: No significant differences in crossings or rearings across groups, confirming anti-immobility effects are not due to locomotor changes
- **Defecation**: RS group showed ~82% increase in defecation (p < 0.01); 200 and 400 mg/kg HE reduced defecation by ~27% (p < 0.05)

### Neurotransmitter Results (Q2)
- Hippocampal NE: Significant restoration only at 400 mg/kg (p < 0.05)
- Hippocampal DA: Significant increase at all three doses (p < 0.001)
- Hippocampal 5-HT: 200 mg/kg raised by 81.6%; 400 mg/kg raised by 92.5%

### Inflammatory Cytokine Results (Q2)
- IL-6: Significantly reduced at 200 mg/kg (p < 0.05) and 400 mg/kg (p < 0.01)
- TNF-alpha: Significantly suppressed at all doses (100: p < 0.05; 200/400: p < 0.01)

### Signaling Pathway (Q2)
- **BDNF/TrkB/PI3K**: Restraint stress downregulated all three; HE at all tested doses reversed this downregulation
- **Akt/GSK-3-beta**: Total protein unchanged; phosphorylation of both was decreased by stress and restored by HE
- **NF-kB/IkB**: Stress reduced cytosolic levels (indicating nuclear translocation); HE normalized cytosolic NF-kB and IkB levels, blocking the inflammatory cascade

## Methodology

- **Animals**: Male ICR mice, 20-25 g, n=10 per group, 5 groups total
- **HE preparation**: Ethanolic extract of *H. erinaceus* mycelium (strain BCRC 35669), cultivated via submerged fermentation and lyophilized
- **Standardization**: Erinacine A content = 5.01 mg/g dry weight (confirmed by HPLC/LC-ESI-MS)
- **Dosing**: 100, 200, 400 mg/kg/day oral gavage for 4 weeks
- **Stress protocol**: 14 days restraint stress (2 h/day in ventilated restrainers), starting on day 15 of HE treatment
- **Behavioral tests**: TST, FST, EPM, OFT (video-recorded, blinded scoring)
- **Biochemical assays**: ELISA for NE, DA, 5-HT (hippocampus) and IL-6, TNF-alpha (plasma)
- **Western blot**: BDNF, TrkB, PI3K, Akt-p, Akt, GSK-3-beta-p, GSK-3-beta, NF-kB, IkB in hippocampus
- **Statistics**: One-way ANOVA with Tukey post-hoc; significance at p < 0.05

## Dose-Response Pattern

A clear dose-response relationship was observed: the 100 mg/kg dose was effective for DA restoration, FST swimming time, and TNF-alpha suppression, but the 200 and 400 mg/kg doses showed broader efficacy across all behavioral and biochemical measures. The 200 and 400 mg/kg doses were roughly equivalent in most measures, suggesting a plateau effect above 200 mg/kg.

## Limitations

- **Animal model only**: Male ICR mice; no female subjects, no human data
- **No positive control**: No pharmaceutical antidepressant (e.g., fluoxetine) was included for comparison
- **Single stress paradigm**: Only restraint stress was used; findings may not generalize to other depression models (chronic unpredictable mild stress, learned helplessness, etc.)
- **Whole extract**: The extract contained multiple erinacines (A, Q, C, S); effects cannot be attributed solely to erinacine A
- **No dose escalation beyond 400 mg/kg**: Cannot determine ceiling effect or optimal dose
- **Mechanistic speculation**: Authors speculate erinacine A may act as a monoamine receptor agonist or reuptake inhibitor but provide no direct evidence for this
- **Industry affiliation**: Co-authors from Grape King Bio Ltd. (company that produces HE supplements)

## Relevance to Research Questions

**Q2 (BDNF/PI3K/Akt/GSK-3-beta pathway)**: Provides the most detailed mechanistic evidence for this specific signaling cascade in Lion's Mane depression research. Demonstrates that HE activates BDNF/TrkB, upregulates PI3K, increases Akt and GSK-3-beta phosphorylation, and inhibits NF-kB -- all in the hippocampus of stressed mice. First study to comprehensively map this pathway for HE antidepressant effects.

**Q7 (Antidepressant-like behavioral measures)**: Robust behavioral evidence across four validated tests (TST, FST, EPM, OFT) with appropriate locomotor controls. Dose-response data at three levels. Well-designed 2-week pre-treatment before stress onset protocol.
