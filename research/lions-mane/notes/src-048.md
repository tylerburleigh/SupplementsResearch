# src-048: Hericium erinaceus Extract Reduces Anxiety and Depressive Behaviors by Promoting Adult Hippocampal Neurogenesis

**Source Quality:** Degraded (paywall -- PubMed abstract only, no full text). PMID 29091526.

## Core Findings

Chronic oral administration of Hericium erinaceus (HE) extract at 60 mg/kg for 4 weeks produced anxiolytic and antidepressant-like effects in male C57BL/6 mice, as measured by open field, tail suspension, and forced swim tests. The behavioral effects were associated with enhanced adult hippocampal neurogenesis: HE increased proliferation markers (PCNA, Ki67) in the subgranular zone and increased neuronal survival markers (BrdU, BrdU/NeuN) in the dentate gyrus.

## Key Evidence

- **Proliferation markers:** HE at 60 mg/kg (but not explicitly reported for 20 mg/kg) increased PCNA- and Ki67-positive cells in the subgranular zone of the hippocampus, indicating enhanced progenitor cell proliferation.
- **Neuronal survival:** BrdU (120 mg/kg, i.p.) given on day 1 of HE treatment, with brain isolation 4 weeks later, showed significantly increased BrdU- and BrdU/NeuN-positive cells in the dentate gyrus at the 60 mg/kg dose, demonstrating enhanced survival and neuronal differentiation of newborn cells.
- **Behavioral outcomes:** Anxiolytic effects (open field test) and antidepressant-like effects (tail suspension test, forced swim test) observed after 4 weeks of daily oral HE administration.

## Methodology

- **Subjects:** Male C57BL/6 mice
- **Treatment:** HE extract at 20 or 60 mg/kg, oral, once daily for 4 weeks; saline control
- **Proliferation assessment:** Immunohistochemistry for PCNA and Ki67 in hippocampal subgranular zone
- **Survival/differentiation assessment:** BrdU (120 mg/kg, i.p.) on day 1; brains isolated 4 weeks later; BrdU and BrdU/NeuN double-labeling in dentate gyrus
- **Behavioral tests:** Open field test and tail suspension test (30 min post last dose); forced swim test (2 days after)

## Limitations

- **Paywall restricted:** Only the abstract is available. Detailed results (exact cell counts, statistical values, dose-response at 20 mg/kg) cannot be verified.
- **Male-only:** Study used only male mice; sex differences in neurogenesis and behavioral responses are not addressed.
- **Mechanistic gap:** The abstract proposes hippocampal neurogenesis as the mechanism but does not rule out other pathways (e.g., neuroinflammation, BDNF, neurotransmitter modulation).
- **Dose specificity:** The abstract reports significant effects at 60 mg/kg but is ambiguous about whether 20 mg/kg produced any effects.
- **No specific extract characterization:** The bioactive components of the HE extract (hericenones, erinacines) are not specified.

- **Q2 (Mechanisms -- hippocampal neurogenesis, PCNA, Ki67, BrdU):** Directly relevant. This study uses all three marker types (PCNA, Ki67 for proliferation; BrdU for survival) and provides evidence that HE enhances hippocampal neurogenesis in adult mice. However, evidence is limited to abstract-level detail due to paywall.
- **Q7 (Animal models of anxiety/depression):** Relevant. Uses standard murine behavioral models (open field, tail suspension, forced swim) to demonstrate anxiolytic and antidepressant-like effects of HE extract.
