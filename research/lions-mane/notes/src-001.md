# Source src-001: Docherty et al. (2023) - Lion's Mane Acute and Chronic Effects on Cognition and Mood

## Citation
Docherty S, Doughty FL, Smith EF. "The Acute and Chronic Effects of Lion's Mane Mushroom Supplementation on Cognitive Function, Stress and Mood in Young Adults: A Double-Blind, Parallel Groups, Pilot Study." *Nutrients*. 2023 Nov 20;15(22):4842. doi: 10.3390/nu15224842. PMCID: PMC10675414.

## Core Findings

This pilot RCT found that a single 1.8 g dose of Hericium erinaceus improved Stroop task reaction time at 60 min post-dose (p = 0.005), representing the first evidence of acute cognitive enhancement from lion's mane in healthy young adults. After 28 days of supplementation, a trend toward reduced subjective stress (p = 0.051) was observed, though this did not reach conventional significance. Critically, some negative findings were also observed: lion's mane participants performed worse on immediate word recall acutely, and placebo outperformed on delayed word recall chronically. The authors emphasize cautious interpretation given the small sample size.

## Key Evidence

### Acute Effects (Single Dose, Day 1, n=43)
- **Stroop task speed**: Significant treatment x timepoint interaction (F(1,41) = 5.284, p = 0.027). Lion's mane group faster post-dose: 688.05 ms vs 737.70 ms baseline (p = 0.005). ~50 ms improvement.
- **Immediate word recall accuracy**: Significant interaction (F(1,41) = 8.028, p = 0.007). Lion's mane group recalled fewer correct words post-dose (p = 0.013). **Negative finding.**
- **Immediate word recall errors**: Significant interaction (F(1,41) = 5.831, p = 0.020). Placebo group made fewer errors post-dose (p = 0.002). **Negative finding.**
- **Episodic memory factor**: Significant interaction but no significant pairwise differences.

### Chronic Effects (28-Day, n=41)
- **Stress VAS**: Trending main effect of treatment (F(1,39) = 4.059, p = 0.051). Lion's mane group scored lower on Day 29 (33.02) vs placebo (42.53), pairwise p = 0.033. Trend toward reduction from Day 1 (40.64) to Day 29 (33.02), p = 0.055.
- **Delayed word recall accuracy**: Significant interaction (F(1,39) = 5.318, p = 0.027). Placebo group recalled more words at Day 29 vs Day 1 (p < 0.001). **Negative finding.**
- **Perceived Stress Scale (PSS)**: No treatment-related differences.
- **All other cognitive tasks and global scores**: No significant effects.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-groups pilot study
- **Population**: 43 healthy adults aged 18-45 (mean 26.35), recruited April-July 2023
- **Intervention**: 3 x 600 mg capsules/day (1.8 g total) of SO-DSX1 (Sempera Organics) proprietary Hericium erinaceus complex vs microcrystalline cellulose placebo
- **Duration**: 28 days
- **Assessments**: COMPASS cognitive battery (word recall, numeric working memory, choice RT, Stroop, peg and ball, word recognition), Stress VAS, VAMS, PSS
- **Timing**: Baseline and 60 min post-dose on Day 1 and Day 29
- **Compliance**: 96% by pill count; blinding confirmed effective
- **Analysis**: 2x2 ANOVAs (treatment x timepoint) with planned comparisons

## Limitations

1. **Small sample size** (n=43 acute, n=41 chronic) -- likely underpowered to detect modest effects in healthy adults at peak cognitive ability
2. **Single acute timepoint** (60 min) may be suboptimal; rat pharmacokinetic data suggest peak plasma concentration of erinacine S at 270 min post-dose
3. **Cognitive tasks may not have been demanding enough** to detect subtle enhancement in healthy young adults
4. **Mixed/negative results** on memory tasks temper enthusiasm for overall cognitive benefit
5. **Stress reduction only on one measure** (Stress VAS) and not on PSS
6. No biomarker data (BDNF, NGF, etc.) collected to confirm mechanistic pathways
7. Proprietary blend (SO-DSX1) -- exact composition not fully characterized

## Relevance to Research Questions

**Q1 (Human clinical trials - cognitive function and mood):** Highly relevant. This is a key pilot RCT in healthy young adults showing both acute (Stroop speed) and chronic (stress reduction trend) effects. The study is notable for being the first to assess acute effects and the first to focus specifically on young healthy adults. Effect sizes are modest and some negative findings exist. Duration was 28 days, insufficient to assess persistence of benefits post-cessation.

**Q4 (Safety, adverse events, dosing):** Relevant for dosing protocols (1.8 g/day x 28 days, capsule form). No serious adverse events reported. Blinding was effective. Compliance was high (96%). The study does not provide detailed adverse event reporting (no specific AE table or systematic symptom checklist appears in the main text).

## Claims to Verify

- The total enrolled sample was 43, with 41 completing. Two discontinued after initial visit and were replaced, with their Day 1 data retained. This is an unusual analytic approach (replacing dropouts while retaining partial data), which could affect randomization integrity.
- Compliance of 96% is self-reported via diary and pill count. Standard approach but not gold standard (would be serum/plasma biomarker verification).
- The product was SO-DSX1 from Sempera Organics -- described as "proprietary blend of organic Hericium erinaceus mushroom complex" but no standardization data (e.g., erinacine/hericenone content) provided in the main text.
