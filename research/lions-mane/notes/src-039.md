# src-039: Hericium erinaceus Improves Mood and Sleep Disorders in Patients Affected by Overweight or Obesity

**Status:** Degraded (paywall -- abstract and figure captions only, no full text)

**URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31118969
**PMID:** 31118969

## Core Findings

Eight weeks of oral Hericium erinaceus supplementation significantly decreased depression, anxiety, and sleep disorders in 77 overweight/obese volunteers who also had mood and/or sleep disorders. H. erinaceus increased circulating pro-BDNF levels without changing mature BDNF levels. Improvements were sustained at wash-out with no statistically significant regression.

## Study Design

- **Population:** 77 volunteers with overweight or obesity, recruited at IRCCS Foundation Policlinico Hospital of Milan (Italy)
- **Inclusion criteria:** Mood and/or sleep disorder and/or binge eating (assessed via self-evaluation questionnaires)
- **Design:** 8-week supplementation with low calorie diet; three timepoints: T0 (baseline), T1 (post-treatment), T2 (wash-out)
- **Outcome measures:** Zung's depression scale, Zung's anxiety scale, Binge Eating Scale (BES), SCL-90, pro-BDNF and BDNF serum levels (ELISA)
- **Statistics:** Two-way ANOVA (group x time), one-way ANOVA with Tukey post hoc, paired Student's t-test for biomarkers

## Key Evidence

- Depression, anxiety, and sleep scores improved significantly at T1 vs T0 (p<0.05 and p<0.01)
- Pro-BDNF increased significantly; mature BDNF did not change significantly
- T1 vs T2 comparison showed no significant differences, suggesting sustained benefit after cessation
- Patients selected for positive symptomatology at T0 showed the strongest improvements

## Dosing Protocol (Q4)

- **Duration:** 8 weeks oral supplementation
- **Context:** Under low calorie diet regimen
- **Specific dose:** Not stated in abstract/available content -- only available in full text
- **Wash-out period included:** T2 timepoint with sustained effects

## BDNF Biomarker Findings (Q1)

- Pro-BDNF serum levels increased significantly after supplementation
- Mature BDNF levels did not change significantly
- The pro-BDNF/BDNF ratio shifted, suggesting altered neurotrophin processing
- Two different ELISA kits were used to discriminate pro-BDNF from mature BDNF

## Limitations

- **Paywall restricted:** Full text not available; dose, extract specification, and detailed results are inaccessible
- Abstract does not specify the H. erinaceus dose, extract type (aqueous vs ethanol), or standardization (e.g., erinacine/hericenone content)
- Population restricted to overweight/obese individuals with mood/sleep disorders -- generalizability uncertain
- Low calorie diet was a confounding factor (mood improvements could partly reflect diet effects)
- No specific p-values reported for individual outcomes in abstract, only thresholds (p<0.05, p<0.01)
