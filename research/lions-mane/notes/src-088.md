# src-088: Prevention of Early Alzheimer's Disease by Erinacine A-Enriched Hericium erinaceus Mycelia

**Citation:** Li I-C, Chang H-H, Lin C-H, et al. (2020). Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience, 12:155. doi: 10.3389/fnagi.2020.00155
**Trial Registration:** NCT04065061
**Study Type:** Pilot double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial

## Core Findings

This 49-week pilot RCT found that daily supplementation with three 350 mg erinacine A-enriched H. erinaceus (EAHE) mycelia capsules (5 mg/g erinacine A) improved MMSE scores (p=0.035 within-group) and showed significantly better IADL scores compared to placebo at week 49 (p=0.012 between-group) in patients with mild Alzheimer's disease. DTI neuroimaging suggested reduced structural deterioration in key brain regions (parahippocampal cingulum and arcuate fasciculus). The treatment was safe with only mild adverse events (abdominal discomfort, nausea, skin rash) in 8.2% of participants.

## Detailed Study Parameters

### Sample Size and Enrollment
- 68 participants initially recruited with mild AD diagnosis
- 49 randomized (19 declined: 5 personal reasons, 14 non-compliance)
- 41 completed the study (20 EAHE, 21 placebo)
- 7 withdrew consent + 1 lost to follow-up
- Completers: 17 male, 24 female
- Mean age: EAHE 74.3 +/- 7.15; Placebo 77.05 +/- 8.2 (p=0.261)
- Education: EAHE 6.35 +/- 4.74 yrs; Placebo 6.0 +/- 5.36 yrs (p=0.826)

### Dosing Protocol
- 3 capsules/day, each containing 350 mg EAHE mycelia
- Standardized to 5 mg/g erinacine A active ingredient
- Total daily dose: ~1050 mg EAHE mycelia (approximately 5.25 mg erinacine A)
- Placebo: identical-appearing capsules
- Taken with meals
- Duration: 49 weeks double-blind treatment (preceded by 3-week screening)

### Cognitive Outcome Measures and Results
| Test | EAHE Baseline | EAHE Wk 49 | Placebo Baseline | Placebo Wk 49 | Key p-value |
|------|---------------|-------------|-------------------|---------------|-------------|
| NPI  | 4.21 +/- 6.62 | 0.67 +/- 1.15 | 3.0 +/- 5.2 | 2.25 +/- 3.3 | 0.129 (intergroup) |
| CASI | 71.75 +/- 17.12 | 75.35 +/- 15.86 | 73.52 +/- 14.51 | 69.67 +/- 16.62 | 0.058 (EAHE intra); 0.064 (placebo intra) |
| MMSE | 21.75 +/- 6.1 | 23.2 +/- 5.92 | 21.33 +/- 5.74 | 20.67 +/- 6.17 | **0.035 (EAHE intra)** |
| IADL | 6.35 +/- 2.81 | 6.7 +/- 2.47 | 5.71 +/- 2.65 | 5.0 +/- 2.7 | **0.012 (intergroup)** |

- Only MMSE (intra-group) and IADL (inter-group) reached statistical significance
- CASI showed trends but did not reach significance
- No MMSE inter-group significance (p=0.261) -- important caveat

### Additional Outcomes
- **Contrast sensitivity**: CS OS significantly different between groups at week 49 (p=0.046); CS OU significantly decreased in placebo (p=0.033)
- **Blood biomarkers**: EAHE group showed significantly improved homocysteine (p=0.007 at wk25, p=0.012 at wk49); placebo showed significant declines in calcium, albumin, hemoglobin, and BDNF
- **DTI neuroimaging**: EAHE group had decreased ADC in PHC dominant hemisphere (p=0.03, indicating better neural organization); placebo group had increased ADC in ARC dominant hemisphere (p=0.033, indicating greater disorganization)

## Safety Outcomes (Q4)

- Total dropout from adverse events: 4 subjects
  - EAHE group (3): abdominal discomfort, nausea, skin rash
  - Placebo group (1): nausea
- Additional 4 withdrew for unsatisfactory efficacy (2 EAHE, 1 placebo) or lost to follow-up (1)
- Overall adverse event incidence: 8.2% over 49 weeks
- Withdrawal rate: 14.3% (7/49)
- Independent safety monitoring committee reviewed adverse events
- Animal toxicity studies (genotoxicity, acute, 28-day, 90-day subchronic, teratotoxicity) showed no adverse effects
- No adverse events reported in Taiwanese market post-launch since 2015

## Limitations (Q5)

1. **Small sample size**: Only 41 completers from 68 recruited -- pilot study design not powered for definitive efficacy conclusions
2. **Sample size justification**: Based on simulation study (Teare et al., 2014) for pilot feasibility, not formal power analysis for efficacy endpoints
3. **No multiplicity correction**: Multiple cognitive and biomarker comparisons without adjustment for multiple testing
4. **Most cognitive outcomes non-significant**: Only MMSE (intra-group) and IADL (inter-group) reached p<0.05; CASI and NPI did not; MMSE inter-group was p=0.261
5. **High attrition**: 39% of enrolled participants did not complete (27 of 68)
6. **Baseline imbalances in ophthalmic measures**: Significant baseline differences in BCVA between groups (OD, OS, OU) -- potential confound
7. **Short duration for AD**: 49 weeks may be insufficient to capture long-term disease trajectory
8. **Industry funding and authorship**: Grape King Bio Ltd. (the manufacturer) employed 5 of the 10 authors

## Claims to Verify

1. **Sample size justification**: The paper cites "at least 60 people were recruited based on a simulation study" (line 316) but 68 were recruited and only 41 completed. The discrepancy between planned and actual sample should be noted.
2. **IADL inter-group p=0.012 at week 49**: This is the only cognitive measure with significant between-group difference. Worth verifying this holds under different statistical approaches.
3. **ADC neuroimaging findings**: The PHC and ARC findings are within-group only -- no between-group comparisons reached significance for DTI measures (intergroup p=0.105 for D.ARC.ADC, p=0.80 for D.PHC.ADC).

## Relevance to Research Questions

- **Q1 (dosing, outcomes, effect sizes)**: Highly relevant -- provides detailed dosing protocol (3 x 350 mg EAHE capsules/day, 5 mg/g erinacine A, 49 weeks), cognitive outcomes with exact scores and p-values, and biomarker/neuroimaging data. Effect sizes are modest and mostly non-significant between groups.
- **Q4 (safety, adverse events)**: Directly relevant -- reports specific adverse events, incidence rates (8.2%), and notes no serious adverse events.
- **Q5 (limitations, funding, COI)**: Directly relevant -- clearly states industry funding and employment relationships, acknowledges small sample size, and notes need for larger trials.
