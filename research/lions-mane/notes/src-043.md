## Core Findings

An ERGO (ergothioneine)-rich Hericium erinaceus (lion's mane) primordium extract (He2) prevented cognitive decline in a murine aging model. The extract demonstrated a selective, preventive neuroprotective effect on the aged hippocampus, preserving both "Knowledge" and "Remember" components of recognition memory while simultaneously reducing key inflammatory markers (IL6, TGFbeta1, GFAP), oxidative stress markers (Nrf2, SOD1, COX2, NOS2), and enhancing glutamatergic neurotransmission markers (NMDAR1, mGluR2).

## Key Evidence

### Behavioral/Cognitive Results
- He2 supplementation preserved recognition memory during aging in mice
- Behavioral tests used: Emergence test, Novel Object Recognition (NOR), Object Location (OL), Y-maze
- Frailty Index (FI) was used as an aging monitoring tool; supplemented mice showed lower FI scores
- Statistical significance: p < 0.05 to p < 0.001 for multiple measures

### Hippocampal Structural Preservation
- H&E staining showed well-preserved hippocampal cytoarchitecture in supplemented aged mice
- Reduced shrunken cell density in DG and CA1 regions (p < 0.01 to p < 0.001)
- Choroid plexus structural alterations (cilia reduction) were observed in non-supplemented controls

### Anti-Inflammatory Effects
- IL6 immunostaining: markedly reduced in supplemented mice vs. strong labeling in controls (both DG and CA1)
- TGFbeta1: strong immunoreactivity in DG of controls, sporadic in supplemented; absent in CA1 of both groups
- GFAP: widespread in both groups but thickened/intense soma in controls vs. reduced in supplemented

### Anti-Oxidative Stress Effects
- Nrf2: heaviest immunopositivity in controls (DG and CA1), rarely detectable in supplemented
- SOD1: heavily immunopositive cells in controls (DG), few in supplemented; absent in CA1 of both
- COX2: strong in DG of both groups; strong in CA1 of supplemented but absent in controls
- NOS2: scarce in DG of both; strong in CA1 of controls, absent in supplemented

### Glutamatergic Neurotransmission Enhancement
- NMDAR1: sporadic in controls; marked immunopositivity in supplemented (DG and CA1), with well-ordered cell chains in GL and immunoreactive neurons in SGZ and PL
- mGluR2: weak/sporadic in controls; heavy immunopositivity in supplemented (DG and CA1)

## Methodology

- **Model:** Murine model of aging
- **Extract:** ERGO-rich Hericium erinaceus primordium extract (He2)
- **Key compound:** Ergothioneine (ERGO), characterized as "the longevity vitamin"
- **Behavioral tests:** Emergence test, NOR, OL, Y-maze (spontaneous behavioral tests)
- **Histological methods:** H&E staining, immunohistochemistry
- **Brain regions analyzed:** Dentate gyrus (DG) and CA1 region of hippocampus
- **Statistical analysis:** Unpaired Student's t-test
