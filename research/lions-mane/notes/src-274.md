# src-274: Absolute Bioavailability, Tissue Distribution, and Excretion of Erinacine S

**Citation:** Hu JH, Li IC, Lin TW, Chen WP, Lee LY, Chen CC, Kuo CF. Molecules. 2019 Apr 24;24(8):1624. doi: 10.3390/molecules24081624. PMCID: PMC6514545.

**Type:** Peer-reviewed original research (open access, MDPI Molecules)

**Quality Assessment:** Full paper text available; substantive methods, results, discussion. Content quality is good.

---

## Core Findings

This is the first pharmacokinetic study of Erinacine S (a sesterterpene found only in H. erinaceus mycelia) in rats. The absolute oral bioavailability was 15.13%, and critically, Erinacine S was shown to cross the blood-brain barrier, reaching a maximum brain concentration of 19.238 ug/g at 8 hours post-dose. Nearly all administered Erinacine S (~99.9%) was absorbed or metabolized, with only 0.1% excreted unchanged in feces and urine combined over 24 hours.

## Key Evidence and Data Points

### Pharmacokinetic Parameters (n=6 per group, 8-week-old male Sprague-Dawley rats)

| Parameter | Oral (mycelia extract) | Intravenous (pure Erinacine S) |
|---|---|---|
| Dose | 2.395 g/kg extract (= 50 mg/kg Erinacine S) | 5 mg/kg Erinacine S |
| Cmax | 0.73 +/- 0.31 ug/mL | 1.64 +/- 0.17 ug/mL |
| Tmax | 270 +/- 73.48 min | - |
| T1/2 | 439.84 +/- 60.98 min | 11.45 +/- 5.76 min |
| AUC | 272.06 +/- 77.82 min*ug/mL | 179.77 +/- 66.46 min*ug/mL |
| **Absolute Bioavailability** | **15.13%** | - |

### Brain Penetration (BBB Crossing)
- Detectable at 0.5 h: 2.069 +/- 0.503 ug/g
- Peak at 2 h: 11.294 +/- 9.662 ug/g
- Maximum at 8 h: 19.238 +/- 14.239 ug/g
- First study ever to demonstrate Erinacine S crosses the BBB

### Tissue Distribution (at 2 h, ranked by concentration)
1. Stomach: 309.672 +/- 175.731 ug/g (highest at 2 h)
2. Small intestine: 72.483 +/- 57.190 ug/g
3. Liver: 24.309 +/- 22.544 ug/g
4. Lung: 14.549 +/- 3.564 ug/g
5. Kidney: 11.800 +/- 5.061 ug/g
6. Brain: 11.294 +/- 9.662 ug/g
7. Heart: 9.816 +/- 7.500 ug/g
8. Large intestine: 7.784 +/- 5.057 ug/g

Most tissues reached maximum concentrations at 8 h (not 2 h), except the stomach.

### Excretion (24 h post-dose)
- Fecal excretion (unchanged): 0.094 +/- 0.053% of dose
- Urinary excretion (unchanged): 0.004 +/- 0.002% of dose
- Total unchanged excreted: ~0.1%
- Implication: ~99.9% absorbed or metabolized

### Formulation Effect (Critical for Translation)
- Pure Erinacine S orally: bioavailability only 1.3%, undetectable in plasma after 240 min
- Mycelia extract orally: bioavailability 15.13% (11.6x improvement)
- Explanation: co-compounds in mycelia extract may protect Erinacine S from gastric acid degradation

## Methodology

- **Study design:** Non-compartmental pharmacokinetic analysis in 8-week-old male Sprague-Dawley rats
- **Analytical method:** LC-MS/MS with MRM detection (m/z 429.3 -> 411.3), validated 5-500 ng/mL linear range
- **PK study:** n=6 per group; blood collected at 15 time points (oral) and 14 time points (IV) over 1440 min
- **Distribution study:** n=3 per time point at 8 time points (0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 24 h)
- **Excretion study:** n=6, feces and urine collected at 4 intervals over 24 h
- **Data analysis:** WinNonlin non-compartmental model
- **Conflict of interest:** Authors affiliated with Grape King Bio Ltd. (industry); declared no conflict of interest

## Limitations

1. **Single dose level only** -- no dose-proportionality data; only one oral dose (50 mg/kg Erinacine S) was tested
2. **Male rats only** -- no female animals included, limiting generalizability
3. **High variability** -- large SD values in tissue distribution (e.g., brain 8h: 19.238 +/- 14.239 ug/g; CV ~74%)
4. **Short excretion window** -- only 24 h collection; authors acknowledge more unchanged Erinacine S could be detected after 24 h
5. **No metabolite identification** -- 99.9% is absorbed or metabolized but metabolites were not characterized
6. **Industry affiliation** -- several authors employed by Grape King Bio Ltd., which produces H. erinaceus products
7. **Enterohepatic circulation inferred, not confirmed** -- double-peak phenomenon observed but biliary recirculation not directly measured
8. **No dose-normalized brain:plasma ratio** reported, making it hard to assess BBB penetration efficiency quantitatively

## Relevance to Research Questions

### Q3 (Bioavailability/Translation Gap)
This is a **directly relevant primary source** for Erinacine S pharmacokinetics. Key findings:
- Absolute bioavailability is modest at 15.13% from mycelia extract (but only 1.3% from pure compound)
- Brain penetration is confirmed -- Erinacine S does cross the BBB
- The formulation effect (mycelia extract vs pure compound yielding 11.6x better bioavailability) has major implications for supplement formulation
- The long half-life (439 min oral, ~7.3 h) suggests once-daily dosing could maintain therapeutic levels
- Brain concentration reaches 19.2 ug/g at 8h, which is substantial relative to other tissues

### Q2 (Mechanistic Implications)
- Erinacine S mechanisms include inhibition of glial cells and enhancement of insulin degrading enzyme expression (cited from prior work)
- The hydrophobic/insoluble nature of Erinacine S may facilitate cell membrane penetration
- Enterohepatic circulation suggests prolonged systemic exposure

## Claims to Verify

1. **Brain concentration values** have very high variability (CV 74% at 8 h). The mean of 19.238 ug/g may not be a reliable central estimate with n=3 and such wide SD.
2. **The 15.13% bioavailability** uses different doses for oral (50 mg/kg) vs IV (5 mg/kg), which is standard but relies on linear pharmacokinetics -- not verified here since only one dose was tested per route.
3. **The 1.3% pure compound bioavailability** is mentioned in the Discussion as preliminary data; the Methods section does not describe this experiment. This number may come from an unreported pilot study.
