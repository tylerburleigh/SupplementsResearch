CLINICAL USES

Supports Brain Health & Cognitive Function

Lion’s Mane (Hericium erinaceus) is renowned for its ability to support brain health by stimulating the production of nerve growth factor (NGF). NGF is essential for the growth, maintenance, and survival of neurons, helping to improve cognitive function, memory, and focus.

Promotes Nerve Regeneration

Lion’s Mane has been shown to support nerve regeneration, encouraging the repair of damaged nerves and promoting overall nervous system health. This is particularly beneficial for individuals dealing with neurological symptoms from chronic illnesses like Lyme disease.

Reduces Brain Fog & Mental Fatigue

By supporting neurogenesis and improving communication between neurons, Lion’s Mane can help reduce symptoms of brain fog and mental fatigue. It enhances mental clarity, focus, and cognitive endurance, making it valuable for those struggling with cognitive dysfunction.

Supports Immune Function

Lion’s Mane has been found to enhance immune function by stimulating the production of immune cells, particularly those involved in the body’s defense against pathogens. Its immune-boosting properties help protect the body from infections, making it useful for individuals with Lyme disease and co-infections.

Improves Mood & Reduces Anxiety

Lion’s Mane has natural mood-balancing properties and has been shown to help reduce anxiety and depression. By promoting the growth of new neurons and enhancing brain function, it can help individuals better manage emotional stress, which is often exacerbated in chronic illness.

Supports Gut-Brain Axis Health

Lion’s Mane plays a key role in supporting the gut-brain axis, the connection between the gastrointestinal system and the brain. A healthy gut microbiome can have a profound effect on mental health, and Lion’s Mane helps promote a balanced gut environment, which in turn supports cognitive and emotional well-being.

Anti-Inflammatory & Antioxidant Effects

Lion’s Mane possesses anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, which help protect the brain and nervous system from oxidative damage. This is especially important for individuals with Lyme disease, as chronic inflammation and oxidative stress often contribute to neurological symptoms.

Promotes Healthy Sleep

Due to its ability to reduce stress and balance mood, Lion’s Mane may also contribute to better sleep quality. By supporting the brain and nervous system’s relaxation responses, it can help individuals with Lyme disease manage sleep disturbances, which are common in chronic illness.

Enhances Neuroplasticity

Lion’s Mane helps to promote neuroplasticity, which is the brain’s ability to reorganize and form new neural connections. This process is essential for recovery from neurological damage and can support cognitive rehabilitation in individuals with Lyme-related neurological symptoms.

Supports Long-Term Brain Health

Regular use of Lion’s Mane has been associated with the potential to protect the brain from neurodegenerative diseases such as Alzheimer's and dementia. Its neuroprotective effects help ensure long-term brain health and may prevent cognitive decline in individuals with chronic conditions.
