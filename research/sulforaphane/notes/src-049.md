## Key Findings

### Validated Target: KEAP1-NRF2 Signaling
- Of many putative cellular targets affected by sulforaphane, **only KEAP1-NRF2 signaling is considered a validated target** at this time.
- NRF2 is described as a "master regulator of cell survival responses to endogenous and exogenous stressors."
- This is a strong authoritative statement from what appears to be a major review.

### Anti-Carcinogenic Efficacy (Preclinical)
- Sulforaphane as a pure chemical protects against chemically-induced carcinogenesis in: skin, oral, stomach, colon, lung, and bladder models.
- Also effective in genetic models of colon and prostate carcinogenesis.
- Critically: "In many of these settings the antitumorigenic efficacy of sulforaphane is dampened in Nrf2-disrupted animals" -- providing causal evidence that NRF2 mediates the chemopreventive effect.

### Clinical Trial Landscape
- "Broccoli preparations rich in glucoraphanin or sulforaphane exert demonstrable pharmacodynamic action in over a score of clinical trials" (i.e., more than 20 clinical trials showing pharmacodynamic effects).
- Measures of NRF2 pathway response are being used as guideposts for optimizing dose, schedule, and formulation.
- Clinical trials with broccoli-based preparations are becoming "more commonplace and more rigorous in design and implementation."
