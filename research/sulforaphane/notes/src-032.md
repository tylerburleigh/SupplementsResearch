# src-032: Study Protocol - SFN as Adjuvant in Bipolar Depressive Disorder

**Citation:** Wu C, Chen X, Lai J, Xu Y, Hu S. The efficacy and safety of sulforaphane as an adjuvant in the treatment of bipolar depressive disorder: study protocol for a randomized, double-blinded, placebo-controlled, parallel-group clinical trial. *Medicine*. 2020;99(26):e20981.

**Quality:** ok -- full protocol text available (not results; this is a study protocol, not a completed trial).

**Registration:** ChiCTR2000028706 (Chinese Clinical Trial Registry)

## Core Findings

This is a study protocol (no results reported) for a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group trial evaluating SFN as an adjuvant to quetiapine for bipolar depressive disorder. The trial was planned for 100 patients over 12 weeks, but recruitment had not yet begun at time of publication (June 2020). The primary outcome is change in MADRS scores; secondary outcomes include anxiety, mania, cognitive function, inflammatory markers, and intestinal flora.

## Study Design Details

- **Design:** Randomized, double-blinded, placebo-controlled, parallel-group clinical trial
- **Population:** 100 patients (50 per arm), ages 16-65, Han Chinese, diagnosed with bipolar depressive disorder (DSM-IV), HAM-D >= 17, YMRS <= 5
- **Duration:** 12 weeks with 5 follow-up visits (baseline, week 2, 4, 8, 12)
- **Randomization:** 1:1 allocation, random number sequence generated by SAS 9.0, sealed envelope concealment by independent third researcher
- **Blinding:** Neither assessors nor subjects know group assignment until study termination; placebo matches Avmacol in size, shape, taste, and packaging

## Key Protocol Elements

### Dosing
- **SFN group:** 1 tablet Avmacol Extra Strength (480 mg) daily -- contains Proprietary Sulforaphane Glucosinolate (Glucoraphanin) & Myrosinase Blend 490 mg, including Broccoli Seed & Sprout Extract with >30 mg glucoraphanin. Swallowed with warm water (<=40 degrees) after breakfast or chewed at same meal.
- **Placebo group:** Identical-appearing placebo, same administration
- **Concomitant medication:** Both groups receive quetiapine starting at 50 mg/day, titrated to 300 mg/day within 2 weeks
- **Product manufacturer:** Nutramax Laboratories Consumer Care (Lancaster, SC, USA)

### Primary Outcome
- Change in MADRS (Montgomery-Asberg Depression Rating Scale) scores from baseline to each time point
- Response defined as >=50% reduction from baseline

### Secondary Outcomes
- Quick Inventory of Depressive Symptomatology -- Self Report (QIDS-SR)
- Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAMA)
- Young Mania Rating Scale (YMRS)
- Cognitive function (THINC-integrated tool)
- Inflammatory factors: IL-2, IL-4, IL-6, IL-10, IL-17A, TNF, IFN-gamma
- Intestinal flora

### Safety Monitoring
- Vital signs, BMI, liver/kidney function, serum electrolytes, blood glucose/lipids, blood cell counts, ECGs
- Adverse events recorded at each visit with severity grading (mild, moderate, severe, serious)
- Trial stopped in case of treatment-related suspected unexpected serious AEs
- Exclusion of patients allergic to broccoli, pregnant/lactating, or with suicide risk (MADRS item 10 >= 3)

### Sample Size Justification
- Estimated response rate of quetiapine alone: 58.9% (from prior 8-week study)
- Estimated response rate of SFN + quetiapine: ~70%
- 80% power, alpha 0.05, anticipated 10% dropout = 100 patients total

### Statistical Analysis
- Intention-to-treat principle
- t-test for continuous variables, chi-square for categorical, Wilcoxon Mann-Whitney as non-parametric alternative
- Missing values handled per Jakobsen et al. guidelines
- SPSS 19.0

## Methodology Notes

- This is a protocol-only paper -- no results are reported. Recruitment had not started at time of submission.
- The study explicitly notes the dose is "conventional" and may not be optimal; further dose-finding studies are acknowledged as needed.
- Inflammatory marker panel is broad (7 cytokines) and intestinal flora analysis adds mechanistic depth.
- Restricted to Han Chinese population, limiting generalizability.

## Limitations

- Protocol only -- no results available. Cannot assess efficacy or actual safety outcomes.
- Han Chinese population only -- limits generalizability.
- Sample size estimate based on assumed effect sizes, not prior SFN-specific data.
- Single dose level tested; authors acknowledge this may not be optimal.

## Claims to Verify

- "Avmacol Extra Strength (480 mg)" contains "Proprietary Sulforaphane Glucosinolate (Glucoraphanin) & Myrosinase Blend 490 mg" -- note the tablet is listed as 480 mg but the blend is 490 mg. This discrepancy should be verified against the actual product label.
- Sample size calculation assumes 70% response rate for combination therapy; this is an estimate not based on prior SFN data.
- The quetiapine response rate of 58.9% and remission rate of 42.1% are cited from Jeong et al. 2013 (ref 28), an open-label observational study -- not an RCT, which may inflate estimates.
