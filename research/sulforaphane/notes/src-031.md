## Study Design

- **Type:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (MeSH confirms "Randomized Controlled Trial" and "Double-Blind Method")
- **Population:** 66 patients with prior cardiac intervention (PCI or CABG) and current mild-to-moderate depression
- **Arms:** Sulforaphane (n=33) vs. placebo (n=33)
- **Duration:** 6 weeks
- **Primary outcome:** Hamilton Rating Scale for Depression (HAM-D) measured at baseline, week 2, week 4, week 6
- **Location:** Iran (Tehran University of Medical Sciences)

## Key Findings

### HAM-D Score Changes
- Repeated measures analysis: sulforaphane group showed greater improvement in HAM-D scores throughout the trial (P < 0.001)
- Exact mean scores at each time point NOT reported in abstract (full text needed)

### Response Rate (>=50% reduction in HAM-D)
- Sulforaphane: 30% (9/30)
- Placebo: 6.67% (2/30)
- P = 0.042

### Remission Rate (HAM-D <= 7)
- Sulforaphane: 23.33% (7/30)
- Placebo: 3.33% (1/30)
- P = 0.052 (not statistically significant)

### Completers
- 60 of 66 enrolled completed the trial (n=30 per group; 6 dropouts total, ~9% attrition)
