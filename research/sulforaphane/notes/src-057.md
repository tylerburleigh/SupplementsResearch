# src-057: Sulforaphane as Adjuvant to Risperidone for ASD Irritability

**Source quality:** Degraded (paywall). Only abstract available from PubMed landing page. No full text accessible.
**Citation:** Psychiatry and Clinical Neurosciences, 2020. Japanese Society of Psychiatry and Neurology. PMID: 32347624.
**Study type:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (RCT)
**Funding:** Tehran University of Medical Sciences (37048)

## Core Findings

Sulforaphane as an adjuvant to risperidone significantly improved irritability (P=0.001) and hyperactivity/noncompliance (P=0.015) in children with ASD aged 4-12 years over 10 weeks, compared to risperidone plus placebo. The study found significant Time x Treatment interaction effects for both Irritability (P=0.007) and Hyperactivity/Noncompliance (P=0.008). No difference in adverse event frequency was observed between groups.

## Study Design

- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
- **Population:** 60 drug-free children with ASD, aged 4-12 years
- **Arms:** Risperidone + sulforaphane (n=30) vs. Risperidone + placebo (n=30)
- **Duration:** 10 weeks
- **Assessments:** Aberrant Behavior Checklist - Community Edition (ABC-C) at baseline, Week 5, and Week 10
- **Primary outcome:** ABC Irritability subscale score
- **Secondary outcomes:** ABC Hyperactivity/Noncompliance, Lethargy/Social Interaction, Stereotypic Behavior, Inappropriate Speech

## Dosing Details

- **Risperidone:** Started at 0.25 mg/day (<20 kg) or 0.5 mg/day (>=20 kg), titrated stepwise to max 1 mg (<20 kg), 2.5 mg (20-45 kg), 3.5 mg (>45 kg)
- **Sulforaphane:** 50 umol/day (<=45 kg) or 100 umol/day (>45 kg)

## Key Results

- Irritability score: Greater improvement in sulforaphane group (P=0.001)
- Hyperactivity/Noncompliance score: Greater improvement in sulforaphane group (P=0.015)
- Time x Treatment interaction for Irritability: P=0.007
- Time x Treatment interaction for Hyperactivity/Noncompliance: P=0.008
- No significant differences for: Lethargy/Social Interaction, Stereotypic Behavior, Inappropriate Speech
- Adverse events: No significant difference in frequency between groups

## Safety Data

The abstract states "no difference was seen in improvements in... frequency of adverse events" between the sulforaphane and placebo groups. Specific adverse event types and rates are not reported in the abstract.

## Limitations

- **Abstract only:** Full text behind paywall; no access to detailed methods, effect sizes (Cohen's d), confidence intervals, or complete safety data
- **Sample size:** 60 participants total (30 per arm) -- modest
- **Combination therapy:** Sulforaphane was tested as adjuvant to risperidone, not as monotherapy. Cannot isolate sulforaphane-only effects.
- **Duration:** 10 weeks only; no long-term follow-up data
- **Missing details:** No effect sizes, no baseline severity scores, no responder rates, no detailed adverse event listing

## Claims to Verify

- The exact P-values (0.001, 0.015, 0.007, 0.008) should be confirmed against the full text when available
- The sample of "60 drug-free patients" -- verify whether this is the enrolled or completed sample
- Effect sizes are not reported in the abstract and should be sought from the full text
