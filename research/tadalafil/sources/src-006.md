. (2005) Chronic treatment with tadalafil improves endothelial function in men with increased cardiovascular risk.
Eur Urol

47
: 214–222
Endothelial impairment is an etiological factor common to cardiovascular disease and erectile dysfunction (ED). As the oral phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitor sildenafil (Viagra
®
; Pfizer, Walton-on-the-Hill, UK) has been shown to improve endothelial function in men with ED, Rosano
et al
. tested the hypothesis that its long-acting counterpart tadalafil (Cialis
®
; Lilly, Basingstoke, UK) would have a similar effect in men at increased risk of cardiovascular disease.
