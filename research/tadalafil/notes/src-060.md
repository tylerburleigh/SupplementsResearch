## Study Type and Methodology

- Cross-sectional comparison study with an interventional component (acute tadalafil administration).
- Endothelium functions assessed by **applanation tonometry**.
- Aortic stiffness and cardiac function evaluated by **transthoracic echocardiography**.
- Study conducted at Uludag University, Bursa, Turkey (Departments of Cardiology and Urology).

## Population

- **ED patients:** n=30, mean age 48.7 +/- 11.7 years. Free from cardiovascular diseases or atherosclerotic risk factors.
- **Healthy male controls:** n=20, mean age 48.3 +/- 8.7 years.
- Age groups were well-matched.

## Key Findings

### Baseline differences (ED vs. healthy controls)
- Pulse pressure was **greater** in the ED group (P<0.05).
- Aortic strain and aortic distensibility were **significantly lower** in ED group (P<0.001).
- Large artery and small artery elasticity indices were reduced in the ED group at baseline.

### Acute tadalafil effects (in ED group)
- **Reduced pulse pressure** (P=0.0179).
- **Reduced systolic blood pressure** (P=0.001).
- **Reduced diastolic blood pressure** (borderline, P=0.054).
- **Increased aortic distensibility** (P=0.001).
- **Increased aortic strain** (P=0.003).
- **Increased large artery elasticity index** (P=0.02).
- **Increased small artery elasticity index** (P=0.003).
- **Positively affected left ventricular diastolic function (LVDF).**

### Overall conclusion
Systemic vascular disease and compromised LVDF are present in ED patients even without known atherosclerotic risk factors. Tadalafil positively affects arterial stiffness and LVDF acutely.
