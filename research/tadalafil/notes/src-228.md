# src-228: PDE5 Inhibitors and Vascular Function - Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

---

## Core Findings

This systematic review and meta-analysis of **63 RCTs involving 3,242 subjects** found that PDE5 inhibitors (sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil, udenafil) significantly improved multiple vascular parameters compared to placebo, including reductions in systolic and diastolic blood pressure, carotid intima-media thickness, and pulse wave velocity, as well as increases in flow-mediated dilation, peak systolic velocity, endothelial progenitor cells, and endothelial microparticles. Critically for the research question, the FMD subgroup analysis showed PDE5i significantly improved FMD in CVD patients but **not in healthy individuals or smokers**.

---

## Study Design and Methods

- **Type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis following PRISMA guidelines
- **Registration:** PROSPERO (CRD42023387924)
- **Search:** PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science from 1998 to 2022
- **Inclusion:** RCTs comparing PDE5is vs. placebo on vascular parameters
- **Exclusion:** Non-English, guidelines, reviews, observational studies, head-to-head studies, non-placebo-controlled trials
- **Statistical approach:** Random-effects model, mean differences with 95% CIs, Review Manager 5.4
- **Subgroup analysis:** By disease status
- **Risk of bias:** Cochrane Collaboration tool; funnel plots for publication bias

## Healthy Population Subgroup Findings

The discussion at line 650 states explicitly: "In the subgroup analysis, PDE5i significantly increased FMD in CVD patients but not in smokers or healthy individuals." This is a critical finding for the research question -- while the overall pooled effect on FMD is significant, the benefit does not extend to healthy people.

The clinical significance section (line 665) recommends: "For instance, in healthy subjects, clinicians should monitor changes in vascular indicators such as BP, PWV, and FMD, and consider incorporating PDE5i into the treatment regimen when these indicators deteriorate." This implies the authors view PDE5is as potentially useful for healthy individuals only when vascular parameters become abnormal, not as a prophylactic.

## Safety Profile

- 20 of 63 RCTs reported minor adverse effects; no major adverse effects in any study
- Headache: 11-37.5% incidence
- Flushing: 8.3-25%
- Dizziness: 20.8%
- Dyspepsia: 5.8-16.7%
- Rate of adverse events was NOT significantly different from placebo

The paper does not provide a consolidated dosing table. The included studies used various PDE5is (sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil, udenafil, lodenafil, avanafil) with different dosing regimens. ## Limitations

1. **Significant heterogeneity** in SBP (I2=57%), MAP (I2=64%) analyses; not fully resolved by subgroup analysis
2. **Publication bias** detected for PWV, AI, and EPCs (asymmetrical funnel plots)
3. **No meta-regression** conducted to explore heterogeneity sources
4. **Mixed populations and interventions** prevent direct comparison across PDE5i types and doses
5. **Confounders not fully evaluated** -- most studies did not report confounder-adjusted results
6. **Limited subgroup numbers** -- only 5 studies measured PWV, 2 measured EMP
7. **Cannot compare outcomes across participant groups** (healthy vs. disease) directly

---

## Relevance to Tadalafil for Vascular Health in Healthy Individuals

**Direct relevance:** HIGH -- this is the largest meta-analysis of PDE5is and vascular function, and it specifically includes 14 RCTs in healthy individuals.

**Key takeaway for healthy population:** While PDE5is show overall vascular benefits, the subgroup analysis suggests these benefits are driven primarily by effects in disease populations. The FMD improvement did NOT reach significance in healthy individuals. The BP reductions (SBP -2.80 mmHg, DBP -1.80 mmHg) may still apply to healthy people but are modest and were not analyzed separately by healthy vs. disease status for blood pressure outcomes.

**Tadalafil-specific:** The meta-analysis pooled all PDE5is together; tadalafil-specific effects cannot be isolated from this analysis. Several individual studies used tadalafil (referenced in the reference list: Bocchio 2008, Larios-Cardenas 2022, Santos 2014, Simsek 2014, Foresta 2006, Aversa 2007, Porst 2012, Pattanaik 2019, Rosano 2005, Shenoy 2010, Sabri 2016, La Vignera 2011).
