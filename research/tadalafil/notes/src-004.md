# src-004: Tadalafil treatment had a modest effect on endothelial cell damage and repair ability markers in men with erectile dysfunction and vascular risk

**Citation:** Pelliccione F, D'Angeli A, D'Andrea S, et al. Asian J Androl. 2014;16(2):290-294. DOI: 10.4103/1008-682X.122347. PMCID: PMC3955343.

## Core Findings

Tadalafil 20 mg every other day for 4 weeks improved erectile function (SHIM score, P=0.0028) in men with ED and vascular risk factors, but had only a **modest** effect on laboratory surrogates of endothelial cell health. Endothelin-1 was significantly reduced (P=0.011), and t-PA was reduced (P=0.005), but sE-selectin was unchanged and there was no improvement in endothelial repair capacity (CACs and CFUs). The authors conclude the proposed beneficial effect of PDE5 inhibition on vascular homeostasis requires further analysis.

## Study Design

- **Design:** Randomized, placebo-controlled trial with 4-week treatment period and 4-week run-in (no PDE5i)
- **Population:** 36 men aged 30-70 with ED for >6 months and vascular risk factors (VRFs); 10 healthy controls (aged 38.2 +/- 12.4)
- **Setting:** University of L'Aquila, Italy, Andrology Clinic
- **Dosing:** Tadalafil 20 mg every other day for 4 weeks (14 tablets total) vs placebo
- **Inclusion:** ED with VRFs (diabetes, hypertension, dyslipidemia, smoking)
- **Exclusion:** Hypogonadism, overt vascular disease, coronary artery disease, stroke, pelvic surgery, nitrate therapy, major organ disease

## Key Endpoints and Measures

### Primary: Endothelial repair capacity
- **CACs (circulating angiogenic cells):** MNCs from healthy men cultured with 20% serum from ED patients; tadalafil had NO effect on serum-mediated inhibition of CACs
- **CFUs (colony forming units):** Same assay; tadalafil had NO effect on serum-mediated inhibition of CFUs
- Serum from ED patients inhibited CACs and CFUs from healthy men more than serum from controls (P < 0.017)

### Secondary: Endothelial damage/dysfunction markers
| Marker | Baseline vs Controls | Effect of Tadalafil |
|--------|---------------------|-------------------|
| ET-1 | Elevated in ED pts | Reduced (F=7.26, P=0.011), restored to control levels |
| sE-selectin | Elevated in ED pts | No change |
| t-PA | Not different from controls | Reduced (F=8.99, P=0.005) -- uncertain clinical significance |

### Erectile function
- SHIM score improved with tadalafil (F=10.38, P=0.0028)
- Baseline: tadalafil 9.11 +/- 6.28, placebo 10.15 +/- 6.88
- Post-treatment: tadalafil 14.00 +/- 8.13, placebo 8.8 +/- 7.5

## Effect Sizes and Significance

All reported as F-statistics from two-factor ANOVA for repeated measures:
- SHIM: F=10.38, P=0.0028
- ET-1: F=7.26, P=0.011
- t-PA: F=8.99, P=0.005
- sE-selectin: no significant change
- CACs: no significant change
- CFUs: no significant change

Note: Raw values for endothelial markers are in tables not rendered in the markdown (Table 2). Exact baseline and post-treatment values for ET-1, sE-selectin, and t-PA are not available from the text alone.

## Methodological Notes

- Ex vivo assay design: healthy donor MNCs exposed to patient serum, which tests serum-borne factors but not direct tadalafil effect on patient's own progenitor cells
- Non-parametric statistics used appropriately (Kruskal-Wallis, Wilcoxon rank-sum)
- Multiple comparison adjustment applied (alpha adjustment for Wilcoxon tests)
- SAS software used for analysis
- No adverse events requiring discontinuation

## Limitations

1. **Small sample size:** 36 patients total (18 per group assumed, not explicitly stated)
2. **Short duration:** Only 4 weeks of treatment
3. **Surrogate endpoints:** Laboratory markers only, no clinical vascular outcomes
4. **Mixed results across markers:** Only 2 of 5 endpoints showed significant change; inconsistency suggests weak overall effect
5. **Ex vivo assay limitation:** Using healthy donor cells with patient serum does not capture direct PDE5i effects on patient endothelial progenitor cells
6. **t-PA finding questionable:** t-PA was not elevated at baseline vs controls, so reduction after treatment may not be clinically meaningful

## Relevance to Healthy Populations

This study has **low direct relevance** to healthy populations:
- All participants had ED plus vascular risk factors (diabetes, hypertension, dyslipidemia, smoking)
- No healthy group received tadalafil
- The ex vivo assay used healthy donor cells, but only as a vehicle for testing patient serum properties
- The modest effects seen in a diseased population suggest even weaker effects would be expected in healthy individuals
- The authors' conclusion that PDE5i has only modest effects on vascular surrogates in a population with active vascular dysfunction argues against meaningful benefit in healthy people
- However, the ET-1 reduction to healthy control levels is notable and suggests partial restoration of endothelial function

## Claims to Verify

1. **Sample size of 36:** Stated in abstract and methods. Appears to be total across both arms. Not broken down per arm in available text (Table 1 not rendered).
2. **F-statistics:** All reported with P-values. Cannot independently verify without raw data, but the values are internally consistent with the stated P-values.
3. **CAC/CFU inhibition percentages for controls:** Stated as 83.0% +/- 34.5% for CACs and 63.8% +/- 18.3% for CFUs -- these are relative to FBS, showing even control serum is somewhat inhibitory.
