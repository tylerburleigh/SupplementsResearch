# src-405: Established and Emerging Therapeutic Uses of PDE Type 5 Inhibitors in Cardiovascular Disease

**Citation:** Tzoumas N, Farrah TE, Dhaun N, Webb DJ. Br J Pharmacol. 2020;177(24):5467-5488. doi:10.1111/bph.14920. PMCID: PMC7707100.

**Type:** Narrative literature review (150 articles included in qualitative synthesis). Literature search of Medline, Embase, Cochrane Library, Google Scholar, and Trip medical database (Dec 2016 - Sep 2019).

**Quality:** High-quality peer-reviewed review in a leading pharmacology journal. Comprehensive treatment of the topic with detailed clinical trial evidence tables.

---

## Core Findings

This review comprehensively covers the established (ED, PAH) and emerging (hypertension, cardioprotection, CKD, diabetes mellitus) therapeutic uses of PDE5 inhibitors. The authors argue that PDE5Is have demonstrated potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, anti-proliferative, and metabolic properties beyond their acute haemodynamic effects, largely attributable to modulation of the NO-cGMP signaling pathway. The recent association of long-term PDE5I treatment with reduced all-cause mortality in diabetic patients is highlighted as meriting broader evaluation.

---

## Key Evidence and Data Points

### Blood Pressure and Hypertension
- Sildenafil monotherapy 16 days reduced ambulatory BP by ~6 mmHg in untreated hypertension (Oliver et al., 2006)
- PF-00489791 (long-acting PDE5I) reduced ambulatory BP by ~6 mmHg in hypertensive patients over 28 days (Wolk et al., 2009)
- Sildenafil + isosorbide mononitrate combination produced additional BP reduction in resistant hypertension (Oliver et al., 2010)
- Acute sildenafil reduced mean BP and improved diastolic dysfunction in resistant hypertension (Quinaglia et al., 2013)
- PDE5Is do not have synergistic hypotensive effects with ACEIs, ARBs, beta-blockers, CCBs, or thiazide diuretics

### Mortality and Cardiovascular Outcomes
- In 5,956 men with T2DM followed for median 7 years, PDE5I use associated with 31% lower all-cause mortality, significant after multivariable adjustment (Anderson et al., 2016)
- Significant reductions in age-adjusted incidence and mortality rates of acute MI with PDE5I history
- Cochrane meta-analysis of 36 studies (2,999 patients) showed PDE5Is improved WHO functional class and 6MWD in PAH, with mortality benefit

### Endothelial Function
- Both single-use and long-term PDE5I administration ameliorates endothelial function in high cardiovascular risk patients
- Improvements may be sustained weeks to months after discontinuation, suggesting cellular-level modifications
- Sildenafil may provide sustained pharmacological preconditioning of human endothelium against ischemia-reperfusion injury

### Cardioprotection (Mechanistic)
- NO-cGMP pathway acts as survival signal in ischemic heart disease
- Cardioprotection via PKG-dependent phosphorylation of ERK and GSK3B, increased NOS2/3 activity, PKC activation, and mitoKATP opening
- Effects are independent of vasoactive properties -- direct myocardial anti-inflammatory and anti-apoptotic processes
- Preclinical studies show PDE5Is reduce infarct size regardless of timing (preconditioning or postconditioning)
- In HFpEF, beneficial effects are offset by reduced cardiac contractility; PDE5I use likely limited to HFrEF and HFpEF with associated PH

### Metabolic Effects
- Daily PDE5I for 1-4 months reduced HbA1c by 0.3-0.59% from baseline
- Comparable to established oral antidiabetic agents given for longer periods
- Sildenafil improved insulin sensitivity and glucose uptake in high-fat diet mice (12 weeks)
- RCTs showed improvement in oral disposition index (beta-cell function) in metabolic syndrome patients

### Chronic Kidney Disease
- Sildenafil reduced microalbuminuria in diabetic nephropathy (Grover-Paez et al., 2007)
- PF-00489791 produced significant 16% reduction in UACR in treatment-resistant macroalbuminuria, BP-independent (Scheele et al., 2016)
- UACR reduction was reversible after treatment discontinuation

## Discussion of Use in Healthy Populations or Primary Prevention

The review does NOT discuss PDE5I use in healthy populations for primary prevention. All clinical trial evidence is in diseased populations. However, the review notes:
- "Long-term evidence has since established the safety of PDE5Is in both health and CVD" (line 549)
- Early Phase I trials included healthy volunteers, demonstrating BP reductions in healthy subjects
- The safety profile discussion focuses on adverse effects in therapeutic use, not preventive use in healthy individuals
- The authors conclude by calling for "larger RCTs" to establish "optimal use and long-term safety of these agents in CVD" -- implying therapeutic rather than preventive context

---

## Safety and Risk Profile

### Well-Established Safety
- All PDE5Is share similar transient, mild side effects: headache (7-22%), facial flushing (3-23%), dyspepsia (2-22%), nasal congestion
- Tadalafil uniquely associated with myalgia (7%) and back pain (9%)
- Sildenafil/vardenafil cross-react with PDE6 causing transient blue/green color vision disturbances
- QT interval changes at higher doses are clinically insignificant
- PDE5Is may exert antiarrhythmic effects on ischemic myocardium
- No synergistic BP effects with most antihypertensive classes

### Key Contraindications and Risks
- Absolute contraindication: concurrent organic nitrates and sGC stimulators (dangerous hypotension)
- Contraindicated in patients with prior NAION or hereditary eye disease
- NAION risk doubled within 5 half-lives of PDE5I use (~3 additional cases/100,000 men 50+ with weekly use)
- STRIDER trial: sildenafil in pregnancy showed increased PPHN (27% vs 5%) and neonatal mortality in Dutch arm
- Caution with CYP3A4 inhibitors (dose reduction recommended)

### Pharmacology of Tadalafil Specifically
- Half-life: 17.5 hours (longest among marketed PDE5Is; duration up to 36 hours)
- Tmax: 2 hours (30-120 min range)
- Unaffected by dietary intake, enabling once-daily dosing
- IC50 for PDE5: 1.8 nmol/L (most potent among marketed agents)
- Cross-reactivity: low activity against PDE11, very low against PDE6
- Metabolized by CYP3A4; clearance not significantly affected by mild/moderate hepatic impairment (unlike sildenafil/vardenafil)

## Mechanistic Rationale for Cardiovascular Benefits

1. **Vasodilation:** PDE5 inhibition prevents cGMP degradation, prolonging NO-mediated vasodilation in vascular smooth muscle
2. **Endothelial function:** PDE5Is reduce ET-1 and inflammatory mediators, increase endothelial progenitor cells
3. **Cardioprotection:** Direct myocardial effects via cGMP-PKG pathway -- anti-inflammatory, anti-apoptotic, preconditioning/postconditioning
4. **Anti-proliferative:** Suppresses vascular smooth muscle cell proliferation (demonstrated in renal ablation model)
5. **Metabolic modulation:** Improves insulin sensitivity via enhanced NOS3 activity; promotes glucose uptake in muscle and adipose tissue
6. **Anti-platelet:** Modest anti-platelet effects demonstrated with intravenous sildenafil

---

## Limitations Noted in Review

- Most cardioprotective evidence is preclinical (animal models); clinical translation uncertain
- PDE5I use in HFpEF without PH limited by reduced cardiac contractility
- PDE5I trials in ischemic stroke have been negative or terminated (futility, recruitment failure)
- No definitive evidence for PDE5I efficacy in PH Groups 2-5
- Evidence for PDE5I in CKD and diabetes is promising but from limited trials
- Observational mortality data subject to confounding (cannot adjust for compliance, doctor selection, socioeconomic position)
- STRIDER trial raised safety concerns about PDE5I use in pregnancy

## Claims to Verify

- **6 mmHg BP reduction** (line 582-584): Cited from Oliver et al. 2006 -- should verify exact figure from primary source. Round number may be approximate.
- **31% mortality reduction** (line 1507): Large observational study; potential confounding by healthy-user bias (men prescribed PDE5Is may be healthier). The authors note inability to adjust for life course socio-economic position, compliance, and doctor selection practices.
- **5,956 men with T2DM** (line 1505): Verify sample size from Anderson et al. 2016 primary source.
- **16% UACR reduction** (line 1486): Verify from Scheele et al. 2016 -- the specificity of this figure and the "BP-independent" claim warrant checking against the primary trial data.
- **HbA1c reduction of 0.3-0.59%** (line 1535): This range comes from two different studies with different designs; should verify each individually.
