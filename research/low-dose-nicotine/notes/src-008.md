## Key Findings

1. **Neuroprotective mechanism:** Nicotine binds nicotinic acetylcholine receptors (especially alpha-7), triggering calcium influx that activates protective protein synthesis pathways, shielding neurons from stress-induced death.
2. **Anti-inflammatory pathway:** Nicotine activates the "cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway" via alpha-7 receptors, reducing pro-inflammatory cytokine production while preserving anti-inflammatory signals.
3. **Parkinson's disease:** Nicotine protects dopaminergic neurons by reducing SIRT6 protein levels. Tobacco users show reduced SIRT6 in brain tissue. Epidemiological data show dose-dependent protective effect of smoking on Parkinson's risk.
4. **Cognitive enhancement:** A Vanderbilt University study showed nicotine improves attention, memory, and cognitive processing in healthy individuals and those with mild cognitive impairment, especially APOE4 carriers.
5. **Therapeutic breadth:** Nicotine patches show promise for depression, ADHD, Tourette's, and schizophrenia.
6. **Safety claim:** Pure nicotine does not contain carcinogens; side effects from transdermal patches are mild (skin irritation, sleep disturbances, nausea).
