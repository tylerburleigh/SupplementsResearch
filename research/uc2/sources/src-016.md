Description

Description

The unique and pioneering formula – Joint Support, developed exclusively by Balkan Pharmaceuticals, includes 12 key ingredients, being robust, highly effective and at the same time affordable;

Contains undenatured type II collagen (UC-II) 40 mg, hydrolysed bovine collagen (type I and III) 5000 mg, glucosamine 1500 mg, chondroitin 1000 mg, MSM 3000 mg, as well as curcumin, Boswellia and hyaluronic acid to maintain healthy joints, relieve joint pain;

Has an anti-inflammatory effect, accelerates joint recovery, rebuilds bones and cartilage making them more resistant, improves stiffness of anatomical joints and restores mobility;

In the Joint Support Ultra Easy Take set, the active substances are separated into a basic preparation and a booster for superior assimilation of nutrients and even greater efficiency of the formula;

Practical ON-THE-GO packaging and booster in capsules;

30 sachets of orange-flavoured oral powder

Benefits

MAINTAINS HEALTHY TISSUE STRUCTURE

Our product contains 1500 mg of glucosamine and 1000 mg of chondroitin designed to ensure healthy and sustainable growth of cartilage tissue. The 3000 mg of MSM is an important source of sulphur and helps repair healthy cartilage, thus ensuring the structural and functional integrity of the joints.

ENSURES BETTER MOBILITY

This revolutionary formula contains UC-II (undenatured type II collagen), CH-I and CH-III (hydrolysed type I and III collagen) and hyaluronic acid, which maintain joint mobility and flexibility, as they are crucial components in the processes of hydration and lubrication of tissues.

INCREASES BONES AND JOINT STRENGTH

This supplement provides you with Calcium and Vitamin D3 which help prevent bone loss and osteoporosis, as well as Vitamin C and Silicon which are essential for collagen formation and bone mineralization, making them flexible and strong enough to withstand stress.

REDUCES PAIN AND INFLAMMATION

Join Support is designed to limit and reduce pain and swelling caused by inflamed and weakened joints. The high dosage of 3000 mg of MSM can help ensure optimal collagen flow to keep your hips and joints in perfect health.

SUPPORTS AN ACTIVE LIFESTYLE

Balkan Pharmaceuticals Joint Supplement provides active people with a high dose of 3000 mg of MSM, 300 mg of curcumin and 200 mg of boswellia, which, according to several studies, have an anti-inflammatory effect, reduce oxidative stress in the body and restore flexibility.

Method of application

As a dietary supplement, take one sachet per day, preferably during meals, with a sufficient amount of water or your favourite beverage, or as directed by your doctor, pharmacist, or nutritionist.
