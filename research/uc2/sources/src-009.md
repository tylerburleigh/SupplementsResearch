Product Description:

Osteoarthritis (OA) is one of the most common joint disorders affecting both humans and animals. It is a chronic, degenerative, and inflammatory condition that primarily affects synovial joints, leading to the gradual breakdown of cartilage, joint stiffness, pain, and reduced mobility. As the disease progresses, the protective cartilage that cushions the joints deteriorates, resulting in discomfort, inflammation, and limitations in normal movement. Managing osteoarthritis often involves a combination of non-pharmacological approaches, lifestyle modifications, and therapeutic agents that aim to reduce symptoms and slow disease progression.

Undenatured Type II Collagen (UC-II) has gained significant attention as a nutraceutical ingredient that may help support joint health in individuals affected by osteoarthritis. UC-II is a specialized form of collagen that retains its natural structure and works through an immune-mediated mechanism known as oral tolerance. By interacting with the immune system in the digestive tract, UC-II may help regulate immune responses associated with joint inflammation and cartilage degradation.

Clinical and experimental studies suggest that supplementation with UC-II may help improve joint comfort, flexibility, and mobility while supporting the maintenance of healthy cartilage. Due to these benefits, UC-II is widely used in joint health supplements designed to support individuals experiencing osteoarthritis and other joint-related conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is UC-II Collagen used for?

UC-II Collagen (Undenatured Type II Collagen) is used as a dietary supplement to support joint health, cartilage protection, and improved mobility. It helps maintain the structural integrity of cartilage and may reduce joint discomfort and stiffness. UC-II works through an immune-mediated mechanism known as oral tolerance, which helps regulate the immune response associated with cartilage breakdown. It is commonly used in supplements intended for joint flexibility, mobility, and overall musculoskeletal health.

What is UC-II Collagen made from?

UC-II Collagen is derived from undenatured type II collagen extracted primarily from chicken sternum cartilage. The manufacturing process preserves the natural triple-helix structure of collagen, which is essential for maintaining its biological activity and effectiveness in supporting joint health.

What is UC-II Collagen trade name?

UC-II® is itself a patented trade name used for undenatured type II collagen. Various nutraceutical products containing this ingredient are marketed under brand names such as NOW UC-II Joint Health, Healthy Origins UC-II, Jarrow Formulas Type II Collagen Complex, and TrueBasics UC-II Joint Support.

What is generic name of UC-II Collagen?
