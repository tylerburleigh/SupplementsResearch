Properties

The Sustaflex
®
dietary supplement contains
undenatured type II collagen (UC-II
®
)
. It is an additional source of collagen, vitamin C, vitamin D
3
, manganese, and copper. In the daily human diet, these components are vital for the restoration of the cartilaginous surface of joints.

A 180-day multicenter clinical study involving 191 patients reliably demonstrated: reduction of pain, decrease in stiffness, improvement of physical function of the knee joint in the group of patients taking UC-II
®
, compared to patients taking a placebo or a combination of glucosamine + chondroitin sulfate. Thus, it was reliably established that UC-II
®
contributed to a reduction in the severity of knee osteoarthritis symptoms and was well tolerated.

Type II collagen is a protein and a component of articular cartilage. Type II collagen can be obtained from various food products. If type II collagen is extracted from chicken sternal cartilage using conventional technology, the initial natural structure of the collagen will change; it becomes denatured and loses its natural properties and quality. Therefore, denatured collagen is useless for the joint. A special patented collagen extraction technology allows preserving its natural properties. The unaltered structure of type II collagen is a very important condition for protecting the healthy state of the joint. Only undenatured type II collagen works in the body because it retains physiological activity.

Undenatured type II collagen (UC-II
®
)
is a patented glycosylated form of collagen derived from chicken sternum cartilage. In studies, it showed active properties at a dose of 40 mg/day. The role of undenatured type II collagen is that it restrains the action of T-killer cells, which, due to a disorder of the immune system, attack the body’s own collagen breakdown cells, promoting inflammation. Oral intake of undenatured collagen reduces autoimmunity to the body’s own type II collagen, which leads to a reduction in inflammation in osteoarthritis and rheumatism and has a positive effect on joint condition. With long-term use, type II collagen supports joint cartilage tissue, ensures their mobility and flexibility, and reduces discomfort. Clinically tested.

Vitamin C
promotes better absorption of collagen.

Vitamin D
3
ensures normal growth and development of bones, regulates mineral metabolism, and promotes calcium deposition in bone tissue.

Manganese
is involved in the synthesis of proteoglycans, which have a beneficial effect on the formation and creation of healthy bones and cartilage.

Copper
promotes the synthesis of collagen and elastin, has anti-inflammatory properties, and is involved in the tissue regeneration process during recovery from injuries and surgeries.
