Undenatured Type II Collagen 40 mg, Sodium Hyaluronate 40 mg, Vitamins and Minerals Tablets

SUMMARY:

Undenatured Type II Collagen is a form of collagen that is minimally processed to maintain its natural structure. It is primarily derived from chicken sternum cartilage and is known for its potential benefits in supporting joint health.

Potential Uses and Benefits:

Joint Health: Undenatured Type II collagen helps maintain and restore joint cartilage. It is particularly beneficial for people with osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis, as it can reduce joint pain, stiffness, and inflammation.

Immune Modulation: It works by modulating the immune system. Instead of suppressing the entire immune response, it helps in retraining the immune system to stop attacking the body’s own cartilage.

Cartilage Repair: Helps in the repair and regeneration of damaged cartilage, thus improving joint flexibility and mobility.

INTRODUCTION:

Treatment of osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis

Support for joint recovery after sports injuries

Maintenance of overall joint health

HOW IT WORKS:

Undenatured Type II collagen works through a process known as oral tolerance. When consumed, small amounts of the collagen enter the digestive tract and are recognized by the immune system. This helps to decrease the autoimmune response against the body’s own cartilage, thus reducing inflammation and joint damage.

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION:

One tablet per day
