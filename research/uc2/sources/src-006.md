What is Undenatured Collagen 2?
Undenatured type II collagen
, also known as UC-II, is a type II collagen extracted from animal cartilage using a patented low-temperature extraction technology that retains the complete triple helix structure and biological activity.UC-II is the type II collagen that has the most structural resemblance to the joint cartilage of humans and animals in nature. Many studies have proved that UC-II plays a very important role in eliminating joint inflammation and improving joint pain and other symptoms.

Type II collagen is another important member of the collagen family. Unlike type I collagen, type II collagen consists of three alpha chains, each containing 1,464 amino acid residues, forming a special helical structure. Type II collagen is mainly found in articular cartilage tissue.

Green Spring Technology supplies non-denatured type II collagen, which is obtained by using chicken cartilage as raw material, and low-temperature extraction technology, which retains the unchanged triple-helix structure of macromolecular collagen intact. Compared to other methods, the extracted collagen retains more of the non-denatured triple helix structure and important bioactive binding regions called epitopes on the collagen triple helix. It is these epitopes that give non-denatured type II collagen its unique mechanism of action, triggering the repair and reconstruction of articular cartilage.

Benefits:
Beneficial for Joint Degeneration
A study in a rabbit model of osteoarthritis highlighted the role of a combined solution of chondroitin sulfate, glucosamine, sodium hyaluronate, and natural type II collagen in maintaining joint health. When natural non-denatured type II collagen was added to the glucosamine solution, articular cartilage degradation was significantly reduced and cartilage appearance and structure improved. The joint condition of the natural non-denatured type II collagen experimental group was also closer to that of healthy joints compared to the control group.

May Improve Knee Discomfort
In a randomized controlled study exploring the efficacy of natural non-denatured type II collagen in patients with osteoarthritis of the knee, a three-month intervention (with paracetamol) significantly improved pain compared to a paracetamol-only group. The results showed that the group containing natural non-denatured type II collagen performed better than the control group without the product.

Beneficial for Osteoarthritis
A retrospective observational study of patients with osteoarthritis found that chondroitin sulfate and glucosamine had beneficial effects in reducing cartilage degradation and osteoarthritis progression. The addition of natural non-denatured type II collagen produced better results than the control group using chondroitin sulfate and glucosamine alone.

Improves Mobility
Non-denatured type II collagen is
a dietary supplement ingredient
that has been shown in studies to support bone and joint health by relieving symptoms of stiffness and discomfort, as well as improving mobility. Hydrolyzed or denatured collagen is a peptide component of collagen that has been broken down enzymatically, by heat or pH changes. In contrast, non-denatured type II collagen retains its natural triple-helical conformation and has a molecular weight of approximately 300 kDa, while collagen hydrolysates have a molecular weight range of 2 to 9 kDa.

The structures are different and have different mechanisms of action. Hydrolyzed collagen is more readily absorbed due to its lower molecular weight and is therefore thought to aid cartilage regeneration by providing the raw material for collagen synthesis. In contrast, preclinical studies have shown that non-denatured collagen influences humoral and cellular immune responses through interactions with gut-associated lymphoid tissue (GALT) during oral tolerance.

For Healthcare Products:
Discovered in the early 1970s and isolated from chicken cartilage tissue by MILLER, non-denatured type II collagen is now widely used as a nutritional supplement worldwide. In Japan, non-denatured type II collagen has been approved as a functional food claim for joint improvement. In China, non-denatured type II collagen (renamed cartilage powder containing type II collagen) was approved by the National Health Commission in 2016 to be manufactured and operated as a general food product. Some of the sports health brands already in use are Vibramax, Calcium Elite, and others.
