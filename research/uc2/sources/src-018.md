About Undenatured Collagen
Undenatured Collagen belongs to the class of medications called nutritional supplements used to treat osteoarthritis. It also helps improve the flexibility and mobility of the joints. Osteoarthritis is a degenerative and inflammatory disease that affects synovial joints and eventually results in loss of mobility.

Undenatured Collagen contains a nutritional supplement named undenatured collagen, which is rich in amino acids. It is crucial in building joint cartilage when taken as a dietary mineral supplement. Through its anti-inflammatory effects, it effectively reduces joint inflammation as well. Thereby, Undenatured Collagen helps treat osteoarthritis symptoms and improves joint flexibility and movement.

Take Undenatured Collagen as recommended. Undenatured Collagen has minimal or no side effects and is generally safe. In rare cases, you may experience minor side effects like heaviness in your stomach, mild diarrhoea, and rashes. These side effects do not necessitate medical treatment and will subside with time. However, if you are experiencing these side effects on a regular basis, you should consult your doctor.

Do not use Undenatured Collagen if you are allergic to any of the contents. Consult your doctor before taking Undenatured Collagen if you are pregnant or breastfeeding. Keep your doctor informed about your health condition and all the medicines to rule out any side effects. It is unknown whether it is safe to consume alcohol with Undenatured Collagen. However, it is advisable not to take or limit alcohol as a precautionary measure.

Uses of Undenatured Collagen
Undenatured Collagen is used in the treatment of Osteoarthritis (joint pain and stiffness). The detailed uses of Undenatured Collagen are as follows:
• Osteoarthritis Management: Undenatured Collagen helps reduce joint pain, stiffness, and inflammation by modulating the immune response and protecting cartilage.
• Joint Pain Relief: Undenatured Collagen is beneficial for people with chronic joint discomfort, including athletes and older adults.
• Improved Mobility & Flexibility: Undenatured Collagen supports smoother joint movement by aiding cartilage repair and reducing inflammation.

Diet & Lifestyle Advise

Weight is the essential connection between diet and osteoarthritis. Obesity or being overweight puts more strain on your joints. Inflammation is also caused by excess fat, which can exacerbate symptoms. Losing weight has decreased pain and improved physical function and mobility.

An appropriate exercise can help you manage your weight. It helps improve joint flexiblity, strengthen muscles around your joints, and offers more support.

One to two portions of oily fish per week are recommended. If you cannot do so, you should consider taking a fish-oil supplement.

Mono-unsaturated oils should be used (e.g. rapeseed oil and olive oil).

If your blood cholesterol is high, make dietary changes to lower it.

Heat and cold treatments can help relieve arthritis pain and inflammation.

A diet that’s rich in fresh fruits, vegetables, and whole foods can help boost your immune system and your overall health.

Avoid a diet like a diet rich in red meat, processed foods, saturated fat, and added sugar and salt might aggravate inflammation, which is a characteristic of arthritis.

Limit alcohol and caffeine intake and quit smoking.
