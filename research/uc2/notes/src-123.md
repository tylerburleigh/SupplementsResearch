## Core Findings (from abstract only)

This meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled trials found that orally administered collagen-based supplements significantly improved osteoarthritis symptoms. The primary outcomes were measured via the WOMAC index and Visual Analog Scale (VAS). Key results:

1. **Total WOMAC index**: Significant reduction with collagen (WMD -8.00; 95% CI -13.04 to -2.95; p = 0.002).
2. **WOMAC stiffness subscore**: Significant decrease (WMD -0.41; 95% CI -0.74 to -0.08; p = 0.01).
3. **WOMAC pain subscore**: No significant difference (WMD -0.22; 95% CI -1.58 to 1.13; p = 0.75).
4. **WOMAC functional limitation subscore**: No significant difference (WMD -0.62; 95% CI -5.77 to 4.52; p = 0.81).
5. **VAS score**: Significant reduction with collagen (WMD -16.57; 95% CI -26.24 to -6.89; p < 0.001).
