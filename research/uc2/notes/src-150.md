# src-150: Lugo et al. 2013 -- UC-II for Joint Support in Healthy Volunteers

**Full title:** Undenatured type II collagen (UC-II) for joint support: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in healthy volunteers

**Journal:** Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition (J Int Soc Sports Nutr), 2013 Oct 24;10:48

**DOI:** 10.1186/1550-2783-10-48 | **PMID:** 24153020 | **PMCID:** PMC4015808

**Authors:** James P Lugo, Zainulabedin M Saiyed, Francis C Lau, Jhanna Pamela L Molina, Michael N Pakdaman, Arya Nick Shamie, Jay K Udani

---

## Core Findings

Daily supplementation with 40 mg UC-II (providing ~10.4 mg native undenatured type II collagen) for 120 days significantly improved knee extension and time to onset of exercise-induced joint pain in healthy adults with no diagnosed arthritis. UC-II was well tolerated with no product-related adverse events. The study is notable for testing UC-II in a healthy (non-osteoarthritic) population using a standardized stepmill stressor to provoke joint pain.

## Methodology

- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled parallel-group trial
- **Duration:** 120 days (17 weeks total with 7 visits: screening, baseline, days 7, 30, 60, 90, 120)
- **Site:** Staywell Research, Northridge, CA; CRO was Medicus Research LLC, Agoura Hills, CA
- **IRB:** Copernicus Group IRB, approved April 25, 2012
- **Randomization:** Simple randomization via atmospheric noise algorithm (random.org), sequential assignment in numbered envelopes
- **Blinding:** Subjects, clinical staff, data analysis and management staff all blinded
- **Analysis:** Per-protocol basis; ITT analysis also reported for key outcomes; SPSS v19

### Inclusion Criteria
- Age 30-65, BMI 18-35
- No knee pain at rest
- Achieved knee discomfort score >=5 on 11-point Likert scale within 10 minutes of stepmill protocol (level 4, StairMaster 7000PT)
- No diagnosable arthritis per ACR criteria (screened by board-certified clinician)

### Exclusion Criteria
- Any indicators of arthritis, joint disorders, autoimmune disorders
- Daily NSAID use (81 mg aspirin for cardioprotection allowed)
- Anti-inflammatory or joint health supplements within 30 days
- History of knee/hip replacement, significant joint injury within 6 months
- Immunosuppressive drugs within 12 months

### Interventions
- **UC-II group:** 40 mg UC-II daily (Lot 1109006), providing 10.4 +/- 1.3 mg native type II collagen, in opaque capsule with excipients (microcrystalline cellulose, magnesium stearate, silicon dioxide)
- **Placebo group:** Identical capsule containing excipients only
- **Administration:** One capsule daily with water before bedtime
- **Washout:** 2-week washout for prior NSAID/supplement users

### Primary Endpoint
- Knee range of motion (ROM) measured by goniometry (knee extension and flexion)

### Secondary Endpoints
- Timed joint discomfort measures (stepmill protocol): time to onset of initial pain, time to maximum pain, time to initial improvement, time to complete recovery
- KOOS knee survey (42-question validated instrument)
- Stanford exercise behavior scale
- Six-minute timed walk
- Daily steps/step length (Fitbit)

### Statistical Methods
- Within-group: non-parametric Sign test or Wilcoxon Signed Rank test
- Between-group: Wilcoxon Mann-Whitney test
- Complete loss of pain: Fisher Exact test (between groups), Binomial test (within visit)
- Results presented as mean +/- SEM; p <= 0.05 considered significant

## Sample Size

- **Screened:** 106 subjects
- **Randomized:** 55 (UC-II n=27, Placebo n=28)
- **Completed (per-protocol):** 46 total (UC-II n=24 [3 dropouts], Placebo n=22 [6 dropouts; 2 did not participate in ROM assessment])
- **Baseline demographics:** Mean age ~46 years; UC-II: 11M/16F, 75.5 kg, BMI 26.8; Placebo: 12M/16F, 77.5 kg, BMI 27.1

## Key Results

### Knee Extension (Primary Outcome)
- **Day 120 vs Placebo:** UC-II 81.0 +/- 1.3 deg vs Placebo 74.0 +/- 2.2 deg (p = 0.011)
- **Day 120 vs Baseline:** UC-II 81.0 +/- 1.3 deg vs 73.2 +/- 1.9 deg (p = 0.002)
- **Day 90 vs Baseline:** UC-II 78.8 +/- 1.9 deg vs 73.2 +/- 1.9 deg (p = 0.045)
- **ITT analysis Day 120 vs Placebo:** UC-II 80.0 +/- 1.3 deg vs Placebo 73.7 +/- 1.8 deg (p = 0.006)
- Net improvement: ~7.8 deg increase in knee extension from baseline at day 120
- No significant changes in placebo at any time point
- Statistical power for per-protocol analysis: 80%

### Knee Flexion
- No significant changes in either group (p > 0.05)

### Time to Onset of Initial Joint Pain (Stepmill)
- **Day 90:** 2.75 +/- 0.5 min vs baseline 1.4 min (p = 0.041)
- **Day 120:** 2.8 +/- 0.5 min vs baseline 1.4 min (p = 0.019)
- No significant between-group differences
- **Conservative analysis** (including pain-free subjects at 10-min cap): Day 90: 3.65 +/- 0.7 min (p = 0.011); Day 120: 4.31 +/- 0.7 min (p = 0.002); between-group at Day 120 p = 0.051

### Complete Loss of Knee Pain (Stepmill)
- UC-II: 5/24 subjects (21%) pain-free at Day 120 (binomial p = 0.031)
- Placebo: 1/22 subjects (5%) pain-free at Day 120
- Between-group difference not significant (p = 0.126) but trend favored UC-II
- Pain-free status was persistent across visits, not random

### Time to Complete Recovery from Pain
- Both groups showed significant reductions from baseline at days 60, 90, 120
- UC-II reductions: 31.9% (day 60, p=0.041), 51.1% (day 90, p=0.004), 51.9% (day 120, p=0.011)
- Placebo reductions: 21.9% (day 60, p=0.017), 22.2% (day 90, p=0.007), 30.0% (day 120, p=0.012)
- Between-group differences not significant

### Time to Maximum Joint Pain
- Significant between-group difference only at Day 60: UC-II 6.39 +/- 0.5 min vs Placebo 4.78 +/- 0.5 min (p = 0.025); authors attributed this to random occurrence as it did not persist

### KOOS Survey
- Both groups improved significantly vs baseline for symptoms, pain, daily function, recreational activities, QoL
- No significant between-group differences (expected given healthy population)

### Six-Minute Timed Walk & Daily Steps
- No significant between-group differences
- Both groups within normal reference ranges for healthy adults

## Adverse Events

- **Total:** 8 adverse events, equally distributed (4 in each group)
- **UC-II group AEs:** 3 upper respiratory infections, 1 food poisoning; 4/27 subjects reporting AEs
- **Placebo group AEs:** 1 bilateral ankle edema, 1 right ankle fracture, 1 sinusitis, 1 skin infection right ankle; 2/28 subjects reporting AEs
- **None considered related to UC-II supplementation**
- All resolved spontaneously; no withdrawals due to AE; no serious adverse events
- No differences in vital signs after 17 weeks of supplementation

## Limitations

1. **Stepmill pain onset capped at 10 minutes.** The protocol did not account for subjects who might cease experiencing pain entirely during the test. Future studies should extend the exertion interval.
2. **Possible undiagnosed early arthritis.** Subjects may have had early OA not meeting ACR criteria. The authors addressed this by extensive medical screening and excluding subjects who reached pain >=5 within 1 minute.
3. **Small sample size.** 55 randomized, 46 completed. Per-protocol analysis only; the study was powered at 80% for the primary outcome.
4. **No inflammatory biomarkers assessed.** Authors argued healthy subjects would not show significant biomarker alterations and the pain was acute/exercise-induced rather than chronic inflammatory.
5. **Industry-funded with substantial author conflicts.** Three authors are employees of the sponsor; PI is a paid consultant.
6. **No dose-ranging.** Single dose tested (40 mg/day).
7. **Non-parametric tests used throughout** (no parametric alternatives reported), suggesting data may not have met normality assumptions.

## Relevance to UC2 Research

This is one of the few RCTs examining UC-II in a healthy (non-arthritic) population. It provides evidence that 40 mg/day UC-II for 120 days can improve knee extension by ~7-8 degrees and roughly double the time to onset of exercise-induced joint pain (from 1.4 to 2.8 minutes). The effect on complete pain resolution (21% of UC-II vs 5% of placebo at day 120) is suggestive but not statistically significant between groups. The study supports the proposed mechanism of oral tolerance via T-regulatory cell induction in gut-associated lymphoid tissue, but no biomarker data are provided to confirm this mechanism in humans. The dosing (40 mg/day providing ~10 mg native type II collagen) aligns with the standard commercial dose used in other UC-II studies.
