# src-030: Mazurov et al. (2023) -- UC-II + Boswellia + MSM vs Glucosamine/Chondroitin for Knee OA

## Core Findings

A 6-month randomized comparative trial (n=70) found that Artneo (a fixed combination of undenatured type II collagen 40 mg, Boswellia acids 32.5 mg, MSM 300 mg, vitamin C 80 mg, vitamin D3 10 mcg, taken once daily) was non-inferior to glucosamine hydrochloride + chondroitin sulfate (GC, Artra brand) for primary knee osteoarthritis (stages I-III). Artneo showed advantages in morning stiffness reduction (greater at all time points, p<0.05), faster improvement in Lequesne index (moderate-to-mild by 3 months vs 6 months for GC), greater synovitis area reduction on MRI (2.95x vs 1.37x), and higher physician-rated improvement (83% "significant improvement" vs 60%). Both groups improved on WOMAC and VAS pain without significant intergroup differences. No clinically significant adverse events occurred in either group.

**Important: This study addresses KNEE osteoarthritis exclusively, NOT back/spine pain.**

## Study Design

- **Design:** Open-label, prospective, randomized, comparative study
- **Registration:** ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06032442
- **Setting:** Medical Unit No. 157, Saint Petersburg, Russia (March 2022 -- July 2023)
- **Duration:** 6 months treatment, 9 visits
- **Randomization:** Block randomization using envelopes

## Participants

- **N = 70** (35 per group)
- **Age:** 40-75 years (mean 55 +/- 9 AN group, 60 +/- 9 GC group; p=0.013)
- **Sex:** 83% female in both groups
- **Diagnosis:** Primary knee OA, Kellgren-Lawrence stages I-III
- **Inclusion criteria:** Moderate pain on VAS walking (45-74 mm), Lequesne index 5-7 (moderate severity)
- **Exclusion:** Systemic connective tissue diseases, fibromyalgia, chronic pain syndromes, gout
- **Comorbidities:** Hypertension (~50%), overweight (~50%), obesity grade 1 (~26%), type 2 diabetes (~10%), chronic gastritis (~14%)
- **Washout:** Analgesics and NSAIDs stopped 7 days before baseline

## Interventions

- **AN group (n=35):** Artneo 1 capsule/day evening (UC-II 40 mg, Boswellia acids 32.5 mg, MSM 300 mg, vitamin C 80 mg, vitamin D3 10 mcg)
- **GC group (n=35):** Glucosamine HCl 500 mg + chondroitin sulfate 500 mg (Artra brand), 1 tablet 2x/day for 3 weeks, then 1 tablet/day for remaining 21 weeks
- **Rescue medication:** Paracetamol up to 1500 mg/day, stopped 48h before assessments
- Only 3 patients (9%) per group needed rescue medication

## Key Outcome Measures and Results

### Pain (VAS, 0-100 mm)
- Baseline: ~57 mm (AN) vs ~59 mm (GC)
- Day 180: 23.6 mm (AN) vs 15.0 mm (GC) -- both significant reductions from baseline (p<0.05)
- AN reduction: 72.9% from baseline; GC reduction: 60.6%
- No significant intergroup difference at endpoint

### WOMAC Index
- Baseline: 35.6 (AN) vs 33.9 (GC)
- Day 180: 16.1 (AN) vs 12.7 (GC)
- Significant improvement from day 30 in both groups (p<0.05)
- No significant intergroup difference

### Lequesne Index (OA Severity)
- Baseline: 6.19 (AN) vs 6.1 (GC) -- moderate severity
- AN: Significant improvement by day 30; severity dropped to mild (<5) by 3 months
- GC: Significant improvement by day 180 only
- At 3 months, AN group significantly lower than GC (p<0.05)
- Clinically meaningful reduction (>=2.75 points) achieved in AN group

### Morning Stiffness
- AN group showed significantly greater reduction at 1, 3, and 6 months (p<0.05)
- AN: baseline 8.6 min -> 2.7 min at 6 months (5.91 min reduction)
- GC: baseline 7.0 min -> 4.4 min at 6 months (2.57 min reduction)

### SF-36 Quality of Life
- Physical component: AN group significantly higher (p<0.05)
  - AN: 37.83 -> 46.03; GC: 36.33 -> 42.37
- Large effect size (>0.6) for "Bodily Pain" subscale in AN group
- Moderate effect sizes for physical functioning, role functioning, social functioning

### MRI Outcomes (6 months)
- **T2 relaxation time** (cartilage ultrastructure): Significant improvement in both groups (p<0.05), no intergroup difference
  - AN: 34.3 +/- 3.4 ms -> 32.9 +/- 3.1 ms (p=0.002)
  - GC: 34.3 +/- 3.1 ms -> 33.1 +/- 3.6 ms (p=0.001)
- **Synovitis area**: More pronounced reduction in AN group
  - AN: 2.95-fold reduction; GC: 1.37-fold reduction
  - Positive dynamics in 53% of AN patients, 60% of GC patients
- **Osteophytes, bone marrow edema, cystic changes**: No significant change in either group

### Physician Assessment (IMOS)
- Significant improvement: 83% (AN) vs 60% (GC), p<0.05
- Patient satisfaction: No significant intergroup difference (p=0.09)

## Safety
- No clinically significant adverse events in either group
- Vital signs, hematology, and biochemistry remained stable
- No dropouts due to adverse events or need for NSAIDs

## Limitations
- **Small sample size** (n=35 per group) -- limited statistical power
- **Open-label** (not blinded)
- **Imbalanced age** between groups (AN mean 55, GC mean 60, p=0.013)
- **Short follow-up** (6 months only)
- **No placebo control**
- Conducted at a single center
- 83% female -- limited generalizability to males

## Relevance to Research Questions

### Back Pain
**Not relevant.** This study exclusively examines knee osteoarthritis. No spine, back, or lumbar outcomes were measured. The intervention's mechanism (oral immune tolerance via UC-II targeting type II collagen) is specific to articular cartilage of peripheral joints. No extrapolation to back pain is supported by this study.

### Combination Therapy for Joint Pain
**Moderately relevant.** Demonstrates that a multi-component supplement (UC-II + Boswellia + MSM + vitamins C/D3) can match or exceed glucosamine/chondroitin for knee OA. The once-daily dosing is a practical advantage. However, the open-label design and small sample limit the strength of evidence.

### UC-II Efficacy
**Relevant as supporting evidence.** The Artneo combination contains UC-II (40 mg/day) as a key component. The study cannot isolate UC-II's individual contribution from the other ingredients, but the combination's success supports further investigation of UC-II in multi-ingredient formulations.

## Claims to Verify
- **Sample size of 70 (35+35):** Verified from Methods and Table 1. Two patients in AN group appear to have been excluded from MRI analysis (n=33 for T2 data) -- reason not clearly stated.
- **Synovitis reduction "2.95 times" in AN group:** Stated in abstract and results, but the raw MRI data in Figure 7 shows baseline synovitis area of 1.86 cm2 (AN) and 1.27 cm2 (GC), dropping to 0.43 cm2 and 1.36 cm2 respectively. The AN reduction (1.86/0.43 = 4.3x) does not match the stated "2.95 times." The GC values (1.27 to 1.36) actually show an increase, not a 1.37x reduction. These figures may come from a different subset or measurement -- needs cross-checking.
- **Age imbalance (p=0.013):** Confirmed in Table 1, a potentially important confound given the older GC group.
