# src-073: Undenatured Collagen for Joint Cartilage Function Normalization

**Citation:** Dydykina IS, Kovalenko PS, Kovalenko AA, Aboleshina AV. *Meditsinskiy Sovet (Medical Council)*. 2022;16(14):145-153. DOI: 10.21518/2079-701X-2022-16-14-145-153

**Language:** Russian with English abstract. Full text read and translated.

**Quality assessment:** Full paper content available (88KB). Review article, not a primary clinical trial. Peer-reviewed Russian rheumatology journal. Authors affiliated with Nasonova Research Institute of Rheumatology (Moscow) and Sechenov University.

## Core Findings

This is a comprehensive narrative review on undenatured (native) type II collagen (UC-II) for osteoarthritis. It synthesizes preclinical and clinical evidence for UC-II, describes the oral tolerance mechanism in detail, and promotes a specific Russian commercial supplement (Artneo/ARTNEO by Petrovax) containing UC-II plus MSM, boswellic acids, vitamin C, and vitamin D3.

## Key Evidence and Data Points

### Mechanism of Action: Oral Tolerance
- UC-II retains intact epitopes (tertiary structure preserved, MW ~300 kDa vs. 2-9 kDa for collagen hydrolysate)
- After oral ingestion, epitopes interact with immune-competent cells in the duodenum
- Stimulates oral tolerance via GALT system: CD103+ dendritic cells take up antigens in Peyer's patches, migrate to mesenteric lymph nodes, differentiate naive T-cells into T-regulatory cells
- T-regs suppress proliferation of inflammatory T-cells and production of pro-inflammatory cytokines systemically
- This distinguishes UC-II from hydrolyzed collagen, which lacks intact epitopes

### Preclinical Studies Reviewed
1. **Mouse collagen-induced arthritis model:** Oral type II collagen reduced CD4+ T-cell expression of RANKL (osteoclastogenic mediator linked to IL-17) [refs 54, 55]
2. **Rat OA model (monoiodoacetate):** Porcine native type II collagen (13 days oral) prevented pain threshold changes, reduced CTX-II in plasma and urine [ref 54]
3. **Rat OA model:** UC-II preserved weight-bearing capacity of affected hind limb, maintained cancellous bone integrity, prevented cartilage destruction progression [ref 56 - Bagi et al. 2017]
4. **Toxicity study (Sprague-Dawley rats):** UC-II + chondroitin sulfate + hyaluronic acid at 5000 mg/kg (acute, 14 days) and 30-1000 mg/kg (subchronic, 90 days) showed no mortality, no weight changes, no organ pathology [ref 57]

### Clinical Studies Reviewed

**Study 1 - Lugo et al. 2013 (J Int Soc Sports Nutr) [ref 58]:**
- 55 healthy volunteers with knee pain after physical activity
- RCT, double-blind, placebo-controlled
- UC-II 40 mg/day for 4 months (n=27) vs. placebo (n=28)
- Significant improvement in knee extension range: 81.0 +/- 1.3 vs. 74.0 +/- 2.0 degrees (p=0.011 vs. placebo); vs. baseline 73.2 +/- 1.9 (p=0.002)
- Time to joint discomfort increased: 2.8 +/- 0.5 vs. 1.4 +/- 0.2 min (p=0.019)
- Well tolerated, no adverse events

**Study 2 - Crowley et al. 2009 (Int J Med Sci) [ref 59]:**
- 52 knee OA patients
- UC-II (n=26) vs. glucosamine + chondroitin (n=26), 3 months
- WOMAC decreased 33% (UC-II) vs. 14% (G+C); significant at months 1, 2, 3 (p<0.005 for UC-II)
- VAS pain decreased 40% (UC-II) vs. 15.4% (G+C)
- Lequesne functional index decreased 20% (UC-II) vs. 6% (G+C)

**Study 3 - Lugo et al. 2016 (Nutr J) [ref 60]:**
- 191 knee OA patients, multicenter RCT, double-blind, 6 months
- UC-II 40 mg/day vs. placebo vs. glucosamine HCl (1500 mg) + chondroitin sulfate (1200 mg)
- Primary endpoint: WOMAC total change
- UC-II WOMAC change: -551 vs. placebo -414 (95% CI: -232 to -42; p=0.002); vs. G+C -454 (95% CI: -190 to -3; p=0.04)
- VAS pain significantly lower in UC-II vs. both placebo (p=0.002) and G+C (p=0.025)
- No significant difference between placebo and G+C on VAS pain
- Sub-analysis: patients with baseline COMP (cartilage oligomeric matrix protein) >= 285 ng/ml showed greater WOMAC improvement

### Spine and Back Pain
- The paper explicitly mentions that UC-II-based compositions improve "functional joint **and spinal** conditions" (Russian: "улучшению функционального состояния суставов и позвоночника") in multiple places
- The specific product Artneo is described as improving functional state of "joints and spine" and reducing discomfort, swelling, and tension "in the joints and spine" (line 676-677)
- Fibrous (fibrocartilage) is noted to predominate in intervertebral discs (line 194-195)
- However, **no specific clinical data on back pain or spine outcomes is presented** -- all reviewed clinical trials focused on knee OA
- The spine claim appears to be extrapolated from the general mechanism and the inclusion of MSM/boswellic acids

### Artneo Supplement Composition
- Undenatured type II collagen: 40 mg
- Methylsulfonylmethane (MSM): 300 mg
- Boswellia serrata extract (65% boswellic acids): 50.05 mg
- Vitamin C (ascorbic acid): 80 mg
- Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol): 400 IU
- Dosage: 1 capsule/day for 2 months, repeatable

## Methodology

Narrative review article. No systematic search methodology described. References include both Russian and English literature (61 references). The review selectively presents positive findings and does not discuss null results or limitations of individual studies. No meta-analysis performed by the authors themselves.

## Limitations

1. **Narrative review only** -- not a systematic review or meta-analysis. No declared search strategy or inclusion criteria.
2. **Product promotion:** The final section explicitly promotes Artneo (Petrovax), a specific commercial Russian product. This creates a potential conflict of interest despite the authors' declaration of no conflicts. The product was registered in Russia and the paper functions partly as promotional material.
3. **No back pain clinical data:** While spine/back claims are made repeatedly, none of the reviewed clinical trials measured spinal outcomes. The spine claims are mechanistic extrapolations only.
4. **Small sample sizes** in the reviewed clinical trials (52-191 patients across 3 studies).
5. **No independent Russian clinical trial data:** All clinical data cited comes from English-language publications by the same UC-II manufacturer's research network.
6. **No adverse events discussed systematically** beyond noting "well tolerated."

## Unique Contributions Not Typically in English Literature

1. Detailed discussion of Artneo, a specific 5-component combination supplement sold in Russia (UC-II + MSM + boswellic acids + vitamin C + vitamin D3) -- this product composition is not commonly discussed in English-language reviews
2. Russian-language clinical practice context for OA management (referencing Russian national guidelines and ESCEO algorithm adaptation for Russian practice)
3. Extensive histological background on cartilage types (hyaline, fibrous, elastic) and their anatomical distribution, including fibrocartilage in intervertebral discs
4. Discussion of hormonal regulation of cartilage metabolism (effects of estrogens, testosterone, thyroid hormones, glucocorticoids on collagen synthesis) -- more detailed than typical English supplement reviews

## Claims to Verify

1. **"Fibrous cartilage predominates in intervertebral discs"** (line 194) -- this is anatomically correct and not controversial.
2. **Artneo improves spinal function** -- this claim appears in the abstract and conclusion but has no supporting clinical data in the paper. It appears to be marketing language rather than evidence-based.
3. **UC-II dose of 40 mg/day** -- this is the standard dose used across multiple published studies and is consistent with the English literature.
4. **WOMAC reduction of 33% (Crowley) and UC-II superiority over G+C** -- these numbers match the original publications (Crowley et al. 2009, Lugo et al. 2016) and are cited accurately.

## Relevance to Research Question

This source provides:
- Strong evidence on UC-II mechanism of action (oral tolerance pathway)
- Moderate evidence on clinical efficacy for knee OA pain reduction (via secondary review of 3 published trials)
- Weak evidence on back pain/spine benefits (claims only, no data)
- Context on combination supplement formulations (UC-II + MSM + boswellic acids + vitamins)
