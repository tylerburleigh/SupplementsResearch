# src-031: Safety and efficacy of undenatured type II collagen in the treatment of osteoarthritis of the knee

**Citation:** Crowley et al., Int J Med Sci 2009; 6(6):312-321. DOI: 10.7150/ijms.6.312
**Type:** Randomized, double-blind, active-comparator clinical trial
**Quality:** PDF conversion degraded but fully readable; all data tables and text extractable.
**Citations:** 92 (per Crossref)

## Core Findings

UC-II (40 mg/day containing 10 mg bioactive undenatured type II collagen) demonstrated superior efficacy to glucosamine + chondroitin (1500 mg + 1200 mg/day) over 90 days in knee OA patients. UC-II reduced WOMAC by 33% vs 14%, VAS by 40% vs 15.4%, and Lequesne's functional index by 20.1% vs 5.9%. UC-II showed a favorable safety profile with fewer total adverse events (35 vs 58) and fewer product-related events (11.4% vs 23% of subjects).

## Study Design

- **Design:** Two-site, randomized, double-blind, active-comparator trial (no placebo arm)
- **Sites:** KGK Synergize Inc. and Corunna Medical Research, Ontario, Canada
- **Approval:** Human Clinical Trial Approval #06UOHI
- **Duration:** 90 days with assessments at Day 0, 30, 60, 90
- **Sample size:** 52 total (26 UC-II, 26 G+C); sample size based on Braham et al. (ref 1), targeting 25 per group
- **Population:** Adults 40-75 years with moderate OA of the knee (Lequesne score 4.5-7.5), confirmed by X-ray
- **Randomization:** Computer-generated randomization tables; no stratification or blocking

## Dosing

- **UC-II group:** 40 mg UC-II daily (two capsules in evening, standardized to 10 mg bioactive undenatured type II collagen; supplied by InterHealth Nutraceuticals, Benicia, CA). Two placebo ("sugar pills") taken in the morning to protect blinding.
- **G+C group:** 1500 mg glucosamine HCl + 1200 mg chondroitin sulfate daily (two capsules AM, two PM; USP Grade)
- **Rescue medication:** Acetaminophen allowed; monitored

## Clinical Outcomes

### WOMAC Score
- UC-II: 33% reduction at Day 90 (statistically significant at all time points vs baseline, p<0.05)
- G+C: 14% reduction at Day 90 (NOT statistically significant at Day 90 vs baseline)
- Significant interaction for specific subscales favoring UC-II: pain walking on flat surface (p=0.034), difficulty walking on flat surface (p=0.038), performing heavy domestic duties (p=0.031), ascending stairs (p=0.013)
- UC-II significantly better than G+C at specific visits for ascending stairs (30d p=0.019, 60d p=0.040), night pain in bed (60d p=0.015), difficulty walking on flat surface (90d p=0.035)

### VAS Score (100-mm)
- UC-II: 40% reduction at Day 90
- G+C: 15% reduction at Day 90
- Significant treatment effects for UC-II: climbing stairs (p=0.035), night pain (p=0.030), resting pain (p=0.024)
- UC-II significantly better at specific visits: night pain (60d p=0.040), resting pain (60d p=0.020, 90d p=0.034), climbing stairs (90d p=0.014)
- UC-II within-group: significant VAS reduction at Day 60 and Day 90
- G+C within-group: significant VAS reduction only at Day 30, not sustained at Day 60 or 90

### Lequesne's Functional Index
- UC-II: 20.1% reduction at Day 90
- G+C: 5.9% reduction at Day 90
- No between-group differences reached statistical significance
- UC-II within-group: significant reduction from baseline to Day 90
- G+C within-group: no significant difference from baseline at any time point

## Safety Data

### Adverse Events (Table 5)
- Total events: UC-II 35 vs G+C 58
- Subjects experiencing AE: UC-II 16/26 (61.5%) vs G+C 20/26 (76.9%)
- Severity: UC-II -- 15 mild, 19 moderate, 1 severe; G+C -- 35 mild, 22 moderate, 1 severe
- Product-related events (possible): UC-II 4 events (11.4% of subjects) vs G+C 8 events (23% of subjects)
- Probable/most probable related events: 0 in both groups
- No significant difference in AE occurrence between groups (stated by authors)

### UC-II product-related AEs
- Constipation and intermittent headaches

### G+C product-related AEs
- Bloating, stomach pain, rash, water retention (edema around eyes and scars), hives on face and chest, headache

### Body system breakdown
- Pain: UC-II 10 vs G+C 17
- GI: UC-II 5 vs G+C 15
- Musculoskeletal/Soft Tissue: UC-II 7 vs G+C 5

### Rescue Medication Use
- Fewer UC-II subjects used rescue medication at every time point
- Day 0-30: UC-II 33.3% vs G+C 88.5%
- Day 30-60: UC-II 54.2% vs G+C 80.8%
- Day 60-90: UC-II 63.6% vs G+C 79.2%

### Blood safety monitoring
- Comprehensive blood panel at screening and Day 90 (uric acid, CBC, albumin, total protein, electrolytes, BUN, creatinine, ALT, AST, bilirubin, ESR, rheumatoid factor)
- Additional liver monitoring (ALT, AST, bilirubin, albumin) at Days 30 and 60 if acetaminophen use >2 g/day for >7 days
- No adverse changes reported

## Methodology Notes

- ANOVA with baseline as covariate for between-group comparisons
- Last-observation-carried-forward (LOCF) for missing data imputation
- SAS version 9.1 for analysis
- p<0.05 considered significant
- Compliance assessed by capsule count and diary review; mean compliance 88-98% across visits

## Limitations

1. **Small sample size:** Only 52 subjects (26 per group); not powered for a definitive efficacy comparison
2. **No placebo control:** Only active-comparator (G+C), which itself showed limited efficacy; cannot distinguish UC-II effect from placebo
3. **High dropout/attrition:** Compliance tables show n=22-26 at later visits, suggesting some dropout
4. **Short duration:** 90 days only
5. **Industry funding and author conflicts:** Funded by the UC-II manufacturer; multiple authors employed by the company producing UC-II
6. **Between-group differences mostly non-significant:** While within-group changes were significant for UC-II, formal between-group comparisons on summary scores (WOMAC total, VAS total, Lequesne total) did not reach statistical significance. Only select subscales showed significant between-group differences.
7. **LOCF imputation** may bias results upward if dropouts were related to treatment failure

## Relevance to Research Question

This is a key trial for UC-II safety and efficacy in human knee OA. It provides:
- Dose data: 40 mg UC-II (10 mg bioactive collagen) daily
- Safety profile: favorable relative to G+C, though no placebo comparison
- Clinical outcomes across three validated instruments (WOMAC, VAS, Lequesne)
- Rescue medication usage as a proxy for symptom control
