# src-051: UC-II (Artneo) in Stage III Knee OA — Russian RCT (Part II)

**Citation:** Alekseeva LI et al. Terapevticheskii Arkhiv. 2025;97(1):54-64. DOI: 10.26442/00403660.2025.01.203145

**Quality:** Full paper text available (Russian + English bilingual). Peer-reviewed journal. Funded by Petrovax LLC (manufacturer of Artneo).

## Core Findings

This is a subgroup analysis (Part II) of a larger 212-patient multicenter RCT, focusing on 50 patients with radiological stage III knee OA. The Artneo combination (undenatured type II collagen [UC-II] 40 mg + MSM 300 mg + Boswellia serrata extract 32.5 mg + vitamin C 80 mg + vitamin D3 10 mcg) was significantly superior to placebo across all measured outcomes over 180 days of treatment, with a good safety profile.

## Study Design

- **Design:** Multicenter (12 centers, Russia), prospective, double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized trial
- **Registration:** ClinicalTrials.gov ID NCT05975879
- **Population subset:** 50 patients with Kellgren-Lawrence stage III knee OA (out of 212 total)
  - Artneo group: n=28 (78.6% female, mean age 64.3 +/- 7.4)
  - Placebo group: n=22 (86.4% female, mean age 65.1 +/- 6.6)
  - BMI ~28 kg/m2 in both groups
- **Dosing:** 1 capsule/day for 180 days
- **Analysis:** ITT population (n=50) and PP population (n=45); results reported for ITT
- **Primary endpoint:** Change in WOMAC total score at visit 7 (day 180) vs baseline, analyzed by ANCOVA

## Key Evidence / Clinical Outcomes

### Primary Endpoint (WOMAC-T)
- ANCOVA showed Artneo group had -217.7 point greater reduction in WOMAC-T vs placebo at day 180 (95% CI -411 to -24; p=0.0284)

### Pain (WOMAC-A)
- Statistically significant greater pain reduction in Artneo group at months 2, 4, and 6 (p<0.05 vs placebo)
- Within-group improvement in Artneo group significant from day 30 onward (p<0.001, Wilcoxon)
- Placebo group showed no significant within-group improvement until month 6

### Stiffness (WOMAC-B)
- Significant reduction in Artneo group at months 4 and 5 vs placebo (p<0.05)
- Maximum intergroup difference at month 4
- Within Artneo group, significant stiffness reduction from month 1 (p<0.05)
- Placebo group showed no significant stiffness improvement until month 6

### Physical Function (WOMAC-C)
- Significant improvement in Artneo group vs placebo at month 4 (p=0.0374)
- Within-group improvement from month 3 in Artneo group
- Placebo group only reached significance at month 6

### KOOS Score
- Statistically significant superiority of Artneo at visits 5, 6, 7 (months 3, 4, 6; p<0.05)
- Improvement in Artneo group from day 30 vs baseline

### Quality of Life (EQ-5D)
- Statistically significant improvement in Artneo group at day 90 (p=0.0499)
- EQ-5D VAS also significantly better in Artneo group at days 90, 120, and 180 (p=0.0161, 0.0387, 0.0444)

### Responder Analysis (at month 3)
- WOMAC_20T (20% improvement in total WOMAC): significantly more responders in Artneo group (p=0.0024)
- WOMAC_20PFS (20% improvement in pain + stiffness/function): significantly more responders in Artneo group (p=0.0112)

### NSAID Use
- No statistically significant difference between groups in NSAID consumption
- No patients dropped out due to need for additional pain medication

## Safety

- 7 adverse events (AEs) in 5 patients (10.0%):
  - Artneo group: 6 AEs in 4 patients (14.3%)
  - Placebo group: 1 AE in 1 patient (4.5%)
- All AEs were mild; none classified as serious or related to the study supplement
- Most common AEs in Artneo group: musculoskeletal complaints (likely disease-related) and rhinitis
- No clinically significant laboratory abnormalities in either group
- Fisher's exact test: no significant difference in AE rates between groups (p=0.7)

## Mechanism of Action

UC-II from chicken sternal cartilage retains its native triple-helix structure and glycosylation, enabling oral immune tolerance: dendritic cells in Peyer's patches present the native epitopes to T and B lymphocytes, generating regulatory T-cells that produce anti-inflammatory cytokines in response to damaged collagen in joints.

## Cost-Effectiveness

- Incremental cost-effectiveness ratio: 1,858.8 thousand rubles/QALY
- Equivalent to 158% of Russian GDP per capita (1,176.7 thousand rubles in 2023)
- Classified as "economically acceptable" per WHO thresholds (<3x GDP per capita)

## Discussion / Context from Paper

- The paper cites a prior RCT (Lugo et al., 2016, Nutr J) with 191 knee OA patients over 180 days, where UC-II was superior to both placebo and glucosamine+chondroitin on WOMAC outcomes
- An expert panel in Russia recommended Artneo for courses up to 6 months in OA complex therapy
- A separate comparative study (Mazurov et al., 2023, n=70) found Artneo not inferior to chondroitin sulfate + glucosamine hydrochloride, and superior on MRI synovitis reduction and Lequesne index

## Limitations

1. **Industry-funded:** Petrovax LLC (the manufacturer of Artneo) funded and participated in the study. Authors claim independence.
2. **Small subgroup:** Only 50 patients in the stage III analysis (28 active, 22 placebo), limiting statistical power
3. **Combination product:** Artneo contains UC-II plus MSM, Boswellia, vitamin C, and vitamin D3 — cannot isolate the effect of UC-II alone
4. **Short follow-up for a chronic condition:** 180 days; no data on durability after cessation
5. **No back pain data:** This study exclusively examined knee OA; no spine or back pain outcomes reported
6. **Geographic limitation:** All centers in Russia; generalizability uncertain
7. **Gender imbalance:** 82% female, limiting applicability to males

## Relevance to Research Questions

### Clinical Outcomes for UC-II
**Strong evidence.** This is a well-designed multicenter DBPCR trial showing UC-II combination product significantly reduces pain, stiffness, and improves function and QoL in advanced (stage III) knee OA. Primary and multiple secondary endpoints all favored active treatment.

### Back Pain Evidence
**Not addressed.** This study focuses exclusively on knee OA. No spine, back, or non-knee joint outcomes reported. UC-II's oral tolerance mechanism could theoretically apply to spinal facet joints, but no direct evidence here.

### Safety
**Strong evidence.** Good safety profile with only mild AEs, no serious AEs, no laboratory abnormalities. The combination was well-tolerated over 6 months.

### Long-term Results
**Moderate evidence.** 180-day treatment period is one of the longer UC-II RCT durations, but no post-treatment follow-up data. The within-group improvements were sustained throughout the 6 months, with progressive benefit over time.
