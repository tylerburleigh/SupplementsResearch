# src-024: UC-II Undenatured Type II Collagen for Knee Joint Flexibility (Schon et al., 2022, J Integr Complement Med)

## Core Findings

Daily supplementation with 40 mg UC-II undenatured type II collagen for 24 weeks significantly improved active knee ROM flexion by 3.23 degrees versus 0.21 degrees for placebo (p=0.025) in healthy adults with activity-related joint discomfort. Subgroup analyses revealed stronger effects in females (+4.79 degrees, p=0.0063) and adults over 35 years old (+6.79 degrees, p=0.0092). UC-II was well tolerated with no product-related adverse events.

## Study Design and Methodology

- **Design:** Prospective, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study
- **Registration:** DRKS00018792 (German Clinical Trials Register)
- **Ethics:** Approved by IRB of Landesärztekammer Baden-Württemberg (F-2019-072)
- **Conducted per:** ICH-GCP E6 (R2) and Declaration of Helsinki (E8)
- **Study period:** September 2019 to January 2021 (conducted during COVID-19 pandemic)
- **Sites:** BioTeSys GmbH (Esslingen) and University of Stuttgart, Institute of Sport and Movement Science

## Sample Size and Participants

- **Screened:** 178 subjects
- **Randomized:** 96 (48 per group)
- **Completed:** 96 (zero dropouts)
- **Age:** 20-55 years (mean: UC-II 34.5 +/- 1.5; PLA 37.8 +/- 1.6)
- **BMI:** 19-29.9 kg/m2 (mean: UC-II 23.91; PLA 24.29)
- **Gender:** 20M/28F per group
- **Inclusion criteria:** Healthy, physically active (sport >= 2x/week), reversible knee-joint discomfort during/after physical activity for >= 3 months, pain level 5 on 11-point Likert NRS during SLSD test (between 30-150 steps)
- **Exclusion criteria:** Joint replacement, planned surgery, intra-articular therapy within 3 months, OA or joint disease history, active cancer, CVD, pregnancy/lactation, smoking >5 cig/day, egg/chicken hypersensitivity, chronic pain medication within 30 days
- **82 screen failures** -- most common: pain level 5 reached too early (<30 reps) or too low pain (<5) during SLSD test

## Dosing and Intervention

- **Intervention:** 40 mg UC-II undenatured type II collagen (from chicken sternum, >= 3% undenatured type II collagen) per day
- **Placebo:** Microcrystalline cellulose in sensory-identical capsules
- **Administration:** One capsule daily with water in the evening before bedtime
- **Duration:** 24 weeks (6 months), 7 study visits at 4-week intervals
- **Compliance:** > 99% in both groups
- **Product supplied by:** Lonza Greenwood LLC (Greenwood, SC, USA)

## Outcome Measures

- **Primary measure:** Knee ROM flexion (active) measured by digital goniometer at each visit
- **Secondary measures:** Knee ROM extension (active, at baseline/week 12/week 24), passive ROM flexion, ROM by gender subgroup, ROM by age subgroup
- **Measurement tool:** Digital goniometer (Halo Medical Devices, Sydney, Australia)
- **Target knee:** The leg that typically experienced more intense pain after physical activity

## Statistical Methods

- **Analysis population:** Intent-to-treat (ITT)
- **Between-group comparison:** ANCOVA with baseline value as covariate
- **Within-group over time:** Repeated-measures ANOVA or Friedman test
- **Post hoc:** Dunnett's or Dunn's multiple comparison test
- **Significance level:** p < 0.05
- **Software:** IBM SPSS Statistics 24
- **Sample size calculation:** Based on pilot study and Lugo et al.; effect size 0.636, 80% power, alpha=0.05, requiring 40 per group + 15% dropout buffer = 48 per group

## Key Results

### Active ROM Flexion (primary)
| Measure | UC-II (n=48) | Placebo (n=48) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline | 127.6 (123.8-131.4) | 126.9 (123.9-129.9) | >0.05 |
| Delta at 24 weeks | +3.23 degrees (0.44 to 6.02) | +0.21 degrees (-1.67 to 2.08) | **0.025** |
| Within-group p | 0.2844 | 0.9134 | -- |

- Significant between-group difference observed as early as **week 8** and sustained through week 24

### Subgroup: Females (Active ROM Flexion)
- UC-II females (n=28): +4.79 degrees (95% CI: 1.21-8.36) vs. PLA females (n=28): -0.32 degrees (95% CI: -2.85 to 2.21); **p=0.0063**
- Within-group: UC-II p=0.0332 (significant over time)

### Subgroup: Males (Active ROM Flexion)

### Subgroup: Age >35 (Active ROM Flexion)
- UC-II (n=19): +6.79 degrees (95% CI: 2.38-11.20) vs. PLA (n=27): +0.30 degrees (95% CI: -2.60 to 3.19); **p=0.0092**

### Subgroup: Age 20-35 (Active ROM Flexion)
- UC-II (n=29): +0.90 degrees vs. PLA (n=21): +0.10 degrees; p=0.6012 (NS)

## Safety and Tolerability

- No study product-related adverse events in either group
- No abnormalities in blood biochemistry, hematology, or vital signs
- Tolerability rated "well tolerated" by 98% of participants
- Safety labs (hematology, liver enzymes, lipid profile, hsCRP, HbA1c, kidney function) assessed at screening and final visit
- Blood pressure and heart rate evaluated at screening, week 12, and week 24

## Limitations

1. **No biomarker assessment** performed to investigate mechanism of action
2. **COVID-19 pandemic impact:** Gym closures disrupted regular physical activity routines; some subjects reported reduced activity frequency, particularly in winter months. Deviations rated as minor; product supply maintained.
3. **Industry-funded:** Study funded by Lonza Greenwood LLC; three authors (V.J., S.D., Z.S.) are Lonza employees
4. **Effect sizes modest:** 3.23 degree improvement in flexion, though statistically significant, is clinically small
5. **Subgroup imbalances:** Age >35 subgroup had unequal n (UC-II n=19 vs. PLA n=27)
6. **Between-group comparison for ROM extension not significant** (p=0.3115); only within-group UC-II was significant
7. **Open-label SLSD screening** could introduce expectation bias

## Relevance to Research Question

This is a direct UC-II clinical trial using the standard 40 mg/day dose, providing primary evidence for UC-II efficacy on joint flexibility (ROM) in a healthy population with activity-related joint discomfort. Key contributions:
- Confirms the 40 mg/day dosing protocol used across the UC-II literature
- 24-week duration is the longest published UC-II RCT
- Zero dropout rate strengthens internal validity
- ROM measured objectively via digital goniometer
- Extensive subgroup analyses by gender and age
- ITT analysis approach

## Claims to Verify

- **Sample size:** 96 randomized (48+48) with zero dropouts -- verified in CONSORT flow and results sections. Unusual but plausible given close monitoring and pandemic context.
- **Compliance >99%:** Stated but method is capsule count (dispensed minus returned), which is standard though subject to overestimation.
- **Effect size 0.636 used for power calculation:** This is the assumed effect, not the observed effect. The observed between-group difference (3.02 degrees for flexion) should be compared against this assumption.
