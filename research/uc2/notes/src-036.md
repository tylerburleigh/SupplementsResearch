# src-036: Combined UC-II + Hydrolysed Collagen for Knee OA (Negative RCT)

**Citation:** Yuenyongviwat V, et al. (2025). *Scientific Reports*, 15:32313. doi:10.1038/s41598-025-17505-0
**Design:** Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. NCT06229951.
**Setting:** Single centre, Prince of Songkla University, Thailand. Nov 2023 - Aug 2024.
**Funding:** Medical Council of Thailand (non-industry). Authors declare no competing interests.

## Core Findings

Combined supplementation with UC-II (20 mg) and hydrolysed collagen (480 mg) daily for 12 weeks produced **no statistically significant improvement** over placebo on any measured outcome in patients with knee osteoarthritis (KL grade 2-3). Both groups improved significantly from baseline (likely natural history / placebo effect), but between-group differences were negligible across pain (VNRS), function (KOOS), rescue medication use, and satisfaction. No adverse events were reported in either group.

## Key Evidence / Data Points

### Pain (VNRS, 0-10 scale, median with IQR)
| Timepoint | Collagen (n=34) | Placebo (n=34) | p-value |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|---------|
| Baseline  | 5.5 (2)         | 6 (2)          | 0.358   |
| 2 weeks   | 4 (2.5)         | 4 (2.3)        | 0.980   |
| 6 weeks   | 3 (3)           | 3 (3)          | 0.861   |
| 12 weeks  | 3 (3)           | 3 (3)          | 0.789   |

- GEE analysis: collagen group mean reduction = 2.21 points (B=-2.21, p<0.001, 95%CI [-3.11, -1.30]); placebo reduction = 2.29 points. Between-group difference = 0.08 points (not significant).

### KOOS subscales (all p > 0.05 between groups at all timepoints)
- Pain, Symptoms, ADL, Sports/Recreation, Quality of Life -- all showed improvement over time in both groups, but no between-group differences.

### Rescue medication (meloxicam 7.5 mg or celecoxib 200 mg)
- Non-significant trend toward lower consumption in collagen group at 2 weeks (median 1 vs 4 tablets, p=0.135). Converged by 12 weeks (both median 0).

### Patient satisfaction (VNRS)
- No significant between-group differences at any timepoint (p = 0.227, 0.355, 0.126 at 2, 6, 12 weeks).

### Adverse events
- **Zero adverse effects reported in either group.** Clean safety profile.

## Dose and Formulation
- **Intervention:** 2 tablets/day taken before bedtime. Each dose: 480 mg hydrolysed collagen + 20 mg UC-II (Bangkok Drug Co., Ltd.). Total daily: 480 mg hydrolysed collagen, 20 mg UC-II.
- **Placebo:** Maltodextrin tablets, identical appearance and dosing schedule.
- Both groups received standardised care: patient education, muscle-strengthening exercises, and PRN rescue medication.

## Methodology
- **Population:** 68 patients aged 50-80, KL grade 2-3 knee OA, medial compartment predominant, varus <10 degrees.
- **Randomisation:** Computer-generated block randomisation (block size 4), allocation concealment via opaque sealed envelopes.
- **Blinding:** Participants, treating physicians, and outcome assessors all blinded.
- **Analysis:** Intention-to-treat. Mann-Whitney U for non-parametric comparisons. GEE for longitudinal pain analysis.
- **Power calculation:** Based on Kumar et al. data; 90% power, alpha 0.05, requiring 28/group; inflated to 34/group for 20% dropout. However, post hoc: observed effect size Cohen's d = 0.04, yielding only 5.3% power -- study was substantially underpowered for the observed effect.

## Limitations (per authors and reviewer assessment)
1. **Short duration (12 weeks):** Authors note collagen effects on cartilage metabolism may require longer to manifest; previous positive studies used 90 days to 6 months.
2. **Small sample / underpowered:** Post hoc analysis showed Cohen's d = 0.04. Would need ~235/group for 90% power at d=0.3.
3. **No biomarkers or imaging:** Cannot assess disease modification or confirm treatment adherence.
4. **Single-centre, Thai population:** Generalisability uncertain.
5. **Predominantly moderate OA (KL 2-3):** Findings may not apply to mild or severe disease.
6. **Low dose of hydrolysed collagen (480 mg):** Much lower than many prior studies of hydrolysed collagen alone (often 5-10 g/day). This is a critical limitation -- the dose may simply be subtherapeutic.

## Combination vs Standalone Context
- Previous UC-II alone studies (Sadigursky, Lugo) reported positive results at 90-180 days.
- Previous hydrolysed collagen alone studies (Trč, Benito-Ruiz, Bruyère) reported positive results at doses typically much higher (5-10 g/day).
- This is the **first study to combine** UC-II + hydrolysed collagen. Authors speculate possible competition for absorption or bioactivity, or suboptimal dose ratio.
- The 480 mg hydrolysed collagen dose is ~10-20x lower than typical standalone trials, which is a major confound for interpreting whether the combination itself lacks synergy or whether the hydrolysed collagen was simply underdosed.

## Claims to Verify
- **N = 68 (34 per group):** Consistently reported throughout. 87 screened, 68 randomised. Appears reliable.
- **Effect size Cohen's d = 0.04:** Derived from between-group difference of 0.08 on VNRS with SD ~2.0. Calculation checks out (0.08/2.0 = 0.04).
- **"5.3% power" claim:** For d=0.04, n=34/group, two-tailed alpha=0.05, this is plausible (very small effect).
- **Dose of 480 mg hydrolysed collagen:** Stated clearly in Methods. This is notably lower than most standalone collagen hydrolysate trials (typically 5,000-10,000 mg/day), which is a significant methodological concern not emphasized enough by the authors.

## Relevance to Research Question
This is a directly relevant RCT testing a collagen combination supplement for knee OA. It is a **negative trial** -- the combination showed no benefit over placebo. Key takeaway for synthesis: the negative result may be driven by the very low hydrolysed collagen dose (480 mg vs typical 5-10 g) rather than by any inherent failure of collagen supplementation. The study is manufacturer-independent, which strengthens its credibility relative to many industry-sponsored positive trials.
