# src-052: Alekseeva et al. (2024) - UC-II for Knee OA (Artneo Trial)

**Citation:** Alekseeva LI, Kashevarova NG, et al. "Efficacy and safety of undenatured type II collagen in patients with knee osteoarthritis: a multicenter, prospective, double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized trial." *Terapevticheskii Arkhiv*. 2024;96(5):500-510. DOI: 10.26442/00403660.2024.05.202788

**Trial Registry:** ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05975879

## Core Findings

This multicenter (12 sites, Russia), double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT of 212 patients with knee OA (Kellgren-Lawrence stages II-III) found that a combination supplement containing 40 mg/day undenatured chicken type II collagen (UC-II) plus MSM, Boswellia serrata extract, vitamin C, and vitamin D3 (brand name "Artneo") significantly improved WOMAC total score vs placebo at 180 days (ANCOVA estimate: -116 points; 95% CI -215 to -17; p=0.021). The supplement was superior to placebo across pain, stiffness, physical function, KOOS, and EQ-5D quality of life measures with a safety profile comparable to placebo.

**Note:** This study tested a multi-ingredient formulation (Artneo), NOT UC-II alone. The product contains UC-II 40 mg + MSM 300 mg + Boswellia serrata extract 32.5 mg + vitamin C 80 mg + vitamin D3 10 mcg per capsule. Efficacy cannot be attributed solely to UC-II.

## Key Evidence / Data Points

### Primary Endpoint
- **WOMAC-T change at day 180 (ANCOVA):** Artneo group vs Placebo = -116 points (95% CI -215 to -17; p=0.021)

### Secondary Endpoints
- **WOMAC pain (WOMAC-A):** Significantly greater reduction in Artneo group at days 60, 120, 180 (p<0.05 vs placebo)
- **WOMAC stiffness (WOMAC-B):** Significantly greater reduction in Artneo group at day 60 (p<0.05)
- **WOMAC physical function (WOMAC-C):** Significantly greater improvement at days 60 and 180 (p<0.05)
- **WOMAC-T (total):** Significantly greater reduction at days 60 and 180 (p<0.05)
- **KOOS:** Significantly greater improvement in Artneo group at days 60, 90, 120, 180 (p<0.05 at days 60, 90; p<0.01 at days 120, 180)
- **VAS pain walking:** Significantly greater reduction in Artneo group at day 60 (3.0 cm difference; p=0.0355), clinically meaningful (>1.37 cm threshold per Hawker et al. 2011)
- **EQ-5D quality of life:** Significant between-group difference at day 180 (p=0.0429)
- **NSAID rescue use:** No statistically significant difference between groups
- **Synovitis (ultrasound):** No statistically significant difference between groups at day 180
- **Responder analysis (WOMAC at day 180):** Artneo group had significantly higher proportions achieving 20%, 50%, and 70% improvement thresholds across pain, stiffness, and combined scores

### Adverse Events
- Total AEs: 41 (19.3%) -- Artneo 22/106 (20.8%) vs Placebo 19/106 (17.9%); p=0.7283 (Fisher's exact test, NS)
- **Serious AEs: 0 in both groups**
- All AEs classified as mild severity
- Most common AEs in Artneo group: rhinitis (4, 3.8%), headache (4, 3.8%), joint swelling (2, 1.9%)
- One withdrawal in Artneo group due to allergic reaction (urticaria, mucosal edema of eyes and mouth)
- No clinically significant lab abnormalities in either group

### Study Population
- **N = 212** randomized 1:1 (Artneo n=106, Placebo n=106)
- ITT population: 209 (98.6%); PP population: 192 (90.6%)
- 208 completed full protocol (103 Artneo, 105 Placebo)
- Demographics: 171 women (80.7%), 41 men (19.3%); mean age 60.2 +/- 9.0 years (range 40-75)
- BMI: Artneo 27.09 +/- 2.48; Placebo 26.36 +/- 3.04
- Knee OA Kellgren-Lawrence stage II or III
- Comorbidities: hypertension (~54-59%), dyslipidemia (~10-11%), type 2 diabetes (~8.5%)

## Methodology

- **Design:** Multicenter (12 Russian centers), prospective, double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized (1:1)
- **Randomization:** Interactive Web Response System (IWRS)
- **Duration:** 180 days (6 months)
- **Dose:** 1 capsule/day containing UC-II 40 mg + MSM 300 mg + Boswellia serrata 32.5 mg + Vit C 80 mg + Vit D3 10 mcg
- **Outcomes:** WOMAC index (primary: total at day 180), KOOS, VAS pain, EQ-5D, NSAID rescue, ultrasound synovitis, responder analysis
- **Statistics:** SAS 9.4; normality tests (Kolmogorov-Smirnov, Shapiro-Wilk); parametric (Student's t) and non-parametric (Wilcoxon, chi-square, Mann-Whitney U, Fisher's exact); ITT and PP analyses (results identical, reported ITT)
- **Visits:** 7 visits (screening, randomization/day 1, days 30, 60, 90, 120, 180)

## Limitations

1. **Multi-ingredient formulation:** Cannot isolate UC-II efficacy from other active ingredients (MSM, Boswellia, vitamins C and D3)
2. **No active comparator arm** (e.g., glucosamine/chondroitin) -- only placebo
3. **Single geographic region** (Russia only) -- may limit generalizability
4. **No OA phenotype analysis** (acknowledged by authors as a limitation)
5. **Industry involvement:** One author affiliated with Meili LLC (likely the product distributor); study product manufactured by Petrovax Pharm LLC
6. **No structural/disease-modification assessment** (no MRI or radiographic progression data)
7. **NSAID use not significantly different** -- unclear if this means UC-II does not reduce NSAID need, or study was underpowered for this endpoint

## Relevance to Research Question

- **Knee OA:** Directly addresses UC-II efficacy for knee OA with strong RCT evidence (N=212, 6-month, placebo-controlled)
- **Back pain:** Does NOT address back pain or spinal OA. No mention of spinal outcomes
- **Dose:** 40 mg/day UC-II -- consistent with standard dosing across the literature (as noted in Kumar et al. 2023 review cited in the paper)
- **Safety:** Excellent safety profile; no serious AEs; comparable to placebo; one allergic withdrawal
- **Unique findings:** One of the larger UC-II RCTs (N=212); multi-ingredient formulation; 6-month duration with multiple assessment points showing time course of benefit (significant differences emerging at day 60 for most outcomes)
