# src-081: Safety of SYSADOAs for Osteoarthritis (Honvo et al., Drugs & Aging 2019)

## Core Findings

This systematic review and meta-analysis assessed safety of SYSADOAs (glucosamine sulfate, chondroitin sulfate, avocado soybean unsaponifiables, diacerein, collagen derivatives, curcuma) in OA via 25 RCTs (13 in meta-analysis) plus 37 parallel studies (18 in parallel meta-analysis). **UC-II (undenatured type II collagen) is NOT mentioned or analyzed anywhere in this paper.** While "collagen derivatives" were included as an eligible intervention category, the three collagen studies identified all involved hydrolyzed collagen or collagen type I -- none examined UC-II specifically.

## Relevance to UC-II

**Minimal/indirect relevance.** This paper does not contain any safety data, adverse event rates, drug interactions, or contraindications specific to UC-II. Its only contribution is:
- It confirms that "collagen derivatives" are considered part of the SYSADOA class, providing contextual taxonomy.
- The three collagen studies it identified (none UC-II) were all excluded from meta-analysis due to inadequate safety data, illustrating the general paucity of rigorous safety reporting for collagen products in OA.

## Collagen Derivative Studies Identified (Table 2, parallel synthesis only)

All three were in the parallel qualitative synthesis (studies with concomitant OA medications allowed). None were included in any meta-analysis.

1. **Kumar et al. [73]** -- Compared peptide collagen product (PCP, Nitta Gelatin) vs. bovine collagen peptide (BCP, Nitta Gelatin India) in knee OA. Dose: 2 x 5 g/d, 13 weeks. Concomitant: aceclofenac sodium 100 mg. Data: summary without details. Not usable for MA. Author did not respond to data request.

2. **McAlindon et al. [82]** -- Fortigel (Gelita AG), hydrolyzed collagen. Dose: 10 g/d CH, 48 weeks. Knee OA. Concomitant: analgesics or NSAIDs. Data: summary without details. Full data provided by author. Still not usable for MA.

3. **Stanik et al. [94]** -- Colafit (Dacom Pharma), pure lyophilized collagen type I in capsule. Dose: 8 mg/d, 13 weeks. Knee OA. No information about concomitant medication use. Data: all AEs reported. Usable published data: Yes. However, corresponding author unable to provide additional information.

**None of these studies involve UC-II (undenatured type II collagen).**

## Key Results for Other SYSADOAs (for context)

- **Glucosamine sulfate (GS):** No significant increase in any AE type vs. placebo (OR 0.96; 95% CI 0.66-1.41 for total AEs). GRADE: mostly high certainty.
- **Chondroitin sulfate (CS):** No significant increase in AEs; actually fewer total AEs than placebo in studies without concomitant meds (OR 0.70; 95% CI 0.51-0.98). Fewer renal/urinary disorders (OR 0.40; 95% CI 0.22-0.74). GRADE: high certainty.
- **ASU:** No significant difference vs. placebo for any AE type (OR 1.09; 95% CI 0.81-1.46). All ASU studies allowed concomitant NSAIDs. GRADE: high certainty.
- **Diacerein:** Significantly increased odds of total AEs (OR 2.22; 95% CI 1.58-3.13), GI disorders (OR 2.85; 95% CI 2.02-4.04), renal/urinary disorders (OR 3.42; 95% CI 2.36-4.96), and dermatological disorders with concomitant meds (OR 2.47; 95% CI 1.42-4.31).

## Methodology

- Systematic review + random-effects meta-analysis of RCTs
- Databases: MEDLINE, Cochrane CENTRAL, Scopus (inception to May 2017, alerts to Sept 2018)
- PROSPERO registered (CRD42017069875)
- PRISMA reported
- Primary outcomes: overall severe/serious AEs, plus MedDRA SOC categories (GI, cardiac, vascular, nervous system, skin, MSCT, renal/urinary)
- Authors/sponsors contacted for full safety data (major strength)
- Parallel analysis for studies with concomitant OA medications

## Limitations (for collagen relevance)

- No collagen derivative study had adequate data for meta-analysis
- No UC-II study was identified at all in the systematic search
- Search limited to English and French publications
- The eligibility criteria included "collagen derivatives" but the search strategy may not have captured UC-II-specific terms (the paper used broad SYSADOA search terms)
