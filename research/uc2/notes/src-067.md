# src-067: HEM + UC-II in ACLT-Induced OA (Preclinical Rat Model)

**Citation:** Lee et al. (2026). Int J Med Sci, 23(3): 815-823. doi: 10.7150/ijms.126220
**Type:** Preclinical animal study (rat ACLT model)
**Quality:** Full paper text available; peer-reviewed open-access journal.

## Core Findings

This study tested whether Hericium erinaceus mycelium (HEM, 100 mg/kg) and undenatured type II collagen (UC-II, 4 mg/kg) -- alone and combined -- could reduce osteoarthritis progression in male Sprague Dawley rats (8 weeks, 300-350 g) following anterior cruciate ligament transection (ACLT). After 6 weeks of treatment, both agents individually and in combination reduced OA-related pain (weight-bearing incapacitance), bone damage (micro-CT), synovial inflammation, and cartilage degradation. The combination appeared more effective than either agent alone on pain behavior, though all three treatments significantly improved outcomes vs. ACLT-only.

## Key Evidence

### Clinical/Surrogate Outcomes
- **Pain (weight-bearing incapacitance):** All treatment groups showed significant improvement vs. ACLT-only by week 1 onward. The HEM+UC-II combination showed greater efficacy than monotherapies (Figure 2, p<0.05 vs. ACLT).
- **Bone microarchitecture (micro-CT):** ACLT reduced BMD, BMC, BV/TV, BS/TV, Tb.Th, Tb.N and increased Tb.Sp. All treatment groups showed significant improvements in these parameters vs. ACLT.
- **Histology (H&E, Safranin-O/Fast Green):** Treatment groups had lower synovium scores, OARSI scores, and cartilage scores vs. ACLT-only.
- **Inflammatory cytokines (IHC):** HEM, UC-II, and combination all significantly reduced IL-1beta and TNF-alpha expression in synovial tissue vs. ACLT.
- **Cartilage matrix markers (IHC):** Treatment groups showed reduced MMP-3, MMP-13, ADAMTS5 and increased aggrecan and COL2A1 vs. ACLT.

### Doses
- HEM: 100 mg/kg (oral, presumably daily via gavage -- route not explicitly stated but implied)
- UC-II: 4 mg/kg (oral, same)
- Duration: 6 weeks post-surgery
- No toxic side effects on body weight observed across all groups.

### Adverse Events
- No adverse events reported. Body weight growth curves were comparable across all groups throughout the 6-week study period.

## Methodology

- **Design:** 5-group preclinical study -- (1) sham surgery, (2) ACLT-only, (3) ACLT+HEM 100 mg/kg, (4) ACLT+UC-II 4 mg/kg, (5) ACLT+HEM+UC-II.
- **Animals:** Male Sprague Dawley rats, 8 weeks old, 300-350 g.
- **OA model:** Anterior cruciate ligament transection (ACLT) -- a well-established surgical OA model.
- **Assessments:** Weekly weight-bearing incapacitance (pain); terminal micro-CT (bone); histology with H&E and Safranin-O/Fast Green staining; IHC for IL-1beta, TNF-alpha, MMP-3, MMP-13, ADAMTS5, aggrecan, COL2A1; OARSI scoring system.
- **Statistics:** GraphPad Prism 5.0; one-way ANOVA with Bonferroni post hoc; paired t-test for two-group comparisons; significance at p<0.05.

## Novel Findings
1. First study to combine HEM with UC-II in an OA model -- combination showed additive/synergistic effect on pain reduction beyond monotherapy.
2. UC-II mechanism evidence in ACLT model: reduced pro-inflammatory cytokines and chondrolytic enzymes, preserved cartilage matrix proteins (aggrecan, COL2A1).
3. Confirms and extends prior HEM findings (Yang et al., Nutrients 2022, ref 17) using the same ACLT model.

## Limitations

1. **Sample size not explicitly reported.** The paper describes 5 groups but never states n per group in the Methods section. This is a significant omission -- impossible to assess statistical power. The figures suggest small n (commonly 6-10 in rat ACLT studies).
2. **Single dose tested** for each compound. Authors acknowledge this limitation and call for dose-combination studies.
3. **Preclinical only** -- rat ACLT model; no human clinical data. Translatability uncertain.
4. **No dose-response data** -- cannot determine optimal dose or therapeutic window.
5. **No mechanistic pathway data** -- IHC shows protein-level changes but no signaling pathway analysis (e.g., NF-kB, MAPK).
6. **Combination effects described as "slightly enhanced"** -- authors themselves characterize the combination benefit as modest (Discussion, p. 821).
7. **Industry involvement:** Authors from Grape King Bio Ltd. (Li-Ya Lee, Wan-Ping Chen, Yu-Wen Chen) are listed -- potential COI despite declared "no competing interests."

## Claims to Verify

1. **Sample size (n per group):** NOT stated anywhere in the paper. This is a critical omission. Figures appear to show bar graphs with error bars (S.D.) but raw n is never disclosed.
2. **p<0.05 significance claims:** Quantitative data are only shown in figures (not reported as numbers in text). Cannot independently verify statistical claims from the text alone.

## Relevance to Research Questions

This is a preclinical (animal) study, not a human clinical trial. It provides mechanistic evidence for HEM and UC-II as OA supplements but does not directly address human clinical outcomes, human dosing, or human adverse events. Its primary value is:
- Supporting rationale for clinical trials of HEM + UC-II combination
- Mechanistic data on anti-inflammatory and chondroprotective pathways
- Dose used in rats (100 mg/kg HEM, 4 mg/kg UC-II) -- requires allometric scaling for human equivalence
