# src-034: Undenatured Collagen Type II for Treatment of OA of the Knee

**Authors:** Sadigursky D, Magnavita VFS, Sa CKC, Monteiro HS, Braghiroli OFM, Matos MAA
**Year:** 2022 | **Venue:** Acta Ortopedica Brasileira | **Citations:** 19
**DOI:** 10.1590/1413-785220223002240572
**Study Design:** Prospective comparative study, randomized block design (Level II evidence)

## Core Findings

UC-II at 40 mg/day for 90 days significantly improved pain (VAS), joint stiffness (WOMAC stiffness), and physical quality of life (SF-12 Physical) in adults aged 60-80 with knee OA compared to a non-supplemented control group. Both groups received standard physical therapy and analgesics as needed. Mental health quality of life (SF-12 Mental) did not differ between groups. WOMAC total and WOMAC pain showed significant improvements; WOMAC function showed a trend (p=0.056).

## Key Evidence and Effect Sizes

### Pain (VAS, 0-10 scale)
- **UC-II group:** Baseline 7.1 +/- 0.9 -> 90 days 3.4 +/- 1.6; change = -3.8 +/- 1.8
- **Control group:** Baseline 7.3 +/- 0.7 -> 90 days 6.0 +/- 1.8; change = -1.3 +/- 2.0
- **Between-group difference (90d - baseline):** -2.4 (95% CI: -3.2 to -1.7), p < 0.001
- Effect is clinically meaningful (MCID for VAS pain typically ~1.5-2.0 points)

### WOMAC Total Score
- **UC-II group:** Baseline 54.0 +/- 16.6 -> 90 days 44.6 +/- 12.0; change = -9.5 +/- 11.9
- **Control group:** Baseline 58.6 +/- 14.3 -> 90 days 57.3 +/- 16.5; change = -1.3 +/- 11.1
- **Between-group difference:** -8.2 (95% CI: -12.6 to -3.7), p < 0.001

### WOMAC Pain Subscale
- **UC-II change:** -6.6 +/- 4.8 vs **Control change:** -1.0 +/- 3.8
- **Between-group difference:** -5.7 (95% CI: -7.3 to -4.0), p < 0.001

### WOMAC Stiffness Subscale
- **UC-II change:** -0.5 +/- 0.9 vs **Control change:** +0.1 +/- 1.1
- **Between-group difference:** -0.6 (95% CI: -1.0 to -0.3), p = 0.001
- Note: Small absolute effect; WOMAC stiffness subscale has limited range

### WOMAC Function Subscale
- **UC-II change:** -3.0 +/- 6.2 vs **Control change:** -0.4 +/- 7.6
- **Between-group difference:** -2.6 (95% CI: -5.3 to 0.1), p = 0.056 (trend, not significant)

### SF-12 Physical Component
- **UC-II group:** Baseline 29.5 +/- 7.1 -> 90 days 45.6 +/- 8.0; change = +16.0 +/- 7.9
- **Control group:** Baseline 31.5 +/- 6.3 -> 90 days 33.0 +/- 8.2; change = +1.5 +/- 7.2
- **Between-group difference:** +14.5 (95% CI: 11.6 to 17.5), p < 0.001
- Large effect size (partial eta-squared reported for some measures)

### SF-12 Mental Component
- No significant interaction between time and intervention (p = 0.978)
- Between-group difference at 90 days: 0.2 (95% CI: -4.0 to 4.4), p = 0.924

## Dose and Duration
- **Dose:** UC-II 40 mg daily (oral)
- **Duration:** 90 days
- UC-II classified as a nutraceutical, authorized by Brazilian ANVISA

## Adverse Events
- The paper mentions recording "presence of UC-II side effects" in the final evaluation, but no specific adverse events are reported in the results or discussion sections. The authors do not describe any safety concerns or adverse events attributable to UC-II.

## Methodology

- **Sample:** 106 patients initially; 105 completed (53 UC-II, 52 control; 1 dropped from control for non-attendance)
- **Population:** Adults aged 60-80 years with clinical and radiological knee OA (Kellgren-Lawrence grades 2-3)
- **Randomization:** Block randomized into two groups
- **Control:** No UC-II; both groups received standard physical therapy (kinesiotherapy with closed kinetic chain exercises, 2x/week) and analgesics/weak opioids as needed, plus nutritional guidance
- **Outcome measures:** SF-12 (quality of life), VAS (pain), WOMAC (pain, stiffness, function), ROM, alignment, radiographic analysis
- **Assessment timepoints:** Baseline, 30 days, 90 days
- **Statistical analysis:** Mixed 2-way ANOVA (time x group), intention-to-treat, Greenhouse-Geisser correction when sphericity violated

## Limitations

1. **No placebo control** -- the control group did not receive a placebo. Authors acknowledge this as the greatest limitation, noting that placebo response can be high with nutraceuticals.
2. **Non-blinded** -- evaluators were not blinded, introducing potential information bias.
3. **Short follow-up** -- only 90 days; authors call for longer studies.
4. **Questionnaire-based outcomes only** -- no objective biomarker or imaging outcome changes reported.
5. **WOMAC function did not reach significance** (p=0.056), though the trend favored UC-II.
6. **Potential cultural bias** in questionnaire interpretation noted by authors.
7. Both groups received standard care (PT + analgesics), so UC-II effect is additive to background treatment.

## Relevance to Research Question

Directly relevant to UC-II efficacy for knee OA. Provides Level II evidence for UC-II at 40 mg/day over 90 days showing statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvements in pain (VAS reduction of ~3.8 points from ~7.1 baseline), total WOMAC score, and physical quality of life. The absence of placebo and blinding are notable weaknesses. Effect sizes are moderate to large for pain and physical QoL outcomes.

## Claims to Verify

- **Sample size calculation:** Based on 15.4% decrease in VAS, 10% margin of error, 95% significance -> 60 per group (actual was 53 per group). The achieved sample was slightly below the calculated target.
- **No adverse events reported:** The methods mention recording side effects, but results do not explicitly state "no adverse events were observed." This could mean none occurred or none were analyzed -- unclear.
- **Baseline WOMAC scores ~54-59:** These are relatively high on a 0-96 scale, indicating moderate-to-severe symptom burden, consistent with the age group and KL grades 2-3.
