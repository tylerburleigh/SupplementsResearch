## Study Design and Inclusion Criteria

- **Design:** Systematic review and meta-analysis following standard methods.
- **Search databases:** PubMed and Cochrane Library.
- **Inclusion:** Randomized controlled trials (RCTs) evaluating Curcuma longa extract or curcumin for treatment of osteoarthritis.
- **Total included:** 15 RCTs, 1621 participants.
- **Comparisons evaluated:**
  1. Curcumin vs. placebo
  2. Curcumin vs. NSAIDs
  3. Curcumin + NSAIDs vs. NSAIDs alone
- **Outcomes measured:** VAS (visual analog scale for pain), WOMAC subscales (pain, function, stiffness), adverse events.

## Key Effect Sizes (VAS, WOMAC Scores)

**Critical limitation:** The abstract does not report exact numerical effect sizes (standardized mean differences, mean differences, confidence intervals, or p-values). It states directional findings only:

- **Curcumin vs. placebo:** Curcumin significantly decreased VAS pain and all three WOMAC subscales (pain, function, stiffness).
- **Curcumin vs. NSAIDs:** No significant difference -- similar effects on joint pain, function, and stiffness.
- **Curcumin + NSAIDs vs. NSAIDs alone:** The combination decreased VAS and all WOMAC subscales compared with NSAIDs alone.

**Note:** Exact SMD/MD values, confidence intervals, heterogeneity statistics (I-squared), and p-values are only available in the full text (figures 4-7), which were not captured. The forest plots (Figures 4-7) would contain these values.

## Safety Profile Compared to NSAIDs and Placebo

- **Curcumin vs. placebo:** Adverse events were comparable -- no significant difference in incidence.
- **Curcumin vs. NSAIDs:** Curcumin had a **lower** incidence of adverse events than NSAIDs.
- **Curcumin + NSAIDs vs. NSAIDs:** Adding curcumin to NSAIDs did not increase adverse events.
- The authors conclude curcumin is a "safer and effective supplement" for OA patients.

## Clinical Recommendation

The authors recommend using Curcuma longa extract and curcumin supplements for OA patients for **more than 12 weeks** (duration threshold based on their analysis of included trials).
