## Core Findings

Nano-curcumin supplementation at 80 mg/day for 8 weeks significantly reduced depression and anxiety scores (DASS-21) in patients with diabetic polyneuropathy compared to placebo, but did not significantly affect stress scores. This is described as the first RCT testing nano-curcumin for mood outcomes in this population.

## Study Design

- **Design:** Parallel, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled clinical trial
- **Population:** 80 patients with diabetes (type 2) and diabetic polyneuropathy
- **Randomization:** 1:1 allocation to intervention (n=40) and control (n=40)
- **Intervention:** 80 mg nano-curcumin capsules daily
- **Control:** Placebo capsules
- **Duration:** 8 weeks
- **Country:** Iran (inferred from MeSH term and funding source)
- **Outcomes assessed at:** Baseline and end of study

## Primary Outcomes (DASS-21)

### Depression
| Group | Baseline Mean (SD) | End Mean (SD) | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------|--------|
| Nano-curcumin | 16.7 (3.1) | 15.3 (2.6) | -1.4 |
| Placebo | 17.5 (3.2) | 17.3 (3.1) | -0.2 |

**p = .02** (between-group comparison)

### Anxiety
| Group | Baseline Mean (SD) | End Mean (SD) | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------|--------|
| Nano-curcumin | 22.4 (4.03) | 20.6 (3.4) | -1.8 |
| Placebo | 21.9 (3.5) | 21.2 (3.5) | -0.7 |

**p = .009** (between-group comparison)

### Stress
- Changes were **not statistically significant** between groups.
