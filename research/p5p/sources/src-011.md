Product Description

Vitamin B6 helps metabolize lipids, carbohydrates and proteins into energy. It also supports the function and growth of red blood cells as well as supporting the nervous and immune systems.** P-5-P is a biologically active form of Vitamin B6 and is utilized by the body.**
