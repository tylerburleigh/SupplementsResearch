## Core Findings

This cross-sectional study of 36 chronic hemodialysis patients (not supplemented with B-complex vitamins) found that PLP (pyridoxal-5'-phosphate) was depleted in 56% of patients (20/36), with 14 more in the lower normal range. High-efficiency hemodialysis actively removed PLP during sessions (27.9% reduction ratio, clearance 54.4 mL/min), contributing to depletion. All patients had elevated homocysteine (mean 40.2 micromol/L), but no significant association was found between PLP/homocysteine levels and vascular disease status.

## Key Evidence / Data Points

- **PLP depletion prevalence**: 20/36 patients (56%) had reduced predialysis PLP; 14 more were low-normal
- **Predialysis PLP**: 11.1 +/- 7.5 nmol/L; Postdialysis PLP: 8.0 +/- 5.9 nmol/L
- **PLP dialysis clearance**: 54.4 +/- 38.2 mL/min; percent reduction ratio: 27.9% +/- 14.2%
- **Folate clearance**: 134.7 +/- 22.2 mL/min; percent reduction ratio: 26.3% +/- 16.0%
- **Homocysteine**: elevated in all patients (mean 40.2 +/- 29.6 micromol/L, range 14.4-158.0)
- **Vascular disease**: present in 21/36 patients; no significant difference in PLP or homocysteine vs. those without vascular disease
- **Urea reduction ratio**: 68.4% +/- 6.6% (confirming adequate dialysis)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Cross-sectional study at a university medical center
- **Population**: 36 chronic dialysis patients (23 men, 13 women, mean age 57 +/- 13 years), on hemodialysis for mean 3.8 +/- 2.2 years, NOT supplemented with hydrosoluble vitamins
- **Dialysis parameters**: Thrice-weekly, CT-190G or F-20 dialyzers, blood flow 371 +/- 40 mL/min, dialysate flow 500 mL/min, session time 3.7 +/- 0.4 hours
- **Measures**: Pre/postdialysis serum folate, PLP, urea; predialysis B12 and homocysteine; blood-side clearances calculated
- **Limitations**: Small sample size (n=36), single-center, cross-sectional (no causal inference), no supplementation arm, limited generalizability beyond dialysis population
