## Summary

This is a review paper on vitamin B6-dependent epilepsies -- a group of rare genetic metabolic disorders (ALDH7A1 deficiency, PNPO deficiency, PLP binding protein deficiency, hyperprolinaemia type II, hypophosphatasia, glycosylphosphatidylinositol anchor synthesis defects) that cause seizures in children and respond to pyridoxine or pyridoxal-5-phosphate (P5P) supplementation.

## Core Findings

1. P5P and pyridoxine are effective treatments for genetically-confirmed B6-dependent epilepsies in pediatric populations.
2. ALDH7A1-deficient patients should also receive lysine-restricted diet and arginine supplementation.
3. Pyridoxine or P5P should be trialed in all intractable seizures, including beyond the first year of life.
4. Prenatal/postnatal pyridoxine supplementation recommended in familial cases until genetic disconfirmation.
